The year at a glance

    Follows the General Roman Calendar with the proper calendar of the United States, applying the precedence and transfer rules of the Universal Norms on the Liturgical Year and the Calendar. Everything is calculated in your browser, so it works for any year.

    Diocesan and parish celebrations (patronal feasts, dedications) are not included; check your diocesan Ordo for those. Saints’ list adapted from the open-source romcal project (MIT License).
