t: A fúlgure et tempestáte, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || From lightning and tempest, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: A flagéllo terræmótus, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || From the scourge of earthquake, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: A peste, fame et bello, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || From plague, famine, and war, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: A morte perpétua, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || From everlasting death, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: Per mystérium sanctæ Incarnatiónis tuæ, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || Through the mystery of Your holy Incarnation, _deliver us, O Lord._

t: Per baptísmum et sanctum jejúnium tuum, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || Through Your baptism and holy fasting, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: Per crucem et passiónem tuam, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || Through Your Cross and Passion, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: Per mortem et sepultúram tuam, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || Through Your death and burial, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: Per sanctam resurrectiónem tuam, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || Through Your holy resurrection, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: Per admirábilem ascensiónem tuam, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || Through Your wonderful ascension, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: Per advéntum Spíritus Sancti Parácliti, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || Through the coming of the Holy Spirit, the Paraclete, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: In die judícii, _líbera nos, Dómine._ || In the day of judgment, _deliver us, O Lord._
t: Peccatóres, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || We sinners, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut nobis parcas, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You spare us, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut nobis indúlgeas, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You pardon us, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut ad veram pœniténtiam nos perdúcere dignéris, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deign to bring us to true repentance, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut Ecclésiam tuam sanctam régere et conserváre dignéris, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deign to govern and preserve Your holy Church, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut Domnum Apostólicum et omnes ecclesiásticos órdines in sancta religióne conserváre dignéris, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deign to preserve the Apostolic Lord and all ranks of the Church in holy religion, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut inimícos sanctæ Ecclésiæ humiliáre dignéris, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deign to humble the enemies of holy Church, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut régibus et princípibus christiánis pacem et veram concórdiam donáre dignéris, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deign to give peace and true concord to Christian kings and rulers, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut cuncto pópulo christiáno pacem et unitátem largíri dignéris, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deign to grant peace and unity to all Christian people, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut omnes errántes ad unitátem Ecclésiæ revocáre, et infidéles univérsos ad Evangélii lumen perdúcere dignéris, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deign to call back all who have strayed to the unity of the Church, and to lead all unbelievers to the light of the Gospel, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut nosmetípsos in tuo sancto servítio confortáre et conserváre dignéris, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deign to strengthen and keep us in Your holy service, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut mentes nostras ad cæléstia desidéria érigas, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You lift up our minds to heavenly desires, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut ómnibus benefactóribus nostris sempitérna bona retríbuas, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You render eternal blessings to all our benefactors, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut ánimas nostras, fratrum, propinquórum et benefactórum nostrórum ab ætérna damnatióne erípias, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deliver our souls and the souls of our brethren, relatives, and benefactors from eternal damnation, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut fructus terræ dare et conserváre dignéris, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deign to give and preserve the fruits of the earth, _we beseech You, hear us._
t: Ut ómnibus fidélibus defúnctis réquiem ætérnam donáre dignéris, _te rogámus, audi nos._ || That You deign to grant eternal rest to all the faithful departed, _we beseech You, hear us._
