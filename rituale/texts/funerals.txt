# funeral-rules | De Exsequiis | On Funerals | The Dying & the Dead | Tit. VI, cap. 1
r: 1. Sacras cæremónias ac ritus, quibus ex antiquíssima traditióne et Summórum Pontíficum institútis sancta mater Ecclésia cathólica in filiórum suórum exséquiis uti solet, tamquam vera religiónis mystéria christianǽque pietátis signa, et fidélium mortuórum salubérrima suffrágia, Parochi summo stúdio serváre debent, atque usu retinére. || 1. The sacred ceremonies and rites which holy Mother the Catholic Church, by most ancient tradition and the ordinances of the Supreme Pontiffs, is accustomed to use in the funerals of her children, as true mysteries of religion, signs of Christian piety, and most wholesome suffrages for the faithful departed, pastors must observe with the greatest care and keep in use.
r: 2. His ítaque præstándis, qua par est modéstia ac devotióne ita se habébunt, ut ad defunctórum salútem, simúlque ad vivórum pietátem, quemádmodum vere sunt, non ad quæstum ejúsmodi ritus sancti institúti esse videántur. || 2. In carrying them out, therefore, they shall conduct themselves with fitting modesty and devotion, so that these holy rites may be seen to have been instituted, as indeed they were, for the salvation of the dead and likewise for the piety of the living, and not for gain.
r: 3. Nullum corpus sepeliátur, præsértim si mors repentína fúerit, nisi post cóngruum témporis intervállum, quod satis sit ad omnem prorsus de vero óbitu dubitatiónem tolléndam. || 3. No body is to be buried, especially if death was sudden, except after a suitable interval of time, sufficient to remove all doubt whatever about the reality of death.
r: 4. Nisi gravis causa obstet, cadávera fidélium, ántequam tumuléntur, transferénda sunt e loco in quo reperiúntur, in ecclésiam, ubi funus, idest totus ordo exsequiárum quæ in probátis litúrgicis libris describúntur, persolvátur. || 4. Unless a grave cause prevents it, the bodies of the faithful, before they are buried, are to be brought from the place where they are found into the church, where the funeral, that is, the whole order of obsequies described in the approved liturgical books, is to be carried out.
r: 5. Ecclésia in quam cadáver pro fúnere transférri debet, ex jure ordinário est ecclésia própriæ defúncti parœ́ciæ, nisi defúnctus áliam fúneris ecclésiam legítime elégerit, vel jure particulári áliter statuátur. || 5. The church to which the body must be brought for the funeral is by ordinary law the church of the deceased's own parish, unless the deceased has lawfully chosen another church for the funeral, or particular law provides otherwise.
r: Si defúnctus plures habúerit parœ́cias próprias, ecclésia fúneris est ecclésia parœ́ciæ in cujus território decéssit. || If the deceased had several proper parishes, the church of the funeral is the church of the parish in whose territory he died.
r: 6. In dúbio de jure alíus ecclésiæ, jus própriæ ecclésiæ parœciális semper prævalére debet. || 6. When the right of another church is doubtful, the right of the proper parish church must always prevail.
r: 7. Quod antiquíssimi est institúti, illud, quantum fíeri póterit, retineátur, ut Missa, præsénte córpore defúncti, pro eo celebrétur, ántequam sepultúræ tradátur. || 7. The most ancient custom is to be kept as far as possible, namely that Mass be celebrated for the deceased, with the body present, before it is committed to burial.
r: 8. Missa exsequiális pro defúnctis celebrári póterit, étiamsi Festum duplex vel dies Domínica occúrrerit; dúmmodo Missa conventuális vel parœciális, et Offícia divína non impediántur, magnáque diei celébritas non obstet, juxta rubrícas Missális. || 8. The funeral Mass for the dead may be celebrated even if a double feast or a Sunday occurs, provided the conventual or parish Mass and the Divine Offices are not hindered, and the great solemnity of the day does not prevent it, according to the rubrics of the Missal.
r: 9. Districte prohibétur ne quis, sepultúræ vel exsequiárum seu anniversárii mortuórum causa, quidquam exígat ultra id quod in diœcesáno taxárum índice statúitur. || 9. It is strictly forbidden for anyone to demand anything, on account of burial, funeral, or anniversary of the dead, beyond what is laid down in the diocesan schedule of fees.
r: 10. Cum autem antiquíssimi ritus ecclesiástici sit, céreos accénsos in exséquiis et funéribus deférre, cáveant Parochi aliíque Sacerdótes ne ejúsmodi ritus omittátur, ac ne quid aváre aut indígne in eo committátur. || 10. Since it is a most ancient rite of the Church to carry lighted candles at obsequies and funerals, pastors and other priests shall take care that this rite is not omitted, and that nothing is done in it out of avarice or unworthily.
r: 11. Páuperes vero, quibus mórtuis nihil, aut tam parum súperest, ut consuétis impénsis humári non possint, gratis omníno ac decénter funeréntur et sepeliántur, cum exséquiis, secúndum litúrgicas leges et diœcesána statúta, præscríptis; ac débita lúmina suis impénsis, si opus sit, adhíbeant Sacerdótes, ad quos defúncti cura pértinet, vel áliqua pia confratérnitas, si sit, juxta loci consuetúdinem. || 11. The poor, however, who at death leave nothing, or so little that they cannot be buried at the usual expense, are to be given a funeral and buried entirely free of charge and with decency, with the obsequies prescribed by the liturgical laws and diocesan statutes; and if necessary the priests to whom the care of the deceased belongs, or some pious confraternity if there is one, shall provide the proper lights at their own expense, according to local custom.
r: 12. Sacérdos, aut cujúsvis órdinis Cléricus defúnctus, véstibus suis communibus, usque ad talárem vestem inclusíve, tum désuper sacro vestítu sacerdotáli, vel clericáli, quem órdinis sui rátio depóscit, quantum fíeri potest, induátur; unúsquisque autem cum tonsúra et biréto. || 12. A deceased priest, or cleric of any order, is to be clothed in his ordinary garments up to and including the cassock, and over them, as far as possible, in the sacred priestly or clerical vesture which his order requires; each one, moreover, with the tonsure and biretta.
r: 13. Sacérdos quidem super talárem vestem, amíctu, alba, cíngulo, manípulo, stola et cásula seu planéta colóris violácei sit indútus. || 13. A priest, over the cassock, is to be vested in amice, alb, cincture, maniple, stole, and chasuble or planeta of violet color.
r: 14. Diáconus vero induátur amíctu, alba, cíngulo, manípulo, stola super húmerum sinístrum, quæ sub axílla déxtera annectátur, et dalmática violácei colóris. || 14. A deacon is to be vested in amice, alb, cincture, maniple, stole over the left shoulder fastened under the right arm, and a violet dalmatic.
r: 15. Subdiáconus autem amíctu, alba, cíngulo, manípulo, et tunicélla colóris violácei. || 15. A subdeacon, in amice, alb, cincture, maniple, and a violet tunicle.
r: 16. Alii prætérea inferióris órdinis Clérici superpellíceo supra vestem talárem ornári debent. || 16. Other clerics of lower order are to be clothed in a surplice over the cassock.
r: 17. Láici cadáver, géneris aut dignitátis cujúsvis ille fúerit, Clérici ne déferant. || 17. Clerics are not to carry the body of a layman, whatever his birth or rank.
r: 18. Córpora defunctórum in ecclésia ponénda sunt pédibus versus Altáre majus; vel si funeréntur in oratóriis, aut capéllis, ponántur cum pédibus versis ad illárum Altária: quod étiam pro situ et loco fiat in sepúlcro. Presbýteri vero hábeant caput versus Altáre. || 18. The bodies of the dead are to be placed in the church with the feet toward the high altar; or, if the funeral is held in oratories or chapels, with the feet turned toward their altars; and this is also to be done in the grave, as the situation and place allow. Priests, however, are to have the head toward the altar.
r: 19. Pállia, aut ália Altáris ornaménta, ad ornátum féretri vel tumbæ ne adhibeántur. || 19. Altar cloths or other altar furnishings are not to be used to adorn the bier or the catafalque.
r: 20. Cadávera fidélium sepeliénda sunt in cœmetério rite benedícto. Parœ́ciæ autem suum quǽque cœmetérium hábeant, nisi unum plúribus commúne ab Ordinário loci sit legítime constitútum. || 20. The bodies of the faithful are to be buried in a duly blessed cemetery. Each parish is to have its own cemetery, unless one common to several has been lawfully established by the local Ordinary.
r: 21. Sepúlcra Sacerdótum et Clericórum cujuscúmque órdinis, ubi fíeri potest, a sepúlcris laicórum separáta sint, ac decentióre loco sita; prætérea, ubi id cómmodum fúerit, ália pro Sacerdótibus, ália pro inferióris órdinis Ecclésiæ minístris paréntur. || 21. The graves of priests and of clerics of any order are, where possible, to be separate from the graves of the laity and situated in a more honorable place; moreover, where convenient, some are to be prepared for priests and others for ministers of the Church of lower order.
r: 22. In ecclésiis cadávera ne sepeliántur, nisi agátur de cadavéribus Episcopórum residentiálium, Abbátum vel Prælatórum nullíus in própria ecclésia sepeliéndis, vel Románi Pontíficis, regálium personárum aut S. R. E. Cardinálium. || 22. Bodies are not to be buried in churches, unless it is a question of the bodies of residential bishops, or of abbots or prelates nullius, to be buried in their own church, or of the Roman Pontiff, royal persons, or Cardinals of the Holy Roman Church.
r: 23. Subtus altáre nullum sit recónditum cadáver; cadávera autem quæ prope altáre sepúlta forte sunt, distent ab eo saltem spátio unius metri; secus Missam in altári celebráre non licet, donec cadáver removeátur. || 23. No body may be placed beneath an altar; bodies that happen to be buried near an altar must be at least one meter away from it; otherwise it is not lawful to celebrate Mass at that altar until the body is removed.
r: 24. Ceterum nemo christiánus in communióne fidélium defúnctus, extra ecclésiam, aut cœmetérium rite benedíctum sepelíri debet; sed si necéssitas cogat ex áliquo evéntu aliquándo ad tempus áliter fíeri, curétur, ut, quátenus fíeri possit, corpus in locum sacrum quamprímum transferátur; et intérim semper Crux cápiti illíus appóni debet, ad significándum illum in Christo quievísse. || 24. Moreover, no Christian who has died in the communion of the faithful ought to be buried outside a church or a duly blessed cemetery; but if necessity, through some circumstance, should at times compel otherwise for a while, care is to be taken that the body be transferred to a sacred place as soon as possible; and meanwhile a cross must always be placed at his head, to signify that he has fallen asleep in Christ.
r: 25. Nullum porro cadáver perpétuæ sepultúræ ecclesiásticæ ubívis tráditum exhumáre licet, nisi de licéntia Ordinárii. || 25. Furthermore, it is not lawful to exhume any body committed anywhere to permanent ecclesiastical burial, except with the permission of the Ordinary.

# burial-denied | De iis quibus neganda est ecclesiastica sepultura | Those to Whom Church Burial Is to Be Denied | The Dying & the Dead | Tit. VI, cap. 2
r: 1. Ignoráre non debet Párochus, qui ab ecclesiástica sepultúra ipso jure sunt excludéndi, ne quemquam ad illam contra sacrórum Canónum decréta umquam admíttat. || 1. The pastor must not be ignorant of who are excluded from ecclesiastical burial by the law itself, so that he never admits anyone to it against the decrees of the sacred canons.
r: 2. Ad sepultúram ecclesiásticam non sunt admitténdi qui sine Baptísmo decésserint. || 2. Those who have died without Baptism are not to be admitted to ecclesiastical burial.
r: Catechúmeni, qui nulla sua culpa sine Baptísmo moriántur, baptizátis accenséndi sunt. || Catechumens who through no fault of their own die without Baptism are to be counted among the baptized.
r: 3. Ecclesiástica sepultúra privántur, nisi ante mortem áliqua déderint pœniténtiæ signa: || 3. The following are deprived of ecclesiastical burial, unless before death they have given some signs of repentance:
r: 1° Notórii apóstatæ a christiána fide, aut sectæ hæréticæ vel schismáticæ aut sectæ massónicæ aliísve ejúsdem géneris societátibus notórie addícti; || 1° Notorious apostates from the Christian faith, or those notoriously belonging to a heretical or schismatic sect, or to the Masonic sect or other societies of the same kind;
r: 2° Excommunicáti vel interdícti post senténtiam condemnatóriam vel declaratóriam; || 2° Those excommunicated or interdicted after a condemnatory or declaratory sentence;
r: 3° Qui se ipsi occíderint deliberáto consílio; || 3° Those who have killed themselves by deliberate design;
r: 4° Mórtui in duéllo aut ex vúlnere inde reláto; || 4° Those who have died in a duel or from a wound received in one;
r: 5° Qui mandáverint suum corpus crematióni tradi; || 5° Those who have ordered their body to be cremated;
r: 6° Alii peccatóres públici et manifésti. || 6° Other public and manifest sinners.
r: Occurrénte prædíctis in cásibus áliquo dúbio, consulátur, si tempus sinat, Ordinárius; permanénte dúbio, cadáver sepultúræ ecclesiásticæ tradátur, ita tamen ut removeátur scándalum. || If some doubt arises in the aforesaid cases, the Ordinary is to be consulted if time permits; if the doubt remains, the body is to be given ecclesiastical burial, but in such a way that scandal is removed.
r: 4. Excluso ab ecclesiástica sepultúra denegánda quoque sunt tum quǽlibet Missa exsequiális, étiam anniversária, tum ália pública offícia funébria. || 4. To one excluded from ecclesiastical burial there must also be denied any funeral Mass, even on the anniversary, and any other public funeral services.

# funeral | Exsequiarum Ordo | The Order of Funerals | The Dying & the Dead | Tit. VI, cap. 3
r: 1. Constitúto témpore quo corpus ad ecclésiam deferéndum est, convocétur Clerus, et álii qui fúneri interésse debent, et in parœciálem vel in áliam ecclésiam, juxta loci consuetúdinem, órdine convéniant; ac, datis certis campánæ signis, eo modo et ritu quo in eo loco fíeri solet, Párochus, indútus superpellíceo et stola nigra, vel étiam pluviáli ejúsdem colóris, clérico præferénte Crucem et álio aquam benedíctam, ad domum defúncti una cum áliis procédit. Distribuúntur cérei et accendúntur intortícia. || 1. At the time appointed for the body to be brought to the church, the clergy and the others who are to take part in the funeral are called together, and they assemble in order in the parish church or in another church, according to local custom; and when the customary signals have been given with the bell, in the manner and rite usual in that place, the pastor, vested in surplice and black stole, or also in a cope of the same color, with a cleric carrying the cross before him and another the holy water, goes with the others to the house of the deceased. Candles are distributed and the torches are lit.
r: Mox ordinátur procéssio, præcedéntibus laicórum confraternitátibus, si adsint: tum séquitur Clerus reguláris et sæculáris per órdinem; biníque procédunt, præláta Cruce, devóte Psalmos, ut infra, decantántes, Párocho præcedénte féretrum cum lumínibus; inde sequúntur álii funus comitántes, et pro defúncto Deum rite deprecántes sub siléntio. || Then the procession is formed, with the confraternities of laymen going first, if they are present; next follow the regular and secular clergy in order; and they go two by two, the cross being carried before them, devoutly singing the psalms as below, the pastor going before the bier with lights; after them follow the others accompanying the funeral, duly praying to God in silence for the deceased.

# funeral-children-rules | De Exsequiis Parvulorum | On the Funerals of Little Children | The Dying & the Dead | Tit. VI, cap. 6
r: 1. In primis curent Párochi, ut, juxta vetústam et laudábilem ecclesiárum consuetúdinem, parvulórum corpúscula non sepeliántur in commúnibus et promíscuis cœmeteriórum sepultúris; sed ut pro illis in parœciálibus cœmetériis, quátenus cómmode fíeri potest, speciáles et separátos ab áliis lóculos et sepultúras hábeant, in quibus non sepeliántur nisi infántes vel púeri, qui ante usum ratiónis obíerint et baptizáti fúerint. || 1. First of all, pastors shall take care that, according to the ancient and praiseworthy custom of the churches, the little bodies of children are not buried in the common and mixed graves of the cemeteries; but that in parish cemeteries, as far as can conveniently be done, they have special places and graves set apart from the others, in which none are buried except infants or children who were baptized and died before the use of reason.
r: 2. In fúnere parvulórum campánæ, si pulséntur, non sono lúgubri sed pótius festívo pulsári debent. || 2. At the funerals of little children, the bells, if they are rung, are to be rung not with a mournful but rather with a festive sound.

t: Benedíctus es, Dómine, in firmaménto cæli: * et laudábilis, et gloriósus, et superexaltátus in sǽcula. || Blessed are You, O Lord, in the firmament of heaven, * and worthy of praise, and glorious, and exalted above all for ever.
t: *Ant.* Benedícite Dóminum, omnes elécti ejus: ágite dies lætítiæ, et confitémini illi. || *Ant.* Bless the Lord, all you His elect; keep days of gladness, and give praise to Him.
r: Deinde ante Altáre dicit Sacérdos: || Then before the altar the priest says:
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Deus, qui miro órdine Angelórum ministéria hominúmque dispénsas: concéde propítius; ut, a quibus tibi ministrántibus in cælo semper assístitur, ab his in terra vita nostra muniátur. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || O God, who in a wonderful order dispose the ministries of Angels and of men, mercifully grant that our life on earth may be guarded by those who always stand ministering before You in heaven. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: 5. Ritus supérius descríptus servándus est étiam a Diácono eándem functiónem peragénte de Ordinárii loci vel Párochi licéntia, gravi de causa concedénda, quæ in casu necessitátis legítime præsúmitur. || 5. The rite described above is to be observed also by a deacon performing the same function with the permission of the local Ordinary or of the pastor, to be granted for a grave cause, which permission is lawfully presumed in a case of necessity.
