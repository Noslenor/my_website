# ship | Benedictio Navis | Blessing of a Ship | Work & Travel | Tit. VIII, cap. 9
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Propitiáre, Dómine, supplicatiónibus nostris, et béne✠dic navem istam déxtera tua sancta et omnes qui in ea vehéntur, sicut dignátus es benedícere arcam Noe ambulántem in dilúvio: pórrige eis, Dómine, déxteram tuam, sicut porrexísti beáto Petro ambulánti supra mare; et mitte sanctum Ángelum tuum de cælis, qui líberet, et custódiat eam semper a perículis univérsis, cum ómnibus quæ in ea erunt: et fámulos tuos, repúlsis adversitátibus, portu semper optábili, cursúque tranquíllo tueáris, transactísque, ac recte perféctis negótiis ómnibus, iteráto témpore ad própria cum omni gáudio revocáre dignéris: Qui vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Be favorable, O Lord, to our prayers, and with Your holy right hand ✠ bless this ship and all who will sail in it, as You deigned to bless the ark of Noah as it moved upon the flood. Stretch out Your right hand to them, O Lord, as You stretched it out to blessed Peter walking upon the sea; and send Your holy Angel from heaven to deliver and guard it always from every danger, together with all that will be in it. Protect Your servants, driving away all adversity, with an ever-welcome harbor and a calm voyage; and when all their business has been rightly accomplished, deign in due time to bring them home again with all joy: who live and reign for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Et aspergatur aqua benedicta. || And it is sprinkled with holy water.

# crops-vineyards | Benedictio super Fruges et Vineas | Blessing of Crops and Vineyards | Fields & Animals | Tit. VIII, cap. 10
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Orámus pietátem tuam, omnípotens Deus, ut has primítias creatúræ tuæ, quas áëris et plúviæ temperaménto nutríre dignátus es, bene✠dictiónis tuæ imbre perfúndas, et fructus terræ tuæ usque ad maturitátem perdúcas. Tríbue quoque pópulo tuo de tuis munéribus tibi semper grátias ágere; ut a fertilitáte terræ esuriéntium ánimas bonis ómnibus affluéntibus répleas, et egénus et pauper laudent nomen glóriæ tuæ. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || We entreat Your loving kindness, almighty God, that You would pour the shower of Your ✠ blessing upon these first-fruits of Your creation, which You have deigned to nourish with the gentle balance of air and rain, and bring the fruits of Your earth to ripeness. Grant also to Your people always to give You thanks for Your gifts; that from the fruitfulness of the earth You may fill the souls of the hungry with an abundance of all good things, and the needy and the poor may praise the name of Your glory. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Et aspergantur aqua benedicta. || And they are sprinkled with holy water.

# pilgrims-departing | Benedictio Peregrinorum ad Loca Sancta Prodeuntium | Blessing of Pilgrims Setting Out for the Holy Places | Work & Travel | Tit. VIII, cap. 11
r: Peregrini ad loca sancta profecturi, antequam discedant, juxta veteris Ecclesiæ institutum, curent accipere patentes seu commendatitias litteras a suo Ordinario, seu Parocho. Quibus obtentis, et rebus suis dispositis, facta peccatorum suorum confessione, audiunt Missam, in qua dicitur Oratio pro peregrinantibus, ad instar Collectæ pro re gravi imperatæ, et Sanctissimam Eucharistiam devote suscipiunt. Expleta Missa, Sacerdos super eos genuflexos dicit sequentes Preces: || Pilgrims about to set out for the holy places should, before they depart and according to the ancient custom of the Church, take care to obtain letters patent or letters of recommendation from their Ordinary or their pastor. Having obtained these and set their affairs in order, they confess their sins, hear Mass, in which the prayer for travelers is said in the manner of a collect ordered for a grave cause, and devoutly receive the Most Holy Eucharist. When Mass is finished, the priest says the following prayers over them as they kneel:

t: Deus, qui Ábraham púerum tuum de Ur Chaldæórum edúctum, per omnes suæ peregrinatiónis vias illǽsum custodísti: quǽsumus, ut hos fámulos tuos custodíre dignéris; esto eis, Dómine, in procínctu suffrágium, in via solátium, in æstu umbráculum, in plúvia et frígore teguméntum, in lassitúdine vehículum, in adversitáte præsídium, in lúbrico báculus, in naufrágio portus: ut te duce, quo tendunt, próspere pervéniant, et demum incólumes ad própria revertántur. || O God, who led Your servant Abraham out of Ur of the Chaldeans and kept him unharmed in all the ways of his pilgrimage: we beseech You to deign to guard these Your servants. t: Præsta, quǽsumus, omnípotens Deus: ut família tua per viam salútis incédat; et beáti Joánnis Præcursóris hortaménta sectándo, ad eum, quem prædíxit, secúra pervéniat, Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum, Fílium tuum. || Grant, we beseech You, almighty God, that Your family may walk in the way of salvation; and that, following the exhortations of blessed John the Forerunner, they may come safely to Him whom he foretold, our Lord Jesus Christ, Your Son.
t: Exáudi, Dómine, preces nostras, et iter famulórum tuórum propítius comitáre, atque misericórdiam tuam, sicut ubíque es, ita ubíque largíre: quátenus a cunctis adversitátibus tua opitulatióne defénsi, gratiárum tibi réferant actiónem. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Hear our prayers, O Lord, and graciously accompany the journey of Your servants; and as You are everywhere, so everywhere bestow Your mercy; that, defended from all adversity by Your help, they may render You thanks. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
t: Pax et benedíctio Dei omnipoténtis, Patris, et Fílii, ✠ et Spíritus Sancti, descéndat super vos, et máneat semper. ℟. Amen. || May the peace and blessing of almighty God, the Father, and the Son, ✠ and the Holy Spirit, come down upon you and remain for ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Et aspergantur aqua benedicta. Quod si unus sit peregrinaturus, Preces dicantur in numero singulari: ac si Sacerdos ipse, qui benedicit, sit socius peregrinationis, eas dicat in persona prima numeri pluralis, quatenus congruere videbitur. || And they are sprinkled with holy water. If only one person is going on pilgrimage, the prayers are said in the singular; and if the priest who gives the blessing is himself a companion on the pilgrimage, he says them in the first person plural, so far as seems fitting.
