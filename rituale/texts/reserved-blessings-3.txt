t: Exténdens cælum sicut pellem: * qui tegis aquis superióra ejus. || Stretching out the heavens like a tent, * You who cover its upper rooms with waters.
t: Qui ponis nubem ascénsum tuum: * qui ámbulas super pennas ventórum. || You make the clouds Your chariot; * You walk upon the wings of the winds.
t: Qui facis Angelos tuos, spíritus: * et minístros tuos ignem uréntem. || You make Your Angels spirits, * and Your ministers a burning fire.
t: Qui fundásti terram super stabilitátem suam: * non inclinábitur in sǽculum sǽculi. || You founded the earth upon its firm base; * it shall not be moved for ever and ever.
t: Abýssus, sicut vestiméntum, amíctus ejus: * super montes stabunt aquæ. || The deep, like a garment, is its clothing; * above the mountains the waters stand.
t: Ab increpatióne tua fúgient: * a voce tonítrui tui formidábunt. || At Your rebuke they flee; * at the voice of Your thunder they tremble.
t: Ascéndunt montes: et descéndunt campi * in locum, quem fundásti eis. || The mountains rise and the plains go down * to the place which You established for them.
t: Términum posuísti, quem non transgrediéntur: * neque converténtur operíre terram. || You set a bound which they shall not pass, * nor shall they return to cover the earth.
t: Qui emíttis fontes in convállibus: * inter médium móntium pertransíbunt aquæ. || You send forth springs in the valleys; * between the midst of the hills the waters shall pass.
t: Potábunt omnes béstiæ agri: * exspectábunt ónagri in siti sua. || All the beasts of the field shall drink; * the wild asses shall wait for them in their thirst.
t: Super ea vólucres cæli habitábunt: * de médio petrárum dabunt voces. || Above them the birds of the air shall dwell; * from the midst of the rocks they shall give forth their voices.
t: Rigans montes de superióribus suis: * de fructu óperum tuórum satiábitur terra: || You water the hills from Your upper rooms; * the earth shall be filled with the fruit of Your works,
t: Prodúcens fænum juméntis, * et herbam servitúti hóminum: || Bringing forth grass for the cattle, * and plants for the service of men,
t: Ut edúcas panem de terra: * et vinum lætíficet cor hóminis: || That You may bring bread out of the earth, * and that wine may gladden the heart of man,
t: Ut exhílaret fáciem in óleo: * et panis cor hóminis confírmet. || That he may make his face cheerful with oil, * and that bread may strengthen man's heart.
t: Saturabúntur ligna campi, et cedri Líbani, quas plantávit: * illic pásseres nidificábunt. || The trees of the field shall be filled, and the cedars of Lebanon which He planted; * there the sparrows shall make their nests.
t: Heródii domus dux est eórum: * montes excélsi cervis: petra refúgium herináciis. || The highest of them is the house of the heron; * the high hills are a refuge for the harts, the rock for the badgers.
t: Fecit lunam in témpora: * sol cognóvit occásum suum. || He made the moon to mark the seasons; * the sun knows its going down.
t: Posuísti ténebras, et facta est nox: * in ipsa pertransíbunt omnes béstiæ silvæ. || You appointed darkness, and it was night; * in it all the beasts of the forest roam about.
t: Cátuli leónum rugiéntes, ut rápiant, * et quærant a Deo escam sibi. || The young lions roar for their prey, * and seek their food from God.
t: Ortus est sol, et congregáti sunt: * et in cubílibus suis collocabúntur. || The sun rises, and they gather together, * and lie down in their dens.
t: Exíbit homo ad opus suum: * et ad operatiónem suam usque ad vésperum. || Man goes forth to his work, * and to his labor until the evening.
t: Quam magnificáta sunt ópera tua, Dómine! * ómnia in sapiéntia fecísti: impléta est terra possessióne tua. || How great are Your works, O Lord! * You have made all things in wisdom; the earth is filled with Your riches.
t: Hoc mare magnum, et spatiósum mánibus: * illic reptília, quorum non est númerus. || So is this great sea, which stretches wide its arms; * there are creeping things without number,
t: Animália pusílla cum magnis: * illic naves pertransíbunt. || Creatures small and great; * there the ships shall pass.
t: Draco iste, quem formásti ad illudéndum ei: * ómnia a te exspéctant ut des illis escam in témpore. || This sea-dragon which You formed to play therein: * all look to You to give them their food in due season.
t: Dante te illis, cólligent: * aperiénte te manum tuam, ómnia implebúntur bonitáte. || When You give to them, they shall gather it up; * when You open Your hand, they shall all be filled with good.
t: Averténte autem te fáciem, turbabúntur: * áuferes spíritum eórum, et defícient, et in púlverem suum reverténtur. || But when You turn away Your face, they shall be troubled; * You shall take away their breath, and they shall fail and return to their dust.
t: Emíttes spíritum tuum, et creabúntur: * et renovábis fáciem terræ. || You shall send forth Your Spirit, and they shall be created; * and You shall renew the face of the earth.
t: Sit glória Dómini in sǽculum: * lætábitur Dóminus in opéribus suis: || May the glory of the Lord endure for ever; * the Lord shall rejoice in His works.
t: Qui réspicit terram, et facit eam trémere: * qui tangit montes, et fúmigant. || He looks upon the earth and makes it tremble; * He touches the mountains, and they smoke.
t: Cantábo Dómino in vita mea: * psallam Deo meo, quámdiu sum. || I will sing to the Lord as long as I live; * I will sing psalms to my God while I have being.
t: Jucúndum sit ei elóquium meum: * ego vero delectábor in Dómino. || May my words be pleasing to Him; * but I will take delight in the Lord.
t: Defíciant peccatóres a terra, et iníqui ita ut non sint: * bénedic, ánima mea, Dómino. || Let sinners vanish from the earth, and the wicked, so that they be no more. * Bless the Lord, O my soul.

Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
