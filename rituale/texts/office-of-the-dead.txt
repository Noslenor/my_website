# office-dead-vespers | Officium Defunctorum: Ad Vesperas | Office of the Dead: Vespers | The Dying & the Dead | Tit. VI, cap. 4
r: Dicitur in Choro in die depositionis et aliis diebus pro temporis opportunitate et ecclesiarum consuetudine. || It is said in choir on the day of burial and on other days as occasion offers and according to the custom of the churches.
r: In die vero depositionis, in die post acceptum mortis nuntium, et tertio, septimo, trigesimo et anniversario, etiam late sumpto, et quoties sollemniter celebratur Officium, duplicantur Antiphonæ; et in fine omnium Psalmorum semper dicitur: Réquiem ætérnam * dona eis, Dómine. Et lux perpétua * lúceat eis; etiam si pro uno tantum fiat Officium. (Psalmi incipiuntur ut infra notatur, etiam quando non duplicantur Antiphonæ.) || On the day of burial, on the day after news of the death is received, on the third, seventh and thirtieth day and on the anniversary (even in the broad sense), and whenever the Office is celebrated solemnly, the antiphons are doubled; and at the end of every psalm there is always said: Eternal rest * grant unto them, O Lord. And let perpetual light * shine upon them; even if the Office is said for one person only. (The psalms are begun as noted below, even when the antiphons are not doubled.)

h: In die depositionis defuncti Oratio || Prayer on the Day of Burial of the Deceased
t: Absólve, quǽsumus, Dómine, ánimam fámuli tui N. (fámulæ tuæ N.), ut defúnctus (-a) sǽculo tibi vivat: et quæ per fragilitátem carnis humána conversatióne commísit, tu vénia misericordíssimæ pietátis abstérge. Per Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti Deus, per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Absolve, we beseech You, O Lord, the soul of Your servant N., that, having died to the world, he (she) may live to You; and whatever sins he (she) committed in human conduct through the frailty of the flesh, wipe away by the pardon of Your most merciful kindness. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, Your Son, who lives and reigns with You in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Vel alia Oratio: || Or another prayer:
t: Deus, cui próprium est miseréri semper et párcere, te súpplices exorámus pro ánima fámuli tui N. (fámulæ tuæ N.) quam hódie de hoc sǽculo migráre jussísti: ut non tradas eam in manus inimíci, neque obliviscáris in finem, sed júbeas eam a sanctis Angelis súscipi, et ad pátriam paradísi perdúci; ut, quia in te sperávit et crédidit, non pœnas inférni sustíneat, sed gáudia ætérna possídeat. Per Dóminum nostrum. || O God, whose property it is ever to have mercy and to spare, we humbly entreat You for the soul of Your servant N., whom You have today bidden to pass out of this world: do not deliver it into the hands of the enemy, nor forget it for ever, but command it to be taken up by the holy Angels and led to the homeland of paradise; that, since it hoped and believed in You, it may not suffer the pains of hell, but may possess eternal joys. Through our Lord.
h: In die tertio, septimo et trigesimo depositionis defuncti Oratio || Prayer on the Third, Seventh and Thirtieth Day after Burial

Per Dóminum nostrum. || We beseech You, O Lord, that to the soul of Your servant N., the third (or seventh, or thirtieth) day of whose burial we commemorate, You would deign to grant the fellowship of Your Saints and elect, and pour upon it the unfailing dew of Your mercy. Through our Lord.
h: In Anniversario Oratio || Prayer on the Anniversary

Per Dóminum nostrum. || O God, Lord of pardon, grant to the soul of Your servant N.
