# sacraments-general | De iis quæ in Administratione Sacramentorum generaliter servanda sunt | What Is to Be Observed Generally in the Administration of the Sacraments | General | Tit. I
r: 1. Ut ea, quæ ex antiquis catholicæ Ecclesiæ institutis, et sacrorum Canonum, Summorumque Pontificum Decretis, de Sacramentorum ritibus ac cæremoniis hoc libro præscribuntur, qua par est diligentia ac religione custodiantur, et ubique fideliter observentur; illud ante omnia scire, et observare convenit, quod Sacrosancta Tridentina Synodus Sess. VII, Can. XIII, de iis ritibus decrevit in hæc verba: || 1. In order that what is prescribed in this book concerning the rites and ceremonies of the Sacraments, drawn from the ancient institutions of the Catholic Church and from the decrees of the sacred Canons and of the Supreme Pontiffs, may be kept with due diligence and reverence and faithfully observed everywhere, it is fitting above all to know and observe what the most holy Synod of Trent decreed concerning these rites in Session VII, Canon XIII, in these words:
r: 2. Si quis dixerit, receptos et approbatos Ecclesiæ Catholicæ ritus in sollemni Sacramentorum administratione adhiberi consuetos, aut contemni, aut sine peccato a Ministris pro libito omitti, aut in novos alios per quemcumque Ecclesiarum Pastorem mutari posse: anathema sit. || 2. "If anyone says that the received and approved rites of the Catholic Church customarily used in the solemn administration of the Sacraments may be despised, or omitted at will by the ministers without sin, or changed into other new rites by any pastor of the churches whatsoever: let him be anathema."
r: 3. Cum igitur in Ecclesia Dei nihil sanctius, aut utilius, nihilque excellentius, aut magis divinum habeatur, quam Sacramenta ad humani generis salutem a Christo Domino instituta, Parochus, vel quivis alius Sacerdos, ad quem eorum administratio pertinet, meminisse in primis debet, se sancta tractare, atque omni fere temporis momento ad tam sanctæ administrationis officium paratum esse oportere. || 3. Since, therefore, nothing in the Church of God is held to be holier or more useful, nothing more excellent or more divine, than the Sacraments instituted by Christ the Lord for the salvation of mankind, the pastor, or any other priest to whom their administration belongs, must above all remember that he handles holy things, and that he ought to be ready at almost every moment for the duty of so holy a ministry.
r: 4. Quamobrem illud perpetuo curabit, ut integre, caste, pieque vitam agat; nam etsi Sacramenta ab impuris coinquinari non possint, neque a pravis Ministris eorum effectus impediri: impure tamen et indigne ea ministrantes, in æternæ mortis reatum incurrunt. Sacerdos ergo, si sit peccati mortalis sibi conscius (quod absit), ad Sacramentorum administrationem ne audeat accedere, nisi prius corde pœniteat; sed si habeat copiam Confessarii, et temporis locique ratio ferat, convenit confiteri. || 4. He shall therefore take constant care to lead an upright, chaste, and devout life; for although the Sacraments cannot be defiled by the impure, nor their effect be hindered by wicked ministers, yet those who administer them impurely and unworthily incur the guilt of eternal death. Hence a priest who is conscious of mortal sin (which God forbid) should not dare to approach the administration of the Sacraments unless he first repents in his heart; and if a confessor is available, and time and place permit, it is fitting that he confess.
r: 5. Quacumque diei ac noctis hora ad Sacramenta ministranda vocabitur, nullam officio suo præstando (præsertim si necessitas urgeat) moram interponat. Ac propterea populum sæpe, prout sese offeret occasio, præmonebit, ut, cum sacro ministerio opus erit, se quamprimum advocet, nulla temporis, aut cujuscumque incommodi habita ratione. || 5. At whatever hour of the day or night he is called to administer the Sacraments, he shall allow no delay in fulfilling his duty, especially if necessity is pressing. And for this reason he shall often admonish the people, as occasion offers, to call him as soon as possible whenever the sacred ministry is needed, without regard to the time or to any inconvenience.
r: 6. Ipse vero, antequam ad hujusmodi administrationem accedat, paululum, si opportunitas dabitur, orationi, et sacræ rei, quam acturus est, meditationi vacabit, atque ordinem ministrandi, et cæremonias pro temporis spatio prævidebit et perleget. || 6. Before he proceeds to such an administration, he shall, if opportunity allows, give a little time to prayer and to meditation on the sacred action he is about to perform, and, as time permits, he shall look over and read through beforehand the order of administration and the ceremonies.
r: 7. In omni Sacramentorum administratione superpelliceo sit indutus, et desuper stola ejus coloris, quem Sacramenti ritus exposcit; nisi in Sacramento Pœnitentiæ ministrando occasio, vel consuetudo, vel locus interdum aliter suadeat. || 7. In every administration of the Sacraments he is to wear a surplice, and over it a stole of the color which the rite of the Sacrament requires; except that in administering the Sacrament of Penance the occasion, custom, or place may sometimes suggest otherwise.
r: 8. Adhibebit quoque unum saltem, si habeat, vel plures Clericos, prout loci et Sacramenti ratio postulabit, decenti habitu, et superpelliceo pariter indutos. || 8. He shall also employ at least one cleric, if he has one, or several, as the place and the Sacrament require, in becoming dress and likewise vested in surplice.
r: 9. Curabit etiam, ut sacra supellex, vestes, ornamenta, linteamina, et vasa ministerii integra, nitidaque sint et munda. || 9. He shall also see to it that the sacred furnishings, vestments, ornaments, linens, and vessels of the ministry are whole, neat, and clean.
r: 10. In Sacramentorum administratione eorum virtutem, usum, ac utilitatem, et cæremoniarum significationes, ut Concilium Tridentinum præcipit, ex sanctorum Patrum et Catechismi Romani doctrina, ubi commode fieri potest, diligenter explicabit. || 10. In administering the Sacraments he shall, wherever it can conveniently be done, carefully explain their power, use, and benefit, and the meaning of the ceremonies, as the Council of Trent commands, according to the teaching of the holy Fathers and of the Roman Catechism.
r: 11. Dum Sacramentum aliquod ministrat, singula verba, quæ ad illius formam et ministerium pertinent, attente, distincte, et pie, atque clara voce pronuntiabit. Similiter et alias orationes et preces devote ac religiose dicet; nec memoriæ, quæ plerumque labitur, facile confidet, sed omnia recitabit ex libro. Reliquas præterea cæremonias ac ritus ita decenter, gravique actione peraget, ut adstantes ad cælestium rerum cogitationem erigat, et attentos reddat. || 11. While administering any Sacrament, he shall pronounce each of the words belonging to its form and ministry attentively, distinctly, devoutly, and in a clear voice. Likewise he shall say the other prayers and petitions devoutly and reverently; nor shall he readily trust his memory, which often fails, but shall recite everything from the book. Moreover, he shall carry out the remaining ceremonies and rites with such decorum and gravity of action as to raise those present to the thought of heavenly things and make them attentive.
r: 12. Ad ministrandum procedens, rei, quam tractaturus est, intentus sit, nec de iis, quæ ad ipsam non pertinent, quidquam cum alio colloquatur; in ipsaque administratione actualem attentionem habere studeat, vel saltem virtualem, cum intentione faciendi, quod in eo facit Ecclesia. || 12. When he goes to administer a Sacrament, let him be intent on the matter he is about to perform, and not converse with anyone about things unrelated to it; and in the administration itself let him strive to have actual attention, or at least virtual attention, with the intention of doing what the Church does in that Sacrament.
r: 13. Illud porro diligenter caveat, ne in Sacramentorum administratione aliquid, quavis de causa vel occasione, directe vel indirecte, exigat, aut petat; sed ea gratis ministret, et ab omni simoniæ, atque avaritiæ suspicione, nedum crimine, longissime absit. Si quid vero nomine eleemosynæ, aut devotionis studio, peracto jam Sacramento, sponte a fidelibus offeratur, id licite pro consuetudine locorum accipere poterit, nisi aliter Episcopo videatur. Licet tamen exigere aut petere oblationes seu taxas quæ a Concilio provinciali aut a conventu Episcoporum provinciæ fuerint præfinitæ et ab Apostolica Sede approbatæ. Gratuitum vero ministerium ne deneget Parochus iis qui solvendo pares non sunt. || 13. Further, he must carefully beware of demanding or asking anything, for any reason or on any occasion, directly or indirectly, in the administration of the Sacraments; rather let him administer them freely, and keep far removed from all suspicion, let alone the crime, of simony and avarice. But if, once the Sacrament has been administered, something is freely offered by the faithful by way of alms or out of devotion, he may lawfully accept it according to the custom of the place, unless the bishop judges otherwise. It is, however, lawful to demand or ask the offerings or fees fixed by a provincial council or by a meeting of the bishops of the province and approved by the Apostolic See. But the pastor must not refuse his ministry free of charge to those who are unable to pay.
r: 14. Vetitum est Sacramenta Ecclesiæ ministrare hæreticis aut schismaticis, etiam bona fide errantibus eaque petentibus, nisi prius, erroribus rejectis, Ecclesiæ reconciliati fuerint. || 14. It is forbidden to administer the Sacraments of the Church to heretics or schismatics, even if they err in good faith and ask for them, unless they have first renounced their errors and been reconciled to the Church.
r: 15. Omnes autem, qui Sacramenta suscipiunt, loco et tempore opportuno monebit, ut, remoto inani colloquio, et habitu actuque indecenti, pie ac devote Sacramentis intersint, et ea, qua par est, reverentia suscipiant. || 15. He shall admonish all who receive the Sacraments, at a suitable time and place, to lay aside idle talk and unbecoming dress and behavior, to be present at the Sacraments piously and devoutly, and to receive them with due reverence.
r: 16. Librum hunc Ritualem (ubi opus erit) semper cum ministrabit, secum habebit, ritusque et cæremonias in eo præscriptas diligenter servabit. || 16. Whenever he administers (where it is needed) he shall always have this Ritual with him, and shall carefully observe the rites and ceremonies prescribed in it.
r: 17. Ceterum illorum tantum Sacramentorum, quorum administratio ad Parochos pertinet, ritus hoc opere præscribuntur, cujusmodi sunt Baptismus, Pœnitentia, Eucharistia, Extrema Unctio, et Matrimonium. Reliqua vero duo Sacramenta, Confirmationis et Ordinis, cum propria sint Episcoporum, ritus suos habent in Pontificali præscriptos. Et ea, quæ de iis, atque aliis Sacramentis scire, servare, et docere Parochi debent, cum ex aliis libris, tum præcipue ex Catechismo Romano et Codice Juris Canonici sumi possunt. Siquidem hic de iis fere tantum agere instituti operis ratio postulat, quæ ad ipsorum quinque Sacramentorum ritus pertinent. || 17. In this work, moreover, only the rites of those Sacraments whose administration belongs to pastors are prescribed, namely Baptism, Penance, the Eucharist, Extreme Unction, and Matrimony. The other two Sacraments, Confirmation and Orders, being proper to bishops, have their rites prescribed in the Pontifical. What pastors must know, observe, and teach about these and the other Sacraments may be drawn from other books, and especially from the Roman Catechism and the Code of Canon Law; for the plan of the present work requires it to treat almost exclusively of what pertains to the rites of these five Sacraments.
r: 18. Postremo quisquis Sacramenta administrare tenetur, habeat libros necessarios ad officium suum pertinentes, eosque præsertim, in quibus variarum parochialium functionum notæ ad futuram rei memoriam describuntur, ut ad finem hujus Ritualis habetur. || 18. Finally, whoever is bound to administer the Sacraments should have the books necessary for his office, and especially those in which the records of the various parochial functions are entered for future remembrance, as set out at the end of this Ritual.

# baptism-rules | De Sacramento Baptismi rite administrando | On the Right Administration of the Sacrament of Baptism | Sacraments | Tit. II, cap. 1
r: 1. Sacrum Baptisma, christianæ religionis et æternæ vitæ janua, quod inter alia novæ Legis Sacramenta a Christo instituta primum tenet locum, omnibus in re vel saltem in voto necessarium esse ad salutem, ipsa Veritas testatur illis verbis: Nisi quis renatus fuerit ex aqua et Spiritu Sancto, non potest introire in regnum Dei. (Joann. 3, 5.) Itaque summa ad illud opportune, riteque administrandum ac suscipiendum diligentia adhibenda est. || 1. Holy Baptism, the gateway to the Christian religion and to eternal life, which holds the first place among the other Sacraments of the New Law instituted by Christ, is necessary for salvation for all, either in fact or at least in desire, as Truth itself testifies in these words: "Unless one is born again of water and the Holy Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God" (John 3:5). The greatest care must therefore be taken that it be administered and received at the proper time and in the proper manner.
r: 2. Cum autem ad hoc Sacramentum conferendum alia sint de jure divino absolute necessaria, ut materia, forma, minister; alia ad illius sollemnitatem pertineant, ut ritus, ac cæremoniæ, quas ex Apostolica et antiquissima traditione acceptas et approbatas, nisi necessitatis causa, omittere non licet; de iis aliqua præmonenda sunt, ut sacrum hoc ministerium rite et sancte peragatur. || 2. Since, in conferring this Sacrament, some things are absolutely necessary by divine law, namely the matter, the form, and the minister, while others belong to its solemnity, namely the rites and ceremonies, which have been received and approved from Apostolic and most ancient tradition and may not be omitted except in case of necessity, some points concerning these must be set out beforehand, so that this sacred ministry may be carried out duly and holily.
r: 3. Cum Baptismus ministratur servatis omnibus ritibus et cæremoniis quæ in hoc Rituali libro præcipiuntur, appellatur sollemnis; secus, non sollemnis seu privatus. || 3. When Baptism is administered with all the rites and ceremonies prescribed in this Ritual observed, it is called solemn; otherwise it is called non-solemn or private.
h: De materia Baptismi || The Matter of Baptism
r: 4. Primum, intellegat Parochus, cum hujus Sacramenti materia sit aqua vera ac naturalis, nullum alium liquorem ad id adhiberi posse. || 4. First, the pastor should understand that, since the matter of this Sacrament is true and natural water, no other liquid may be used for it.
r: 5. Aqua vero sollemnis Baptismi sit eo anno benedicta in pervigilio Paschatis vel Pentecostes, quæ in fonte mundo nitida et pura diligenter conservetur; et hæc, quando nova benedicenda est, in ecclesiæ, vel potius Baptisterii sacrarium effundatur. || 5. The water for solemn Baptism should be water blessed that year on the vigil of Easter or of Pentecost, carefully kept clear and pure in a clean font; and when new water is to be blessed, the old is to be poured into the sacrarium of the church, or rather of the baptistery.
r: 6. Si aqua benedicta in Baptisterio adeo sit imminuta, ut minus videatur sufficere, alia non benedicta admisceatur, etiam iterato, minore tamen copia. Si vero corrupta fuerit, aut effluxerit, aut quovis modo defecerit, Parochus in fontem bene mundatum ac nitidum, recentem aquam infundat, eamque benedicat ex formula, quæ infra præscribitur. || 6. If the blessed water in the baptistery has so diminished that it seems insufficient, other unblessed water may be mixed with it, even repeatedly, but in a smaller quantity. If, however, it has become foul, or has leaked away, or has failed in any way, the pastor is to pour fresh water into the well-cleaned and polished font and bless it according to the formula prescribed below.
r: 7. Sed si aqua conglaciata sit, curetur, ut liquefiat: sin autem ex parte congelata sit, aut nimium frigida, poterit parum aquæ naturalis non benedictæ calefacere, et admiscere aquæ baptismali in vasculo ad id parato, et ea tepefacta ad baptizandum uti, ne noceat infantulo. || 7. If the water is frozen, it should be thawed; but if it is partly frozen or too cold, a little natural unblessed water may be warmed and mixed with the baptismal water in a small vessel prepared for the purpose, and this warmed water used for baptizing, so that the infant may not be harmed.
h: De forma Baptismi || The Form of Baptism
r: 8. Quoniam Baptismi forma his verbis expressa: *Ego te baptízo in nómine Patris, et Fílii, et Spíritus Sancti,* omnino necessaria est; ideo eam nullo modo licet mutare, sed eadem verba uno et eodem tempore, quo fit ablutio, pronuntianda sunt. || 8. Since the form of Baptism, expressed in these words, *I baptize you in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,* is absolutely necessary, it may in no way be changed; and these same words are to be pronounced at one and the same time as the washing is done.
r: 9. Latinus Presbyter latina forma semper utatur. Cum Baptismum absolute iterare nullo modo liceat, si quis sub condicione (de quo infra) sit baptizandus, ea condicio explicanda est hoc modo: *Si non es baptizátus, ego te baptízo in nómine Patris, etc.* Hac tamen condicionali forma non passim aut leviter uti licet, sed prudenter; et ubi, re diligenter pervestigata, prudens dubium exsistat, num revera vel num valide Sacramentum Baptismi collatum fuerit. || 9. A Latin priest is always to use the Latin form. Since it is in no way permitted to repeat Baptism absolutely, if anyone is to be baptized conditionally (concerning which see below), the condition is to be expressed in this way: *If you are not baptized, I baptize you in the name of the Father,* etc. This conditional form, however, may not be used indiscriminately or lightly, but with prudence, and only where, after careful investigation, a prudent doubt remains whether the Sacrament of Baptism was really, or validly, conferred.
r: 10. Licet Baptismus conferri valide possit aut per infusionem aquæ, aut per immersionem, aut per aspersionem; primus tamen, vel secundus modus, aut mixtus ex utroque, qui magis sit in usu, pro Ecclesiarum consuetudine retineatur; ita ut trina ablutione caput baptizandi perfundatur, vel immergatur in modum crucis uno et eodem tempore, quo verba proferuntur, et idem sit aquam adhibens et verba pronuntians. || 10. Although Baptism can be validly conferred by pouring water, by immersion, or by sprinkling, the first or second manner, or a mixture of both, whichever is more in use, is to be retained according to the custom of the churches; so that the head of the one being baptized is washed with a threefold pouring, or is immersed, in the form of a cross, at one and the same time as the words are spoken; and the one applying the water and the one pronouncing the words must be the same person.
r: 11. Ubi vero Baptismus fit per infusionem aquæ, cavendum est, ne aqua ex infantis capite in fontem, sed vel in sacrarium Baptisterii prope ipsum fontem exstructum defluat, aut in aliquo vase ad hunc usum parato recepta, in ipsius Baptisterii, vel in ecclesiæ sacrarium effundatur. || 11. Where Baptism is given by pouring water, care must be taken that the water does not flow from the infant's head back into the font, but either runs into the sacrarium of the baptistery built near the font, or is caught in a vessel prepared for this purpose and poured into the sacrarium of the baptistery or of the church.
h: De ministro Baptismi || The Minister of Baptism
r: 12. Minister ordinarius Baptismi sollemnis est Sacerdos; sed ejus collatio reservatur Parocho vel alii Sacerdoti de ejusdem Parochi vel Ordinarii loci licentia, quæ in casu necessitatis legitime præsumitur. || 12. The ordinary minister of solemn Baptism is a priest; but its conferral is reserved to the pastor, or to another priest with the permission of that pastor or of the local Ordinary, which in case of necessity is lawfully presumed.
r: Etiam peregrinus a Parocho proprio in sua Parœcia sollemniter baptizetur, si id facile et sine mora fieri potest; secus peregrinum quilibet Parochus in suo territorio potest sollemniter baptizare. || A traveler, too, should be solemnly baptized by his own pastor in his own parish, if this can be done easily and without delay; otherwise any pastor may solemnly baptize a traveler within his own territory.
r: 13. In alieno territorio nemini licet, sine debita licentia, Baptismum sollemnem conferre ne sui quidem loci incolis. || 13. No one may, without due permission, confer solemn Baptism in another's territory, not even on residents of his own place.
r: 14. Ubi parœciæ aut quasi-parœciæ nondum sunt constitutæ, statutorum peculiarium et receptarum consuetudinum ratio habenda est, ut constet cuinam Sacerdoti, præter Ordinarium, in universo territorio vel in ejus parte jus insit baptizandi. || 14. Where parishes or quasi-parishes have not yet been established, account must be taken of particular statutes and accepted customs, to determine which priest, besides the Ordinary, has the right to baptize in the whole territory or in a part of it.
r: 15. Extraordinarius Baptismi sollemnis minister est Diaconus; qui tamen sua potestate ne utatur sine loci Ordinarii vel Parochi licentia, justa de causa concedenda, quæ, ubi necessitas urgeat, legitime præsumitur. || 15. The extraordinary minister of solemn Baptism is a deacon; he may not, however, use this power without the permission of the local Ordinary or of the pastor, to be granted for a just cause, which is lawfully presumed when necessity is pressing.
r: 16. Baptismus non sollemnis, in mortis periculo, potest a quovis ministrari, servata debita materia, forma et intentione; quatenus vero fieri potest, adhibeantur duo testes vel saltem unus, quibus baptismi collatio probari possit. || 16. Non-solemn Baptism may, in danger of death, be administered by anyone, provided the proper matter, form, and intention are observed; as far as possible, however, two witnesses, or at least one, should be present, by whom the conferral of baptism can be proved.
r: Si tamen adsit Sacerdos, Diacono præferatur, Diaconus Subdiacono, Clericus laico et vir feminæ, nisi pudoris gratia deceat feminam potius quam virum baptizare, vel nisi femina noverit melius formam et modum baptizandi. || If a priest is present, however, he is to be preferred to a deacon, a deacon to a subdeacon, a cleric to a layman, and a man to a woman, unless for modesty's sake it is more fitting for a woman to baptize rather than a man, or unless the woman knows the form and manner of baptizing better.
r: Patri aut matri suam prolem baptizare non licet, præterquam in mortis periculo, quando alius præsto non est, qui baptizet. || A father or mother may not baptize their own child, except in danger of death when no one else is at hand to baptize.
r: 17. Curet Parochus ut fideles, præsertim obstetrices, medici et chirurgi, rectum baptizandi modum pro casu necessitatis probe ediscant. || 17. The pastor should see to it that the faithful, especially midwives, physicians, and surgeons, thoroughly learn the correct manner of baptizing in case of necessity.
r: 18. Adultorum Baptismus, ubi commode fieri possit, ad loci Ordinarium deferatur, ut, si voluerit, ab eo vel ab ejus delegato sollemnius conferatur. || 18. The Baptism of adults, where this can conveniently be done, should be referred to the local Ordinary, so that, if he wishes, it may be conferred more solemnly by him or by his delegate.
h: De baptizandis parvulis || The Baptism of Infants
r: 19. Cum agitur de Baptismo: || 19. Where Baptism is concerned:
r: 1° Parvulorum seu infantium nomine veniunt, qui nondum rationis usum adepti sunt, eisdemque accensentur amentes ab infantia, in quavis ætate constituti; || 1° Under the name of little ones or infants are included those who have not yet attained the use of reason; and with them are counted those who have lacked the use of reason from infancy, at whatever age;
r: 2° Adulti autem censentur, qui rationis usu fruuntur; idque satis est ut suo quisque animi motu Baptismum petat et ad illum admittatur. || 2° Those are considered adults who enjoy the use of reason; and this suffices for each to ask for Baptism of his own accord and to be admitted to it.
r: 20. Nemo in utero matris clausus baptizetur, donec probabilis spes sit ut rite editus baptizari possit. || 20. No one enclosed in the mother's womb is to be baptized as long as there is a probable hope that the child can be baptized after being properly born.
r: Si infans caput emiserit et periculum mortis immineat, baptizetur in capite; nec postea, si vivus evaserit, est iterum sub condicione baptizandus. || If the infant has put forth its head and danger of death is imminent, it is to be baptized on the head; and afterwards, if it survives, it is not to be baptized again conditionally.
r: Si aliud membrum emiserit, in illo, si periculum immineat, baptizetur sub condicione; at tunc, si natus vixerit, est rursus sub condicione baptizandus. || If it has put forth another limb, it is to be baptized conditionally on that limb, if danger is imminent; but then, if it lives after birth, it must be baptized again conditionally.
r: Si mater prægnans mortua fuerit, fetus ab iis ad quos spectat extractus, si certo vivat, baptizetur absolute; si dubie, sub condicione. || If a pregnant mother has died, the fetus, having been extracted by those whose duty it is, is to be baptized absolutely if it is certainly alive, and conditionally if this is doubtful.
r: Fetus, in utero baptizatus, post ortum denuo sub condicione baptizari debet. || A fetus baptized in the womb must be baptized again conditionally after birth.
r: 21. Curandum ut omnes fetus abortivi, quovis tempore editi, si certo vivant, baptizentur absolute; si dubie, sub condicione. || 21. Care must be taken that all aborted fetuses, at whatever stage delivered, are baptized absolutely if they are certainly alive, and conditionally if this is doubtful.
r: 22. Monstra et ostenta semper baptizentur saltem sub condicione: *Si tu es homo, ego te baptízo, etc.* In dubio autem unusne an plures sint homines, unus absolute baptizetur, ceteri sub condicione: *Si non es baptizátus, ego te baptízo, etc.* || 22. Monstrous and abnormal births are always to be baptized at least conditionally: *If you are a human being, I baptize you,* etc. But in doubt whether there is one human being or more, one is to be baptized absolutely and the others conditionally: *If you are not baptized, I baptize you,* etc.
r: 23. Infantes expositi et inventi nisi, re diligenter investigata, de eorum Baptismo constet, sub condicione baptizentur. || 23. Abandoned and foundling infants are to be baptized conditionally, unless after careful investigation their Baptism is established.
r: 24. Infans infidelium, etiam invitis parentibus, licite baptizatur, cum in eo versatur vitæ discrimine, ut prudenter prævideatur moriturus, antequam usum rationis attingat. || 24. The infant of unbelievers, even against the parents' will, is lawfully baptized when its life is in such danger that it is prudently foreseen that it will die before it attains the use of reason.
r: Extra mortis periculum, dummodo catholicæ ejus educationi cautum sit, licite baptizatur: || Outside danger of death, provided its Catholic upbringing is assured, it is lawfully baptized:
r: 1° Si parentes vel tutores, aut saltem unus eorum, consentiant; || 1° If the parents or guardians, or at least one of them, consent;
r: 2° Si parentes, idest pater, mater, avus, avia, vel tutores desint, aut jus in eum amiserint, vel illud exercere nullo pacto queant. || 2° If there are no parents, that is, father, mother, grandfather, grandmother, or guardians, or if they have lost their right over the child, or are in no way able to exercise it.
r: 25. Circa Baptismum infantium duorum hæreticorum aut schismaticorum, aut duorum catholicorum qui in apostasiam vel hæresim vel schisma prolapsi sint, generatim serventur normæ in superiore rubrica constitutæ. || 25. With regard to the Baptism of infants of two heretics or schismatics, or of two Catholics who have fallen into apostasy, heresy, or schism, the norms laid down in the preceding rubric are in general to be observed.
h: De ritibus et cæremoniis Baptismi || The Rites and Ceremonies of Baptism
r: 26. Baptismus sollemniter conferatur, salvo præscripto rubr. num. 28. || 26. Baptism is to be conferred solemnly, without prejudice to the prescription of rubric no. 28.
r: Loci Ordinarius potest, gravi et rationabili de causa, indulgere ut cæremoniæ præscriptæ pro Baptismo infantium adhibeantur in Baptismo adultorum. || The local Ordinary may, for a grave and reasonable cause, permit the ceremonies prescribed for the Baptism of infants to be used in the Baptism of adults.
r: 27. Proles ritu parentum baptizari debet. || 27. A child must be baptized in the rite of its parents.
r: Si alter parentum pertineat ad ritum latinum, alter ad orientalem, proles ritu patris baptizetur, nisi aliud jure speciali cautum sit. || If one parent belongs to the Latin rite and the other to an Eastern rite, the child is to be baptized in the father's rite, unless special law provides otherwise.
r: Si unus tantum sit catholicus, proles hujus ritu baptizanda est. || If only one parent is Catholic, the child is to be baptized in that parent's rite.
r: 28. In mortis periculo Baptismum privatim conferre licet; et, si conferatur a ministro qui nec Sacerdos sit nec Diaconus, ea tantum ponantur, quæ sunt ad Baptismi validitatem necessaria; si a Sacerdote vel Diacono, serventur quoque, si tempus adsit, cæremoniæ quæ Baptismum sequuntur. || 28. In danger of death Baptism may be conferred privately; and if it is conferred by a minister who is neither a priest nor a deacon, only what is necessary for the validity of Baptism is to be done; if by a priest or deacon, the ceremonies that follow Baptism are also to be observed, if there is time.
r: Extra mortis periculum Baptismum privatum loci Ordinarius permittere nequit, nisi agatur de hæreticis qui in adulta ætate sub condicione baptizentur. || Outside danger of death the local Ordinary cannot permit private Baptism, unless it is a question of heretics who are being baptized conditionally as adults.
r: Cæremoniæ autem quæ in Baptismi collatione prætermissæ quavis ratione fuerint, quamprimum in ecclesia suppleantur, nisi agatur de hæreticis qui in adulta ætate sub condicione privatim baptizati fuerint ex Ordinarii loci licentia, ut supra. || The ceremonies, however, that were omitted for any reason in the conferral of Baptism are to be supplied in church as soon as possible, unless it is a question of heretics who, as adults, were baptized conditionally and privately with the permission of the local Ordinary, as above.
r: 29. Cum Baptismus sub condicione iteratur, cæremoniæ, si quidem in priore Baptismo omissæ fuerunt, suppleantur, salvo præscripto rubr. num. 28; sin autem in priore Baptismo adhibitæ sunt, repeti in altero aut omitti possunt. || 29. When Baptism is repeated conditionally, the ceremonies, if they were omitted in the earlier Baptism, are to be supplied, without prejudice to the prescription of rubric no. 28; but if they were used in the earlier Baptism, they may be either repeated or omitted in the second.
r: 30. Curent Parochi ut ei qui baptizatur, christianum imponatur nomen; quod si id consequi non poterunt, nomini a parentibus imposito addant nomen alicujus Sancti et in libro baptizatorum utrumque nomen perscribant. || 30. Pastors should see to it that a Christian name is given to the one being baptized; if they cannot obtain this, they are to add the name of some Saint to the name given by the parents, and record both names in the baptismal register.
h: De patrinis || Sponsors
r: 31. Ex vetustissimo Ecclesiæ more nemo sollemniter baptizetur, nisi suum habeat, quatenus fieri possit, patrinum. || 31. According to the most ancient custom of the Church, no one is to be solemnly baptized without having, as far as possible, his own sponsor.
r: Etiam in Baptismo privato patrinus, si facile haberi queat, adhibeatur; si non interfuerit, adhibeatur in supplendis Baptismi cæremoniis, sed hoc in casu nullam contrahit spiritualem cognationem. || In private Baptism also a sponsor is to be employed, if one can easily be had; if none was present, one is to be employed when the ceremonies of Baptism are supplied, but in this case he contracts no spiritual relationship.
r: 32. Cum Baptismus iteratur sub condicione, idem patrinus, quatenus fieri possit, adhibeatur, qui in priore Baptismo forte adfuit; extra hunc casum in Baptismo condicionato patrinus non est necessarius. || 32. When Baptism is repeated conditionally, the same sponsor who perhaps assisted at the earlier Baptism is to be employed as far as possible; apart from this case a sponsor is not necessary in conditional Baptism.
r: Iterato Baptismo sub condicione, neque patrinus qui priori Baptismo adfuit, neque qui posteriori, cognationem spiritualem contrahit, nisi idem patrinus in utroque Baptismo adhibitus fuerit. || When Baptism has been repeated conditionally, neither the sponsor who assisted at the first Baptism nor the one at the second contracts a spiritual relationship, unless the same sponsor was employed at both Baptisms.
r: 33. Patrinus unus tantum, licet diversi sexus a baptizando, vel ad summum unus et una adhibeantur. || 33. Only one sponsor is to be employed, even if of a different sex from the one being baptized, or at most one man and one woman.
r: 34. Ut quis sit patrinus, oportet: || 34. For anyone to be a sponsor, it is required:
r: 1° Sit baptizatus, rationis usum assecutus et intentionem habeat id munus gerendi; || 1° That he be baptized, have attained the use of reason, and have the intention of undertaking this office;
r: 2° Ad nullam pertineat hæreticam aut schismaticam sectam, nec sententia condemnatoria vel declaratoria sit excommunicatus aut infamis infamia juris aut exclusus ab actibus legitimis, nec sit clericus depositus vel degradatus; || 2° That he belong to no heretical or schismatic sect, and not be excommunicated by a condemnatory or declaratory sentence, nor be infamous with infamy of law, nor excluded from legitimate acts, nor be a deposed or degraded cleric;
r: 3° Nec sit pater vel mater vel conjux baptizandi; || 3° That he be neither the father nor the mother nor the spouse of the one to be baptized;
r: 4° Ab ipso baptizando ejusve parentibus vel tutoribus aut, his deficientibus, a ministro sit designatus; || 4° That he be designated by the one to be baptized, or by his parents or guardians, or, failing these, by the minister;
r: 5° Baptizandum in actu Baptismi per se vel per procuratorem physice teneat aut tangat vel statim levet seu suscipiat de sacro fonte aut de manibus baptizantis. || 5° That in the act of Baptism he, in person or by proxy, physically hold or touch the one being baptized, or immediately lift or receive him from the sacred font or from the hands of the one baptizing.
r: 35. Ut autem quis licite patrinus admittatur, oportet: || 35. For anyone to be lawfully admitted as sponsor, it is required:
r: 1° Decimum quartum suæ ætatis annum attigerit, nisi aliud justa de causa ministro videatur; || 1° That he have reached his fourteenth year of age, unless for a just cause the minister judges otherwise;
r: 2° Non sit propter notorium delictum excommunicatus vel exclusus ab actibus legitimis vel infamis infamia juris, quin tamen sententia intercesserit, nec sit interdictus aut alias publice criminosus vel infamis infamia facti; || 2° That he not be excommunicated on account of a notorious offense, or excluded from legitimate acts, or infamous with infamy of law, even though no sentence has been passed; nor be interdicted, or otherwise a public criminal, or infamous with infamy of fact;
r: 3° Fidei rudimenta noverit; || 3° That he know the rudiments of the faith;
r: 4° In nulla religione sit novicius vel professus, nisi necessitas urgeat et expressa habeatur venia Superioris saltem localis; || 4° That he be neither a novice nor a professed member in any religious institute, unless necessity presses and the express permission of at least the local Superior is obtained;
r: 5° In sacris ordinibus non sit constitutus, nisi accedat expressa Ordinarii proprii licentia. || 5° That he not be in sacred orders, unless the express permission of his own Ordinary is given.
r: 36. In dubio utrum quis valide vel licite admitti possit, necne, ad patrini munus, parochus, si tempus suppetat, consulat Ordinarium. || 36. In doubt whether or not someone can validly or lawfully be admitted to the office of sponsor, the pastor, if time permits, should consult the Ordinary.
r: 37. Ex Baptismo spiritualem cognationem contrahunt tantum cum baptizato baptizans et patrinus. || 37. From Baptism only the one baptizing and the sponsor contract spiritual relationship, and that with the person baptized.
r: 38. Patrinorum est, ex suscepto munere, spiritualem filium perpetuo sibi commendatum habere, atque in iis quæ ad christianæ vitæ institutionem spectant, curare diligenter ut ille talem in tota vita se præbeat, qualem futurum esse sollemni cæremonia spoponderunt. || 38. It belongs to sponsors, by reason of the office they have undertaken, to regard their spiritual child as committed to their care for ever, and, in all that concerns training in the Christian life, to see diligently that he shows himself throughout his whole life such as they promised in the solemn ceremony that he would be.
h: De tempore et loco Baptismi conferendi || The Time and Place for Conferring Baptism
r: 39. Infantes quamprimum baptizentur; et Parochi ac contionatores frequenter fideles de hac gravi eorum obligatione commoneant. || 39. Infants are to be baptized as soon as possible; and pastors and preachers should frequently remind the faithful of this grave obligation of theirs.
r: 40. Baptismus privatus, urgente necessitate, quovis tempore et loco administrandus est. || 40. Private Baptism, when necessity presses, is to be administered at any time and in any place.
r: 41. Etiam sollemnis Baptismus qualibet die administrari potest; decet tamen adultorum Baptismum, secundum antiquissimum Ecclesiæ ritum, conferri, si fieri commode queat, in pervigilio Paschatis et Pentecostes, præcipue in metropolitanis aut cathedralibus ecclesiis. || 41. Solemn Baptism also may be administered on any day; it is fitting, however, according to the most ancient rite of the Church, that the Baptism of adults be conferred, if this can conveniently be done, on the vigil of Easter and of Pentecost, especially in metropolitan or cathedral churches.
r: 42. Proprius Baptismi sollemnis administrandi locus est Baptisterium in ecclesia vel oratorio publico. || 42. The proper place for administering solemn Baptism is the baptistery in a church or public oratory.
r: 43. Quælibet parœcialis ecclesia, revocato ac reprobato quovis contrario statuto vel privilegio vel consuetudine, baptismalem habeat fontem, salvo legitimo jure cumulativo aliis ecclesiis jam quæsito. || 43. Every parish church, any contrary statute, privilege, or custom being revoked and reprobated, is to have a baptismal font, without prejudice to a lawful cumulative right already acquired by other churches.
r: Loci Ordinarius potest pro fidelium commoditate permittere vel jubere ut fons baptismalis ponatur etiam in alia ecclesia vel publico oratorio intra parœciæ fines. || The local Ordinary may, for the convenience of the faithful, permit or order that a baptismal font be placed also in another church or public oratory within the boundaries of the parish.
r: 44. Si ad ecclesiam parœcialem, aut ad aliam quæ jure fontis gaudeat, baptizandus, propter locorum distantiam aliave adjuncta, sine gravi incommodo aut periculo, accedere aut transferri nequeat, Baptismus sollemnis a Parocho conferri potest et debet in proxima ecclesia aut oratorio publico intra parœciæ fines, licet hæc baptismali fonte careant. || 44. If, because of distance or other circumstances, the one to be baptized cannot without grave inconvenience or danger come or be brought to the parish church, or to another that enjoys the right of a font, solemn Baptism can and must be conferred by the pastor in the nearest church or public oratory within the boundaries of the parish, even though it lacks a baptismal font.
r: 45. In domibus autem privatis Baptismus sollemnis administrari non debet, nisi hisce in adjunctis: || 45. In private houses, however, solemn Baptism is not to be administered except in these circumstances:
r: 1° Si baptizandi sint filii aut nepotes eorum qui supremum actu tenent populorum principatum vel jus habent succedendi in thronum, quoties isti id rite poposcerint; || 1° If those to be baptized are the children or grandchildren of persons who actually hold the supreme rule over peoples or have the right of succession to the throne, whenever these have duly requested it;
r: 2° Si loci Ordinarius, pro suo prudenti arbitrio et conscientia, justa ac rationabili de causa, in casu aliquo extraordinario id concedendum censuerit. || 2° If the local Ordinary, in his prudent judgment and conscience, for a just and reasonable cause, has judged that it should be granted in some extraordinary case.
r: In memoratis casibus Baptismus conferendus est in sacello domus aut saltem in alio decenti loco, et aqua baptismali de more benedicta. || In the cases mentioned, Baptism is to be conferred in the chapel of the house, or at least in some other fitting place, and with baptismal water blessed in the customary way.
r: 46. Baptisterium sit decenti loco et forma, materiaque solida, et quæ aquam bene contineat, decenter ornatum, et cancellis circumseptum, sera et clave munitum, atque ita obseratum, ut pulvis, vel aliæ sordes intro non penetrent, in eoque, ubi commode fieri potest, depingatur seu collocetur imago sancti Joannis Christum baptizantis. || 46. The baptistery should be in a fitting place and of fitting form, made of solid material that holds water well, becomingly adorned, enclosed by a railing, secured with lock and key, and so closed that dust or other dirt cannot get in; and in it, where this can conveniently be done, there should be painted or placed an image of Saint John baptizing Christ.
h: De sacris Oleis et aliis requisitis || The Holy Oils and Other Requisites
r: 47. Sacrum Chrisma, et sanctum Oleum, quod et Catechumenorum dicitur, quorum usus est in Baptismo, debent esse ab Episcopo benedicta Feria V in Cena Domini proxime superiore; neque adhibeantur vetera, nisi necessitas urgeat. || 47. The sacred Chrism and the holy Oil, also called the Oil of Catechumens, which are used in Baptism, must have been blessed by the bishop on the most recent Holy Thursday; the old oils are not to be used unless necessity presses.
r: 48. Curet Parochus, ut ea suo tempore quamprimum habeat a suo Ordinario, et tunc vetera in ecclesia comburat. || 48. The pastor should take care to obtain them from his Ordinary at the proper time, as soon as possible, and then burn the old oils in the church.
r: 49. Mox deficienti Oleo benedicto aliud Oleum de olivis non benedictum adjiciatur, etiam iterato, minore tamen copia. || 49. When the blessed oil is about to run out, other olive oil that is not blessed may be added to it, even repeatedly, but in a smaller quantity.
r: 50. Chrisma, et Oleum sacrum sit in suis vasculis argenteis, aut saltem stanneis, bene obturatis: quæ vascula sint inter se distincta et propriam unumquodque inscriptionem habeat, majusculis litteris incisam, ne quis error committatur. || 50. The Chrism and the holy Oil are to be kept in their own vessels of silver, or at least of pewter, well stoppered; these vessels are to be distinct from one another, and each is to bear its own inscription engraved in capital letters, so that no mistake may be made.
r: 51. Ad usum vero cotidianum minora habeantur vascula ex argento, si fieri potest, aut stanno, sive separata, sive etiam conjuncta: apte tamen distincta, et bene cooperta, et cum suis inscriptionibus, ut supra, ne Parochus aberret, et unum pro altero sumat, quod cavere debet diligenter. || 51. For daily use, however, smaller vessels of silver, if possible, or of pewter are to be kept, either separate or joined together, but suitably distinguished, well covered, and bearing their inscriptions as above, lest the pastor make a mistake and take one for the other, which he must carefully avoid.
r: 52. In ea igitur ex majoribus vasculis Chrismatis et Olei, quod sufficiat, infundatur, atque ut effusionis periculum caveatur, commodum erit, in his vasculis bombacium, seu quid simile habere, Oleo sacro, et Chrismate separatim perfusum, in quo pollex, cum opus est, ad inungendum immittatur. || 52. Into these, then, a sufficient quantity of Chrism and Oil is poured from the larger vessels; and to guard against the danger of spilling, it will be convenient to keep in these vessels some cotton or the like, soaked separately with the holy Oil and with the Chrism, into which the thumb may be dipped for anointing when needed.
r: 53. Hæc vascula ita parata in ecclesia, loco proprio, honesto, ac mundo, sub clave, ac tuta custodia decenter asserventur, ne ab aliquo, nisi a Sacerdote, temere tangantur, aut eis sacrilege quisquam abuti possit; nec ea domi Parochus retineat, nisi propter necessitatem aliamve rationabilem causam, accedente Ordinarii licentia. || 53. These vessels, so prepared, are to be reverently kept in the church in a proper, worthy, and clean place, under lock and in safe custody, so that they may not be rashly touched by anyone but a priest, nor anyone sacrilegiously misuse them; nor is the pastor to keep them at home, except because of necessity or some other reasonable cause, with the permission of the Ordinary.
r: 54. Parochus, quantum fieri potest, curet, ne per laicos, sed per se, vel alium Sacerdotem, vel saltem per alium Ecclesiæ ministrum hæc Olea deferantur: caveat item, ne de iis quidquam ulli umquam tribuat cujusvis rei prætextu. || 54. The pastor should see to it, as far as possible, that these Oils are carried not by lay people but by himself, or by another priest, or at least by another minister of the Church; he must likewise take care never to give any of them to anyone on any pretext whatever.
r: 55. Sal, quod in os baptizandi immittendum est, sit benedictum sua peculiari benedictione, quæ infra præscribitur; neque utatur sale exorcizato ad benedicendam aquam; sitque prius bene confractum et attritum, siccum ac mundum. Sal ita benedictum nemini tradatur, neque etiam iis, qui benedicendum attulerint, reddatur, sed ad alios baptizandos servetur, aut in sacrarium abjiciatur. || 55. The salt to be put into the mouth of the one to be baptized is to be blessed with its own special blessing, prescribed below; he is not to use salt exorcized for the blessing of water; and the salt should first be well crushed and ground, dry and clean. Salt so blessed is to be given to no one, nor even returned to those who brought it to be blessed, but is to be kept for others to be baptized, or thrown into the sacrarium.
r: 56. Cum igitur Baptismi Sacramentum jam administrandum est, hæc in promptu esse debent: || 56. When, therefore, the Sacrament of Baptism is about to be administered, the following must be at hand:
r: 57. Vascula sacri Olei Catechumenorum, et Chrismatis. || 57. The vessels of the holy Oil of Catechumens and of the Chrism.
r: 58. Vasculum cum sale benedicendo, vel jam, ut dictum est, benedicto. || 58. A small vessel with salt to be blessed, or already blessed, as has been said.
r: 59. Vasculum, seu cochleare ex argento, vel alio metallo nitidum, ad aquam Baptismi fundendam super caput baptizandi, quod nulli præterea alii usui deserviat. || 59. A small vessel or ladle of silver or other bright metal, for pouring the water of Baptism on the head of the one being baptized, which is to serve no other use.
r: 60. Pelvis, seu bacile ad excipiendam aquam ex capite defluentem, nisi statim in sacrarium defluat. || 60. A basin or bowl to catch the water flowing from the head, unless it flows directly into the sacrarium.
r: 61. Gossipium, alio nomine bombacium, seu quid simile, ad abstergenda loca sacris Oleis inuncta. || 61. Cotton wool, otherwise called bombacium, or something similar, to wipe the places anointed with the holy Oils.
r: 62. Stolæ duæ, ubi commode haberi possunt, una violacea, et altera alba, ut infra notatur, mutanda; sin minus, una saltem adhibeatur. || 62. Two stoles, where they can conveniently be had, one violet and the other white, to be changed as noted below; otherwise, at least one is to be used.
r: 63. Medulla panis, qua inuncti Sacerdotis digiti, cum manus lavat, abstergantur; et vas pro manuum lotione post Baptismum, quod huic tantum usui deservire decet. || 63. Crumb of bread, with which the priest's anointed fingers may be cleansed when he washes his hands; and a vessel for washing the hands after Baptism, which should properly serve for this use alone.
r: 64. Alba vestis in modum pallioli, seu linteolum candidum, infantis capiti imponendum. || 64. A white garment in the form of a small mantle, or a white linen cloth, to be placed on the infant's head.
r: 65. Cereus, seu candela cerea, baptizato ardens tradenda. || 65. A taper or wax candle, to be given lighted to the one baptized.
r: 66. Hic denique Ritualis liber sit paratus; et item liber Baptismalis, in quo baptizati describuntur. || 66. Finally, this Ritual book is to be ready, and likewise the baptismal register, in which the baptized are recorded.
r: 67. Omnibus igitur opportune præparatis, Sacerdos ad tanti Sacramenti administrationem, lotis manibus, superpelliceo et stola violacea indutus, accedat: Clericum unum, seu plures, si potest, secum adhibeat, superpelliceo pariter indutos, qui sibi ministrent. || 67. Everything having thus been suitably prepared, the priest, having washed his hands and put on surplice and violet stole, approaches to administer so great a Sacrament. If he can, he should take with him one or more clerics, likewise vested in surplice, to assist him.
r: 68. Ita paratus accedat ad limen ecclesiæ, ubi foris exspectant qui infantem detulerunt. || 68. So prepared, he goes to the threshold of the church, where those who have brought the infant wait outside.
r: 69. Interroget (nisi de his bene sibi constet), an sit suæ parœciæ, masculus, an femina, an sit domi baptizatus, et a quo, et quam rite, et qui sint compatres, qui infantem teneant, pro eoque respondeant: quos pie ac decenter assistere, ac, prout opus erit, pro baptizando ad interrogationes respondere admoneat. || 69. He asks (unless he is already well informed of these things) whether the infant belongs to his parish, whether it is a boy or a girl, whether it has been baptized at home, and by whom, and how correctly, and who are the sponsors who are to hold the infant and answer for it; and he admonishes them to assist devoutly and becomingly and, as need arises, to answer the questions on behalf of the one to be baptized.
r: 70. Et quoniam iis qui baptizantur, tamquam Dei filiis in Christo regenerandis, et in ejus militiam adscribendis, nomen imponitur, curet, ne obscena, fabulosa, aut ridicula, vel inanium deorum, vel impiorum ethnicorum hominum nomina imponantur, sed potius, quatenus fieri potest, Sanctorum, quorum exemplis fideles ad pie vivendum excitentur, et patrociniis protegantur. || 70. And since a name is given to those who are baptized, as to children of God who are to be reborn in Christ and enrolled in His service, he should take care that no obscene, fabulous, or ridiculous names be given, nor names of false gods or of impious pagans, but rather, as far as possible, names of Saints, by whose example the faithful may be stirred to live devoutly and by whose patronage they may be protected.
r: 71. His igitur expeditis, et accepto nomine baptizandi, positi, si infans fuerit, super brachium dextrum illius, qui eum defert, Parochus ad Baptismum procedat, in hunc modum nominatim interrogans. || 71. These things done, and the name of the one to be baptized having been received, the infant (if it is an infant) being placed on the right arm of the person carrying it, the pastor proceeds to the Baptism, questioning by name in this manner.
