# parish-books | De libris habendis apud Parochos | The Books to Be Kept by Pastors | Other | Tit. XII, cap. 1
r: 1. Habeat Parochus libros parœciales, idest librum baptizatorum, confirmatorum, matrimoniorum, defunctorum; etiam librum de statu animarum accurate conficere pro viribus curet; et omnes hos libros, secundum usum ab Ecclesia probatum vel a proprio Ordinario præscriptum, conscribat ac diligenter asservet. || 1. The pastor is to have the parish books, that is, the book of the baptized, of the confirmed, of marriages, and of the dead; he is also to take care, as far as he is able, to draw up accurately a book of the state of souls; and all these books he is to write up and diligently preserve according to the practice approved by the Church or prescribed by his own Ordinary.
r: 2. In libro baptizatorum adnotetur quoque si baptizatus confirmationem receperit, matrimonium contraxerit, aut sacrum subdiaconatus ordinem susceperit, vel professionem sollemnem emiserit, eæque adnotationes in documenta accepti baptismatis semper referantur. || 2. In the book of the baptized it is also to be noted whether the baptized person has received Confirmation, contracted marriage, received the sacred order of subdiaconate, or made solemn profession; and these notes are always to be carried over into certificates of baptism received.
r: 3. Matrimonium conscientiæ non est adnotandum in consueto matrimoniorum ac baptizatorum libro, sed in peculiari libro servando in secreto Curiæ archivo. || 3. A marriage of conscience is not to be entered in the ordinary book of marriages and of the baptized, but in a special book to be kept in the secret archive of the Curia.
r: 4. Advertat autem Parochus, ut in libris tam baptizatorum et confirmatorum, quam matrimoniorum et defunctorum exprimat semper non solum nomen personarum, quæ ibi nominantur, sed etiam cognomen seu familiam. || 4. The pastor is to see to it that in the books of the baptized and the confirmed, as well as in those of marriages and of the dead, he always records not only the Christian name of the persons named there but also their surname or family.

# register-baptism | Forma describendi baptizatos in primo libro | Form for Recording the Baptized, in the First Book | Other | Tit. XII, cap. 2
t: Anno Domini ..., die ... mensis ..., ego N. Parochus hujus ecclesiæ S. N., civitatis vel loci N., in ecclesia S. N. baptizavi infantem natum vel natam die ... ex N. et N. conjugibus hujus parœciæ vel parœciæ S. N., civitatis vel loci N.; cui impositum est nomen N. Patrini fuerunt N., filius N., ex parœcia seu loco N., et N., conjux N., filia N., ex parœcia seu loco N. || In the year of the Lord ..., on the ... day of the month of ..., I, N., pastor of this church of St. N., in the city or place of N., baptized in the church of St. N. an infant, male or female, born on the ... day, of N. and N., spouses, of this parish or of the parish of St. N., in the city or place of N.; to whom was given the name N. The sponsors were N., son of N., of the parish or place of N., and N., wife of N., daughter of N., of the parish or place of N.
t: In quorum fidem testimonium hoc mea manu subscripsi. || In witness whereof I have signed this testimony with my own hand.
t: (Locus Sigilli) N. N. Parochus. || (Place of the Seal) N. N., Pastor.
r: Ubi vero de illegitimis filiis agatur, matris nomen est inserendum, si publice ejus maternitas constet, vel ipsa sponte sua scripto vel coram duobus testibus id petat; item nomen patris, dummodo ipse sponte sua a Parocho vel scripto vel coram duobus testibus id requirat, vel ex publico authentico documento sit notus; in ceteris casibus inscribatur natus tamquam filius patris ignoti vel ignotorum parentum. || Where illegitimate children are concerned, the mother's name is to be entered if her motherhood is publicly established, or if she herself of her own accord asks for it in writing or before two witnesses; likewise the father's name, provided that he himself of his own accord requests it of the pastor, either in writing or before two witnesses, or that he is known from a public authentic document. In all other cases the child is to be entered as the child of an unknown father or of unknown parents.
r: Si expositus sit infans, exprimatur quo die, ubi, et a quo repertus, et quot dierum verisimiliter sit. || If the infant is a foundling, it is to be stated on what day, where, and by whom it was found, and how many days old it probably is.
r: Si infans domi ob imminens mortis periculum baptizatus sit, tunc ita scribatur: || If the infant was baptized at home because of imminent danger of death, it is to be written thus:
t: Anno ... die ... mensis ... natus est N., filius N. et N. conjugum, etc. ut supra: quem ob imminens mortis periculum, in domo rite baptizavit N. obstetrix probata, vel N. filius N., ut mihi rettulit N. || In the year ..., on the ... day of the month of ..., N. was born, son of N. and N., spouses, etc., as above; whom, because of imminent danger of death, N., an approved midwife, or N., son of N., duly baptized at home, as N. reported to me.
r: Si supervixerit infans, et ei adhibitæ sint in ecclesia sacræ cæremoniæ, ita addatur: || If the infant has survived and the sacred ceremonies have been supplied for it in church, this is to be added:
t: Die ... ejusdem mensis ad ecclesiam portatus est infans prædictus, ipsique ego Parochus sacras cæremonias et preces adhibui, et N. nomen imposui. || On the ... day of the same month the aforesaid infant was brought to the church, and I, the pastor, supplied the sacred ceremonies and prayers for it, and gave it the name N.
r: Si alius, loco Parochi, infantem baptizaverit, id exprimatur. || If someone else baptized the infant in place of the pastor, this is to be stated.
r: Si infans fuerit baptizatus sub condicione, id pariter exprimatur. || If the infant was baptized conditionally, this likewise is to be stated.

# register-confirmation | Forma describendi confirmatos in secundo libro | Form for Recording the Confirmed, in the Second Book | Other | Tit. XII, cap. 3
t: Anno Domini ..., die ... mensis ... (in hebdomada Pentecostes), N., filius N. et N. conjugum, vel N., filia N. et N. conjugum (et, si fuerit nupta, addatur uxor N.), Sacramentum Confirmationis accepit a Reverendissimo D. N., Episcopo N., in ecclesia S. N., civitatis vel loci N. Patrinus fuit N., filius N., parœciæ S. N., civitatis vel loci N. || In the year of the Lord ..., on the ... day of the month of ... (in the week of Pentecost), N., son of N. and N., spouses, or N., daughter of N. and N., spouses (and if she is married, add: wife of N.), received the Sacrament of Confirmation from the Most Reverend N., Bishop of N., in the church of St. N., in the city or place of N. The sponsor was N., son of N., of the parish of St. N., in the city or place of N.
t: In quorum fidem testimonium hoc mea manu subscripsi. || In witness whereof I have signed this testimony with my own hand.
t: (Locus Sigilli) N. N. Parochus. || (Place of the Seal) N. N., Pastor.
r: Si non constet, an confirmatus ex legitimo matrimonio genitus sit, vel ejus parentes ignorentur, servetur quod in libro baptizatorum præscriptum est. || If it is not established whether the confirmed person was born of a lawful marriage, or if the parents are unknown, what is prescribed for the book of the baptized is to be observed.

# register-marriage | Forma describendi conjugatos in tertio libro | Form for Recording the Married, in the Third Book | Other | Tit. XII, cap. 4
t: Anno Domini ..., die ... mensis ..., publicationibus præmissis tribus continuis diebus festivis, quarum prima die ..., secunda die ..., tertia die ..., inter Missarum sollemnia habita est, nulloque canonico impedimento detecto, ego N., Parochus hujus ecclesiæ S. N., civitatis vel loci N., N., filium N., annorum ..., parœciæ S. N., et N., filiam N. seu relictam quondam N. (si fuerit vidua), annorum ..., parœciæ S. N., in ecclesia S. N., interrogavi, eorumque mutuo consensu habito sollemniter per verba de præsenti in Matrimonium conjunxi, præsentibus testibus notis N., filio N., qui habitat in parœcia S. N., et N., filio N., etc., et N., filio N., etc. Postea eis ex ritu S. Matris Ecclesiæ (si tamen nuptias benedixerit) in Missæ celebratione benedixi. || In the year of the Lord ..., on the ... day of the month of ..., the banns having first been published on three consecutive feast days, of which the first was held on the ... day, the second on the ... day, and the third on the ... day, during the solemnities of Mass, and no canonical impediment having been discovered, I, N., pastor of this church of St. N., in the city or place of N., questioned N., son of N., aged ..., of the parish of St. N., and N., daughter of N., or widow of the late N. (if she is a widow), aged ..., of the parish of St. N., in the church of St. N.; and, having obtained their mutual consent, I solemnly joined them in Matrimony by words of present consent, in the presence of the known witnesses N., son of N., who lives in the parish of St. N., and N., son of N., etc., and N., son of N., etc. Afterwards I blessed them according to the rite of Holy Mother Church (if, however, he gave the nuptial blessing) in the celebration of Mass.
t: In quorum fidem testimonium hoc mea manu subscripsi. || In witness whereof I have signed this testimony with my own hand.
t: (Locus Sigilli) N. N. Parochus. || (Place of the Seal) N. N., Pastor.
r: Si unus ex iis, qui Matrimonium contrahere velint, sit alterius parœciæ, antequam admittatur, Parochus, in cujus ecclesia Matrimonium celebrari debet, publicationum in ejus parœcia rite factarum fidem scriptam habeat, quæ asservetur, et res tota exprimatur in ipsomet libro matrimoniorum, hac ratione: || If one of those who wish to contract Matrimony belongs to another parish, then before he or she is admitted, the pastor in whose church the marriage is to be celebrated is to have written attestation of the banns duly published in that person's parish; this is to be preserved, and the whole matter is to be recorded in the book of marriages itself, in this manner:
t: Publicationes ejus Matrimonii factæ sunt etiam a R. D. N., Parocho ecclesiæ S. N., sub cujus cura dictus N. (vel dicta N.) habitat, ut ex ipsius Parochi scripto, servato apud me, apparet. Publicationum autem prima facta est die ..., secunda die ..., tertia die ..., inter Missarum sollemnia, nullumque impedimentum canonicum detectum est. || The banns of this marriage were also published by the Reverend N., pastor of the church of St. N., under whose care the said N. lives, as appears from the written statement of that pastor, kept in my possession. Of the banns, the first was published on the ... day, the second on the ... day, the third on the ... day, during the solemnities of Mass, and no canonical impediment was discovered.
r: Ubi vero Ordinarii concessu (quod scripto constare debet) publicationes aliquæ omittendæ interdum sint, ita notetur: || Where, by permission of the Ordinary (which must be established in writing), some of the banns are at times to be omitted, it is to be noted thus:
t: Anno Domini ..., die ... mensis ..., publicationum una die festo rite facta, reliquis vero omissis, ex facultate scripto concessa a Reverendissimo D. Episcopo N. (vel ejus Vicario N.) sub die ... datis, etc., infrascripti tenoris, quam penes me servo cum aliis hujusmodi facultatibus, nulloque canonico impedimento allato, ego, etc., ut supra. || In the year of the Lord ..., on the ... day of the month of ..., one of the banns having been duly published on a feast day and the others omitted by virtue of a faculty granted in writing by the Most Reverend N., Bishop of N. (or his Vicar N.), dated the ... day, etc., of the tenor written below, which I keep in my possession together with other faculties of this kind, and no canonical impediment having been alleged, I, etc., as above.
r: Si autem publicationes omnes omittendæ sint, ita scribatur: || If all the banns are to be omitted, it is to be written thus:
t: Anno Domini ..., die ... mensis ..., publicationibus omnibus omissis, ex facultate, etc., ut supra. || In the year of the Lord ..., on the ... day of the month of ..., all the banns having been omitted by virtue of a faculty, etc., as above.
r: Ceterum si alteri Presbytero ab Ordinario vel a Parocho ipso facultas facta sit conjungendi aliquos, id in libro proprii Parochi sic adnotetur: || Moreover, if faculty to join certain persons in marriage has been given to another priest by the Ordinary or by the pastor himself, it is to be noted in the book of the proper pastor thus:
t: N. Presbyter vel Capellanus ecclesiæ S. N., de licentia Reverendissimi D. Episcopi N. (seu ejus Vicarii N.), aut mea, quæ penes me exstat, N., filium N., et N., filiam N., etc., in Matrimonium conjunxit, etc., ut supra. Et ego N., Parochus hujus ecclesiæ S. N., testor, rem ita se habere, et mea manu subscripsi. || N., priest or chaplain of the church of St. N., with the permission of the Most Reverend N., Bishop of N. (or of his Vicar N.), or with my own, which is in my keeping, joined in Matrimony N., son of N., and N., daughter of N., etc., as above. And I, N., pastor of this church of St. N., testify that this is so, and have signed with my own hand.
r: Quod si ex publicationibus compertum sit, conjuges aliquo consanguinitatis aut affinitatis gradu conjunctos esse, et nihilominus ad contrahendum fuerit cum ipsis Apostolica auctoritate dispensatum, annotetur gradus consanguinitatis vel affinitatis dispensatæ, et compendium decreti super ea relati cum die et anno, ac Notarii de illo rogati nomine; hoc modo: || But if it has been discovered through the banns that the spouses are related in some degree of consanguinity or affinity, and they have nevertheless been dispensed by Apostolic authority to contract marriage, the degree of consanguinity or affinity dispensed is to be noted, together with a summary of the decree issued concerning it, with its day and year, and the name of the notary who drew it up, in this manner:
t: Anno Domini ..., die ... mensis ..., præmissis publicationibus, ac comperto impedimento secundi (vel tertii) gradus consanguinitatis (vel affinitatis, seu alio quovis impedimento), inter N. et N., etc., obtentoque per eos Apostolicæ Sedis mandato de dispensando, et cum eis per Reverendissimum D. Episcopum N. auctoritate dispensato sub die ... mensis ..., anno ..., ut constat ex Actis Notarii Officii prædicti Episcopi, eos in Matrimonium conjunxi, etc., ut in prædicta formula. || In the year of the Lord ..., on the ... day of the month of ..., the banns having first been published, and an impediment of the second (or third) degree of consanguinity (or of affinity, or any other impediment) having been discovered between N. and N., etc., and they having obtained a mandate of the Apostolic See to grant a dispensation, and having been dispensed by authority of the Most Reverend N., Bishop of N., on the ... day of the month of ..., in the year ..., as is established from the Acts of the Notary of the Office of the said Bishop, I joined them in Matrimony, etc., as in the aforesaid formula.
r: Publicationes autem factæ in diversis parœciis, sponsi videlicet et sponsæ, ab utroque Parocho in libro notari debent, etiamsi Matrimonium non sequatur. Quo in casu ita scribetur: || Banns published in different parishes, namely those of the bridegroom and of the bride, must be noted in the book by each pastor, even if the marriage does not follow. In that case it is to be written thus:
t: Anno Domini ..., tribus continuis diebus festis, nempe diebus ... mensis ..., ego N., Parochus ecclesiæ S. N., inter Missarum sollemnia publicationes habui Matrimonii contrahendi inter N., filium N., parœciæ S. N., et N., filiam N., parœciæ S. N.; nullumque canonicum impedimentum ab aliquo allatum est. In quorum fidem, etc. || In the year of the Lord ..., on three consecutive feast days, namely on the ... days of the month of ..., I, N., pastor of the church of St. N., published during the solemnities of Mass the banns of the marriage to be contracted between N., son of N., of the parish of St. N., and N., daughter of N., of the parish of St. N.; and no canonical impediment was alleged by anyone. In witness whereof, etc.

# register-deaths | Forma describendi defunctos in quarto libro | Form for Recording the Dead, in the Fourth Book | Other | Tit. XII, cap. 5
r: Describatur quis, et quando, et quæ Sacramenta ministraverit, quando quis mortuus fuerit, et ubi sepultus, quod hoc pacto fieri poterit: || It is to be recorded who administered which Sacraments, and when; when the person died, and where he was buried. This may be done in the following way:
t: Anno Domini ..., die ... mensis ..., N., filius vel filia N. et N. (vel conjux N.), annorum ..., in via vel platea N., in domo N., in communione S. Matris Ecclesiæ animam Deo reddidit; cujus corpus die ... sepultum est in cœmeterio ecclesiæ S. N.; mihi N., vel N. Confessario probato, confessus die ..., sanctissimoque Viatico refectus die ..., et sacri Olei unctione roboratus die ... per me, etc. || In the year of the Lord ..., on the ... day of the month of ..., N., son or daughter of N. and N. (or spouse of N.), aged ..., in the street or square of N., in the house of N., gave back his soul to God in the communion of Holy Mother Church; his body was buried on the ... day in the cemetery of the church of St. N. He made his confession to me, N., or to N., an approved confessor, on the ... day; was refreshed with the most holy Viaticum on the ... day; and was strengthened with the anointing of the holy Oil on the ... day, by me, etc.
t: In quorum fidem testimonium hoc mea manu subscripsi. || In witness whereof I have signed this testimony with my own hand.
t: (Locus Sigilli) N. N. Parochus. || (Place of the Seal) N. N., Pastor.

# register-status-animarum | Forma describendi statum animarum in quinto libro | Form for Recording the State of Souls, in the Fifth Book | Other | Tit. XII, cap. 6
r: Familia quæque distincte in libro notetur, intervallo relicto ab unaquaque ad alteram subsequentem, in quo singillatim scribantur nomen, cognomen, ætas singulorum, qui ex familia sunt, vel tamquam advenæ in ea vivunt. || Each family is to be entered separately in the book, with a space left between each one and the next, in which are written one by one the Christian name, surname, and age of each person who belongs to the family or lives in it as a stranger.
r: Qui vero ad sacram Communionem admissi sunt, hoc signum in margine e contra habeant: C. || Those who have been admitted to Holy Communion are to have this mark opposite them in the margin: C.
r: Qui Sacramento Confirmationis sunt muniti, hoc signum habeant: Chr. || Those who have been strengthened by the Sacrament of Confirmation are to have this mark: Chr.
r: Siqui ad alium locum habitandum accesserint, id adnotetur. || If any have gone to live in another place, this is to be noted.
r: Hac igitur ratione fiat, videlicet: || Let it be done, therefore, in this manner, namely:
t: Anno Domini ..., die ... mensis ..., in via vel platea N., in domo N., habitant: || In the year of the Lord ..., on the ... day of the month of ..., in the street or square of N., in the house of N., there live:
t: C. Chr. Paulus N., filius Petri, annorum ... || C. Chr. Paul N., son of Peter, aged ...
t: C. Chr. Apollonia ejus uxor, filia Jacobi N., annorum ... || C. Chr. Apollonia, his wife, daughter of James N., aged ...
t: C. Dominicus eorum filius, annorum ... || C. Dominic, their son, aged ...
t: C. Lucia eorum filia, annorum ... || C. Lucy, their daughter, aged ...
t: C. Chr. Antonius, filius N., famulus, annorum ... || C. Chr. Anthony, son of N., manservant, aged ...
t: C. Chr. Catharina N., filia N., ancilla, annorum ... || C. Chr. Catherine N., daughter of N., maidservant, aged ...
t: Martinus, filius N., annorum ... || Martin, son of N., aged ...

# altar-execrated-table | Ritus seu formula brevior consecrationis altaris immobilis quod amisit consecrationem ob separationem tabulæ a stipite | Shorter Rite for Consecrating a Fixed Altar That Has Lost Its Consecration by Separation of the Table from the Base | Churches & Sacred Things | Appendix, De consecratione altarium exsecratorum, 1
r: Ritus seu formula brevior consecrationis altaris immobilis quod amisit consecrationem ob separationem, etsi momentaneam, tabulæ seu mensæ a stipite; uti in casu de quo agit Codex Juris Canonici in can. 1200, § 1. || Shorter rite or formula for the consecration of a fixed altar that has lost its consecration through the separation, even momentary, of the table or top from the base, as in the case treated by the Code of Canon Law in canon 1200, § 1.
r: Instaurato altari immobili, et mensa, integrum Reliquiarum sepulcrum habente, cum stipite conjuncta, Pontifex, indutus rochetto et stola alba, vel Presbyter ab Ordinario delegatus, indutus superpelliceo et stola alba, accedit versus altare, et pollice dexteræ manus Chrismate intincto inungat, ad modum crucis, conjunctiones mensæ cum stipite in quatuor angulis, quasi illas conjungens, ad singulas cruces dicens: || When the fixed altar has been restored, and the table, with the sepulcher of the relics intact, has been joined to the base, the bishop, vested in rochet and white stole, or a priest delegated by the Ordinary, vested in surplice and white stole, approaches the altar and, with the thumb of his right hand dipped in Chrism, anoints in the form of a cross the joints of the table with the base at the four corners, as though joining them together, saying at each cross:
t: In nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus✠ Sancti. || In the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the ✠ Holy Spirit.
r: Dein recitet sequentes Orationes: || Then he recites the following prayers:
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Majestátem tuam, Dómine, humíliter implorámus, ut altáre hoc sacræ unctiónis libámine ad suscipiénda pópuli tui múnera inúnctum poténter bene✠dícere et sancti✠ficáre dignéris; ut quod nunc a nobis indígnis, sub tui nóminis invocatióne, in honórem beatíssimæ Vírginis Maríæ, et ómnium Sanctórum, atque in memóriam sancti tui N., sacrosáncti Chrísmatis unctióne delibútum est, pláceat tibi, atque altáre máneat perpétuum; ut quidquid deínceps super illud oblátum sacratúmve fúerit, dignum tibi fiat holocáustum; atque ómnium hic offeréntium sacrifícia a te pio Dómino benígne suscipiántur, et per ea víncula peccatórum nostrórum absolvántur; máculæ deleántur; véniæ impetréntur; et grátiæ acquirántur: quátenus una cum Sanctis, et Eléctis tuis vitam percípere mereámur ætérnam. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || We humbly implore Your majesty, O Lord, that You would mightily ✠ bless and ✠ sanctify this altar, anointed with the outpouring of holy unction to receive the gifts of Your people; so that what has now been anointed by us, unworthy as we are, with the holy Chrism, calling upon Your name, in honor of the most blessed Virgin Mary and of all the Saints, and in memory of Your Saint N., may be pleasing to You, and may remain an altar for ever; that whatever shall henceforth be offered or consecrated upon it may become a worthy holocaust to You; and that the sacrifices of all who offer here may be graciously received by You, the loving Lord, and through them the bonds of our sins be loosed, our stains be blotted out, pardon be obtained, and graces be acquired: so that together with Your Saints and Your Elect we may be worthy to receive eternal life. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Súpplices te deprecámur, omnípotens ætérne Deus, per unigénitum Fílium tuum, Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum, ut altáre hoc sanctis úsibus præparátum, cælésti bene✠dictióne sanctífices; et, sicut Melchísedech sacerdótis præcípui oblatiónem dignatióne mirábili suscepísti, ita impósita huic novo altári múnera, semper accépta ferre dignéris; ut pópulus, qui in hanc Ecclésiæ domum sanctam convéniet, per hæc libámina cælésti sanctificatióne salvátus, animárum quoque suárum salútem perpétuam consequátur. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟.
