# general-rules | De Benedictionibus: Regulæ Generales | On Blessings: General Rules | General | Tit. VIII, cap. 1
r: 1. Benedictiones impertire potest quilibet Presbyter, exceptis iis quæ Romano Pontifici aut Episcopis aliisve reserventur. Benedictio reservata quæ a Presbytero detur sine necessaria licentia, illicita est, sed valida, nisi in reservatione Sedes Apostolica aliud expresserit. Diaconi et Lectores illas tantum valide et licite benedictiones dare possunt, quæ ipsis expresse a jure permittuntur. || 1. Any priest may impart blessings, except those reserved to the Roman Pontiff, to bishops, or to others. A reserved blessing given by a priest without the necessary permission is unlawful but valid, unless the Apostolic See has stated otherwise in making the reservation. Deacons and lectors can validly and lawfully give only those blessings expressly permitted to them by law.
r: 2. Benedictiones sive constitutivæ sive invocativæ invalidæ sunt, si adhibita non fuerit formula ab Ecclesia præscripta. || 2. Blessings, whether constitutive or invocative, are invalid if the formula prescribed by the Church has not been used.
r: 3. Benedictiones, imprimis impertiendæ catholicis, dari quoque possunt catechumenis, immo, nisi obstet Ecclesiæ prohibitio, etiam acatholicis ad obtinendum fidei lumen vel, una cum illo, corporis sanitatem. || 3. Blessings, which are to be given first of all to Catholics, may also be given to catechumens, and even, unless a prohibition of the Church stands in the way, to non-Catholics, to obtain for them the light of faith or, together with it, bodily health.
r: 4. Res benedictæ constitutiva benedictione, reverenter tractentur neque ad usum profanum vel non proprium adhibeantur, etiamsi in dominio privatorum sint. || 4. Things blessed with a constitutive blessing are to be treated with reverence and are not to be put to profane or improper use, even if they are privately owned.
r: 5. Benedictionem illius sacræ supellectilis quæ ad normam legum liturgicarum benedici debet antequam ad usum sibi proprium adhibeatur, impertire possunt: 1° S. R. E. Cardinales et Episcopi omnes; 2° Locorum Ordinarii, charactere episcopali carentes, pro ecclesiis et oratoriis proprii territorii; 3° Parochus pro ecclesiis et oratoriis in territorio suæ parœciæ positis, et rectores ecclesiarum pro suis ecclesiis; 4° Sacerdotes a loci Ordinario delegati, intra fines delegationis et jurisdictionis delegantis; 5° Superiores religiosi, et Sacerdotes ejusdem religionis ab ipsis delegati, pro propriis ecclesiis et oratoriis ac pro ecclesiis monialium sibi subjectarum. || 5. The blessing of those sacred furnishings which, according to the liturgical laws, must be blessed before being put to their proper use may be given by: (1) the Cardinals of the Holy Roman Church and all bishops; (2) local Ordinaries who are not bishops, for the churches and oratories of their own territory; (3) the pastor, for the churches and oratories within the territory of his parish, and rectors of churches for their own churches; (4) priests delegated by the local Ordinary, within the limits of the delegation and of the delegating person's jurisdiction; (5) religious superiors, and priests of the same institute delegated by them, for their own churches and oratories and for the churches of nuns subject to them.
r: 6. In omni benedictione extra Missam, Sacerdos saltem superpelliceo et stola coloris tempori convenientis utatur, nisi aliter notetur. || 6. For every blessing outside Mass the priest wears at least a surplice and a stole of the color suited to the season, unless otherwise noted.
r: 7. Stando semper benedicat, et aperto capite. In principio cujusque benedictionis, nisi aliter notetur, dicat: || 7. He always blesses standing and with head uncovered. At the beginning of each blessing, unless otherwise noted, he says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
r: Deinde dicat Orationem propriam, unam vel plures, prout suo loco notatum fuerit. Postea rem aspergat aqua benedicta, et, ubi notatum fuerit, pariter incenset, nihil dicendo. || Then he says the proper prayer, one or more, as noted in each place. Afterward he sprinkles the object with holy water and, where so noted, incenses it as well, saying nothing.
r: 8. Cum Sacerdos aliquid benedicturus est, habeat ministrum cum vase aquæ benedictæ et aspersorio, et cum hoc Rituali libro, seu Missali. || 8. When the priest is about to bless something, he should have a server with the vessel of holy water and the sprinkler, and with this Ritual or the Missal.
r: 9. Caveat, ne benedictionis causa ponat aliquid indecens super Altare, veluti esculenta; sed quod ejusmodi est, ponatur super mensam, commodo loco paratam. || 9. He must take care not to place anything unbecoming on the altar for the purpose of blessing it, such as food; things of that kind are to be placed on a table prepared in a convenient place.

# holy-water | Ordo ad Faciendam Aquam Benedictam | The Order for Making Holy Water | Holy Water | Tit. VIII, cap. 2
r: 1. Diebus Dominicis, et quandocumque opus sit, præparato sale et aqua munda benedicenda in ecclesia, vel in sacristia, Sacerdos, superpelliceo et stola violacea indutus, primo dicit: || 1. On Sundays, and whenever it is needed, salt and clean water to be blessed having been prepared in the church or in the sacristy, the priest, vested in surplice and violet stole, first says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
r: 2. Deinde absolute incipit exorcismum salis. || 2. Then he begins the exorcism of the salt directly.
h: Exorcismus salis || Exorcism of the salt
t: Exorcízo te, creatúra salis, per Deum ✠ vivum, per Deum ✠ verum, per Deum ✠ sanctum, per Deum, qui te per Eliséum Prophétam in aquam mitti jussit, ut sanarétur sterílitas aquæ: ut efficiáris sal exorcizátum in salútem credéntium; et sis ómnibus suméntibus te sánitas ánimæ et córporis; et effúgiat, atque discédat a loco, in quo aspérsum fúeris, omnis phantásia, et nequítia, vel versútia diabólicæ fraudis, omnísque spíritus immúndus, adjurátus per eum, qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos, et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || I exorcise you, creature of salt, by the ✠ living God, by the ✠ true God, by the ✠ holy God, by God who commanded you to be cast into the water by the prophet Elisha, that its barrenness might be healed: that you may become exorcised salt for the salvation of believers; that you may be health of soul and body to all who take you; and that from the place where you are sprinkled every illusion and wickedness and cunning of the devil's deceit may flee and depart, together with every unclean spirit, adjured by Him who is to come to judge the living and the dead, and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Imménsam cleméntiam tuam, omnípotens ætérne Deus, humíliter implorámus, ut hanc creatúram salis, quam in usum géneris humáni tribuísti, bene✠dícere et sancti✠ficáre tua pietáte dignéris: ut sit ómnibus suméntibus salus mentis et córporis; et quidquid ex eo tactum vel respérsum fúerit, cáreat omni immundítia, omníque impugnatióne spiritális nequítiæ. Per Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Almighty, everlasting God, we humbly implore Your boundless mercy, that in Your goodness You would deign to ✠ bless and ✠ sanctify this creature of salt, which You have given for the use of mankind, so that it may be health of mind and body to all who take it; and that whatever is touched or sprinkled with it may be free from all uncleanness and from every attack of spiritual wickedness. Through our Lord. ℟. Amen.
h: Exorcismus aquæ (dicitur absolute) || Exorcism of the water (said directly)
t: Exorcízo te, creatúra aquæ, in nómine Dei ✠ Patris omnipoténtis, et in nómine Jesu ✠ Christi, Fílii ejus, Dómini nostri, et in virtúte Spíritus ✠ Sancti: ut fias aqua exorcizáta ad effugándam omnem potestátem inimíci, et ipsum inimícum eradicáre et explantáre váleas cum ángelis suis apostáticis, per virtútem ejúsdem Dómini nostri Jesu Christi: qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos, et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || I exorcise you, creature of water, in the name of God the ✠ Father almighty, and in the name of Jesus ✠ Christ, His Son, our Lord, and in the power of the Holy ✠ Spirit: that you may become water exorcised to put to flight every power of the enemy, and that you may be able to uproot and cast out the enemy himself with his apostate angels, by the power of the same Jesus Christ our Lord, who is to come to judge the living and the dead, and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Deus, qui ad salútem humáni géneris, máxima quæque sacraménta in aquárum substántia condidísti: adésto propítius invocatiónibus nostris, et eleménto huic multímodis purificatiónibus præparáto, virtútem tuæ bene✠dictiónis infúnde; ut creatúra tua, mystériis tuis sérviens, ad abigéndos dæmones, morbósque pelléndos, divínæ grátiæ sumat efféctum; ut quidquid in dómibus, vel in locis fidélium, hæc unda respérserit, cáreat omni immundítia, liberétur a noxa: non illic resídeat spíritus péstilens, non aura corrúmpens: discédant omnes insídiæ laténtis inimíci; et si quid est, quod aut incolumitáti habitántium ínvidet, aut quiéti, aspersióne hujus aquæ effúgiat: ut salúbritas per invocatiónem sancti tui nóminis expetíta, ab ómnibus sit impugnatiónibus defénsa. Per Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || O God, who for the salvation of mankind have built Your greatest sacraments upon the substance of water: graciously hear our prayers, and pour forth the power of Your ✠ blessing upon this element, prepared by many kinds of purification; so that this Your creature, serving Your mysteries, may receive the effect of divine grace to drive away demons and to banish sickness; that whatever this water sprinkles in the homes or places of the faithful may be freed from all uncleanness and delivered from harm. Let no pestilent spirit, no corrupting air remain there; let all the snares of the hidden enemy depart; and if there is anything that begrudges the safety or the peace of those who dwell there, let it flee at the sprinkling of this water; so that the well-being sought by calling upon Your holy name may be defended against every attack. Through our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: 3. Hic ter mittat sal in aquam in modum crucis, dicendo semel: || 3. Here he puts the salt into the water three times in the form of a cross, saying once:
t: Commíxtio salis et aquæ páriter fiat, in nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. ℟. Amen. || May this mingling of salt and water be made, in the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit. ℟. Amen.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Deus, invíctæ virtútis auctor, et insuperábilis impérii Rex, ac semper magníficus triumphátor: qui advérsæ dominatiónis vires réprimis: qui inimíci rugiéntis sævítiam súperas: qui hostíles nequítias poténter expúgnas: te, Dómine, treméntes et súpplices deprecámur, ac pétimus: ut hanc creatúram salis et aquæ dignánter aspícias, benígnus illústres, pietátis tuæ rore sanctífices; ut, ubicúmque fúerit aspérsa, per invocatiónem sancti nóminis tui, omnis infestátio immúndi spíritus abigátur: terrórque venenósi serpéntis procul pellátur: et præséntia Sancti Spíritus nobis, misericórdiam tuam poscéntibus, ubíque adésse dignétur. Per Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum, Fílium tuum: qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte ejúsdem Spíritus Sancti Deus, per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || O God, author of invincible power, King of an empire that cannot be overcome, and ever glorious conqueror; who hold in check the forces of the adversary's dominion; who overcome the fury of the roaring enemy; who mightily vanquish his hostile wickedness: trembling and humbly we beseech and entreat You, O Lord, graciously to look upon this creature of salt and water, kindly to make it shine, and to sanctify it with the dew of Your goodness; so that wherever it is sprinkled, every assault of the unclean spirit may be driven off by the invocation of Your holy name, the dread of the poisonous serpent may be cast far away, and the presence of the Holy Spirit may everywhere be with us who ask for Your mercy. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, Your Son, who lives and reigns with You in the unity of the same Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: 4. Post benedictionem aquæ, Sacerdos Dominicis diebus, antequam incipiat Missam, aspergit Altare, deinde se, et Ministros, ac populum, prout in Missali præscribitur, et in Appendice hujus Ritualis habetur. || 4. After the blessing of the water, on Sundays before beginning Mass, the priest sprinkles the altar, then himself, the ministers, and the people, as prescribed in the Missal and given in the Appendix of this Ritual.
r: 5. Christifideles autem possunt de ista aqua benedicta in vasculis suis accipere, et secum deferre ad aspergendos ægros, domos, agros, vineas, et alia, et ad eam habendam in cubiculis suis, ut ea cotidie et sæpius aspergi possint. || 5. The faithful may take some of this holy water in their own containers and carry it home to sprinkle the sick, their homes, fields, vineyards, and other things, and to keep it in their rooms, so that they may be sprinkled with it daily and often.
