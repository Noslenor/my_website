// Blessing of the Font or Baptismal Water (Shorter Form for Missionaries)
// ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# font-short | Benedictio Fontis seu Aquæ Baptismalis (formula brevior) | Blessing of the Font or Baptismal Water (Shorter Form for Missionaries) | Sacraments | Appendix, De Baptismo
r: Hæc formula brevior pro benedictione Fontis seu Aquæ baptismalis, a Paulo Papa III Missionariis Peruanis apud Indos olim concessa, nonnisi in iis locis adhiberi potest, ad quæ speciali Apostolicæ Sedis indulto extensa fuit. || This shorter form for blessing the font or baptismal water, granted long ago by Pope Paul III to the missionaries in Peru among the Indians, may be used only in those places to which it has been extended by special indult of the Apostolic See.
n: Parish ritual: this short form was prescribed by the First Provincial Council of Baltimore and approved by Pope Pius VIII for the missionaries of North America (October 16, 1830). The font is first washed and cleansed, and the part for the baptismal water filled with fresh water.
h: Exorcismus aquæ || Exorcism of the Water
t: (Must use the Latin) Exorcízo te, creatúra aquæ, in nómine Dei ✠ Patris omnipoténtis, et in nómine Jesu ✠ Christi, Fílii ejus, Dómini nostri, et in virtúte Spíritus ✠ Sancti. Exorcízo te, omnis virtus adversárii diáboli: ut omnis phantásia eradicétur, ac effugétur ab hac creatúra aquæ, et fiat fons aquæ saliéntis in vitam ætérnam: ut, qui ex ea baptizáti fúerint, fiant templum Dei vivi, et Spíritus Sanctus hábitet in eis, in remissiónem peccatórum: in nómine Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos, et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || (Must use the Latin) I exorcize you, creature of water, in the name of God ✠ the Father almighty, and in the name of Jesus ✠ Christ, his Son, our Lord, and in the power of the Holy ✠ Spirit. I exorcize you, every power of our enemy, the devil. May every wicked apparition be rooted out and cast forth from this creature of water, that it may be a fountain of water, springing up unto life everlasting. May those who will be baptized in this water become the temple of the living God, and may the Holy Spirit live in them, for the forgiveness of sins: in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, who is to come to judge the living and the dead and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: (Must use the Latin) Dómine sancte, Pater omnípotens, ætérne Deus, aquárum spirituálium sanctificátor, te supplíciter deprecámur: ut hoc ministérium humilitátis nostræ respícere dignéris, et super has aquas, abluéndis et vivificándis homínibus præparátas, Ángelum sanctitátis emíttas; ut peccátis prióris vitæ ablútis, reatúque detérso, purum sacráto Spirítui habitáculum regeneratiónibus procúret. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || (Must use the Latin) Lord, holy Father, almighty everlasting God, the sanctifier of spiritual waters, humbly we beg you: look down upon our lowly service and send the angel of holiness upon these waters, prepared to wash and to give life to men, so that, with the sins of their past life washed away and their guilt wiped out, they may be reborn as a pure dwelling place for the Holy Spirit. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Sacerdos deinceps infundat Oleum Catechumenorum in aquam, in modum crucis, dicens: || The priest then pours oil of catechumens (O.S.) into the water, in the form of a cross, saying:
t: (Must use the Latin) Conjúnctio Olei unctiónis, et aquæ Baptísmatis sanctificétur, et fecundétur. In nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. ℟. Amen. || (Must use the Latin) May this union of the oil of anointing with the water of baptism be made holy and fruitful. In the name of the Father ✠, and of the Son ✠, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit. ℟. Amen.
r: Deinde infundat Chrisma in aquam, in modum crucis, dicens: || Then he pours chrism (S.C.) into the water, in the form of a cross, saying:
t: (Must use the Latin) Conjúnctio Chrísmatis sanctificatiónis et Olei unctiónis et aquæ Baptísmatis sanctificétur, et fecundétur. In nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. ℟. Amen. || (Must use the Latin) May this union of the chrism of salvation with the oil of anointing and the water of baptism be made holy and fruitful. In the name of the Father ✠, and of the Son ✠, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit. ℟. Amen.
h: Formula Concilii Baltimorensis || Closing Form of the Council of Baltimore
r: Denique dicat Sacerdos, benedicens ipsam aquam: || Finally the priest says, as he blesses the water itself:
t: (Must use the Latin) Sanctificétur et fecundétur Fons iste, et ex eo renascéntes. In nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. ℟. Amen. || (Must use the Latin) May this water be made holy and fruitful, together with those who will be reborn in it. In the name of the Father ✠, and of the Son ✠, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit. ℟. Amen.
