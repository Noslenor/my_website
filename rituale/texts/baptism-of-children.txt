# baptism-infants | Ordo Baptismi Parvulorum | The Rite of Baptism for Children | Sacraments | Tit. II, cap. 2
r: 1. Sacerdos interrogat infantem (si plures sint baptizandi, interrogat singulariter singulos): || 1. The priest questions the child (if several are to be baptized, he questions each one individually):
t: *Sacerdos:* N., Quid petis ab Ecclésia Dei? || *Priest:* N., what do you ask of the Church of God?
t: *Patrinus respondet:* Fidem. || *The godparent answers:* The Faith.
t: *Sacerdos:* Fides, quid tibi præstat? || *Priest:* What does faith offer you?
t: *Patrinus respondet:* Vitam ætérnam. || *The godparent answers:* Eternal life.
r: 2. Sacerdos (etiam singulariter singulis): || 2. The priest (again to each individually):
t: Si ígitur vis ad vitam íngredi, serva mandáta. Díliges Dóminum Deum tuum ex toto corde tuo, et ex tota ánima tua, et ex tota mente tua, et próximum tuum sicut teípsum. || If, then, you wish to enter into life, keep the commandments: you shall love the Lord your God with your whole heart, and with your whole soul, and with your whole mind, and your neighbor as yourself.
r: 3. Deinde ter exsufflat leniter in faciem infantis, et dicit semel (singulariter singulis): || 3. Then he breathes gently three times on the child's face, and says once (to each individually):
t: Exi ab eo (ea), immúnde spíritus, et da locum Spirítui Sancto Paráclito. || Depart from him (her), unclean spirit, and give place to the Holy Spirit, the consoler.
r: 4. Postea pollice facit signum crucis in fronte, et in pectore infantis, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 4. Then with his thumb he makes the sign of the cross on the forehead and on the breast of the child, saying (to each individually):
t: Accipe signum Crucis tam in fron✠te, quam in cor✠de, sume fidem cæléstium præceptórum: et talis esto móribus, ut templum Dei jam esse possis. || Receive the mark of the Cross both on your fore✠head and within your ✠heart. Embrace the faith with its divine teachings. So live that you will indeed be be a temple of God.
t: Orémus. *(In plurali pro pluribus.)* || Let us pray. *(In the plural for several.)*
t: Preces nostras, quǽsumus, Dómine, cleménter exáudi: et hunc eléctum tuum N. (hanc eléctam tuam N.) Crucis Domínicæ impressióne signátum (-am) perpétua virtúte custódi: ut, magnitúdinis glóriæ tuæ rudiménta servans, per custódiam mandatórum tuórum ad regeneratiónis glóriam perveníre mereátur. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || We beg you Lord God, graciously hear our prayers. Guard your chosen one, N., with the never-failing power of the cross of Christ, with which he (she) has been marked. Protect him (her) so that, remaining true to the first lessions he (she) has learned about the great glory you will confer upon him (her), he (she) may, by keeping your commandments, attain to the gory of a new birth. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: 5. Deinde imponit manum super caput infantis (singulorum infantium), et postea manum extensam tenens dicit: || 5. Then he lays his hand on the head of the child (of each child), and afterward, holding his hand extended, he says:
t: Orémus. *(In plurali pro pluribus.)* || Let us pray. *(In the plural for several.)*
t: Omnípotens sempitérne Deus, Pater Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, respícere dignáre super hunc fámulum tuum N. (hanc fámulam tuam N.), quem (quam) ad rudiménta fídei vocáre dignátus es: omnem cæcitátem cordis ab eo (ea) expélle: disrúmpe omnes láqueos sátanæ, quibus fúerat colligátus (-a): áperi ei, Dómine, jánuam pietátis tuæ, ut signo sapiéntiæ tuæ imbútus (-a), ómnium cupiditátum fœtóribus cáreat, et ad suávem odórem præceptórum tuórum lætus (-a) tibi in Ecclésia tua desérviat, et profíciat de die in diem. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Almighty and eternal God, Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, look with favor upon this Your servant N., whom You have called to take his first steps in the faith. Take from him (her) all blindness of heart. Free him (her) from the snares of Satan which until now have held him (her). Open to him (her), Lord, the gate of Your mercy. Then, seasoned by the salt which is symbolic of your wisdom, may he (she) be relieved of the corruption of evil desires; and, finding pleasure in your commandments, may he (she) serve You in Your Church and make progress from day to day in the way of perfection. Through the same Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: 6. Deinde Sacerdos benedicit sal, quod semel benedictum alias ad eundem usum deservire potest. || 6. Then the priest blesses the salt, which, once blessed, may be used again for the same purpose.
h: Benedictio salis || Blessing of the salt (Must be said in Latin)
t: Exorcízo te, creatúra salis, in nómine Dei ✠ Patris omnipoténtis, et in caritáte Dómini nostri Jesu ✠ Christi, et in virtúte Spíritus ✠ Sancti. Exorcízo te per Deum ✠ vivum, per Deum ✠ verum, per Deum ✠ sanctum, per Deum ✠, qui te ad tutélam humáni géneris procreávit, et pópulo veniénti ad credulitátem per servos suos consecrári præcépit, ut in nómine sanctæ Trinitátis efficiáris salutáre sacraméntum ad effugándum inimícum. Proínde rogámus te, Dómine Deus noster, ut hanc creatúram salis sanctificándo sanctí✠fices, et benedicéndo bene✠dícas, ut fiat ómnibus accipiéntibus perfécta medicína, pérmanens in viscéribus eórum, in nómine ejúsdem Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos, et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || (Use Latin above) I exorcise you, creature of salt, in the name of God the ✠ Father almighty, and in the love of our Lord Jesus ✠ Christ, and in the power of the Holy ✠ Spirit. I exorcise you by the ✠ living God, by the ✠ true God, by the ✠ holy God, by ✠ God who created you for the protection of the human race and commanded that you be consecrated by His servants for the people coming to the faith, so that in the name of the Holy Trinity you may become a saving sacrament to put the enemy to flight. Therefore we ask You, O Lord our God, that by sanctifying You may sanc✠tify, and by blessing You may ✠ bless this creature of salt, so that it may become for all who receive it a perfect medicine, abiding in their inmost being; in the name of the same Jesus Christ our Lord, who is to come to judge the living and the dead, and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
r: 7. Deinde immittit modicum salis benedicti in os infantis, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 7. Then he puts a little of the blessed salt into the child's mouth, saying (to each individually):
t: N., Accipe sal sapiéntiæ: propitiátio sit tibi in vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || N., receive the salt, which is a symbol of wisdom. May it bring you God's favor for life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
t: *Sacerdos:* Pax tecum. ℟. Et cum spíritu tuo. || *Priest:* Peace be with you. ℟. And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. *(In plurali pro pluribus.)* || Let us pray. *(In the plural for several.)*
t: Deus patrum nostrórum, Deus univérsæ cónditor veritátis, te súpplices exorámus, ut hunc fámulum tuum N. (hanc fámulam tuam N.) respícere dignéris propítius, et hoc primum pábulum salis gustántem, non diútius esuríre permíttas, quo minus cibo expleátur cælésti, quátenus sit semper spíritu fervens, spe gaudens, tuo semper nómini sérviens. Perduc eum (eam), Dómine, quǽsumus, ad novæ regeneratiónis lavácrum, ut cum fidélibus tuis promissiónum tuárum ætérna prǽmia cónsequi mereátur. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || God of our fathers, God, the author of all truth, we humbly ask You to look with favor on this Your servant, N., who has had his (her) first taste of blessed food in the form of salt. Satisfy him (her) with the bread of heaven, so that he (she) may be forever fervent in spirit, joyful in hope, and zealous in your service. We ask you, Lord, to lead him (her) to the waters in which he (she) will be born again, so that he (she) with all who believe in you, may obtain the unending rewards which you have promised. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
t: (Must be done in Latin) Exorcízo te, immúnde spíritus, in nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti, ut éxeas, et recédas ab hoc fámulo (hac fámula) Dei N.: Ipse enim tibi ímperat, maledícte damnáte, qui pédibus super mare ambulávit, et Petro mergénti déxteram porréxit. || (Do the Latin above) I exorcise you, unclean spirit, in the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit, that you go out and depart from this servant of God, N. For He commands you, accursed and damned one, who walked upon the sea and stretched out His right hand to Peter as he was sinking.
t: (Must be done in Latin) Ergo, maledícte diábole, recognósce senténtiam tuam, et da honórem Deo vivo et vero, da honórem Jesu Christo Fílio ejus, et Spirítui Sancto, et recéde ab hoc fámulo (hac fámula) Dei N., quia istum (istam) sibi Deus et Dóminus noster Jesus Christus ad suam sanctam grátiam, et benedictiónem, fontémque Baptísmatis vocáre dignátus est. || (Do the Latin above) Therefore, accursed devil, acknowledge your sentence, and give honor to the living and true God; give honor to Jesus Christ, His Son, and to the Holy Spirit; and depart from this servant of God, N., for our God and Lord Jesus Christ has deigned to call him (her) to His holy grace and blessing and to the font of Baptism.
r: 8. Hic pollice in fronte signat infantem, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 8. Here with his thumb he signs the child on the forehead, saying (to each individually):
t: Et hoc signum sanctæ Cru✠cis, quod nos fronti ejus damus, tu, maledícte diábole, numquam áudeas violáre. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Accursed devil, never dare to desecrate this sign of the holy ✠ Cross, which we are tracing upon his (her) forehead. Through the same Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: 9. Mox imponit manum super caput infantis (singulorum infantium), et postea manum extensam tenens dicit: || 9. Next he lays his hand on the head of the child (of each child), and afterward, holding his hand extended, he says:
t: Orémus. *(In plurali pro pluribus.)* || Let us pray. *(In the plural for several.)*
t: Ætérnam ac justíssimam pietátem tuam déprecor, Dómine sancte, Pater omnípotens, ætérne Deus, auctor lúminis et veritátis, super hunc fámulum tuum N. (hanc fámulam tuam N.), ut dignéris eum (eam) illumináre lúmine intellegéntiæ tuæ: munda eum (eam), et sanctífica: da ei sciéntiam veram, ut dignus (-a) grátia Baptísmi tui efféctus (-a), téneat firmam spem, consílium rectum, doctrínam sanctam. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Lord, holy Father, almighty and eternal God, source of light and truth, I humble beg your never-ending and most holy mercy upon this servant of yours, N. May it please you to grant him (her) the light of your own wisdom. Cleanse him (her) and make him (her) holy. Give him (her) true knowledge, so that he (she) may be made worthy of the grace of Your Baptism and may maintain firm hope, sound judgment, and a grasp of holy doctrine. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: 10. Postea Sacerdos imponit extremam partem stolæ pendentem a suo humero sinistro super primum infantem, et introducit eum (quem sequuntur ceteri) in ecclesiam, dicens (in plurali pro pluribus): || 10. Then the priest places the end of the stole hanging from his left shoulder upon the first child and leads him (the others following) into the church, saying (in the plural for several):
t: N., Ingrédere in templum Dei, ut hábeas partem cum Christo in vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || N., enter into the temple of God, so that you may have part with Christ in everlasting life. ℟. Amen.
r: 11. Cum fuerint ecclesiam ingressi, Sacerdos procedens ad Fontem, cum susceptoribus conjunctim clara voce dicit: || 11. When they have entered the church, the priest, proceeding to the font, says aloud together with the sponsors:
t: Credo in Deum, Patrem omnipoténtem, Creatórem cæli et terræ. Et in Jesum Christum, Fílium ejus únicum, Dóminum nostrum: qui concéptus est de Spíritu Sancto, natus ex María Vírgine, passus sub Póntio Piláto, crucifíxus, mórtuus, et sepúltus: descéndit ad ínferos; tértia die resurréxit a mórtuis; ascéndit ad cælos; sedet ad déxteram Dei Patris omnipoténtis: inde ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos. Credo in Spíritum Sanctum, sanctam Ecclésiam cathólicam, Sanctórum communiónem, remissiónem peccatórum, carnis resurrectiónem, vitam ætérnam. Amen. || I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth; and in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried. He descended into hell; the third day He arose again from the dead; He ascended into heaven, sitteth at the right hand of God, the Father almighty; thence He shall come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Ghost, the holy Catholic Church, the communion of Saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.
t: Pater noster, qui es in cælis, sanctificétur nomen tuum. Advéniat regnum tuum. Fiat volúntas tua, sicut in cælo et in terra. Panem nostrum cotidiánum da nobis hódie. Et dimítte nobis débita nostra, sicut et nos dimíttimus debitóribus nostris. Et ne nos indúcas in tentatiónem: sed líbera nos a malo. Amen. || Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be Thy name; Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.
r: 12. Ac deinde, antequam accedat ad Baptisterium, versis renibus ostio cancellorum Baptisterii, dicit (in plurali pro pluribus): || 12. And then, before he approaches the baptistery, with his back turned to the gate of the baptistery railing, he says (in the plural for several):
h: Exorcismus || Exorcism (Must be done in Latin)
t: Exorcízo te, omnis spíritus immúnde, in nómine Dei ✠ Patris omnipoténtis, et in nómine Jesu ✠ Christi Fílii ejus, Dómini et Júdicis nostri, et in virtúte Spíritus ✠ Sancti, ut discédas ab hoc plásmate Dei N., quod Dóminus noster ad templum sanctum suum vocáre dignátus est, ut fiat templum Dei vivi, et Spíritus Sanctus hábitet in eo. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum, qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos, et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || (Do the Latin above) I exorcise you, every unclean spirit, in the name of God the ✠ Father almighty, and in the name of Jesus ✠ Christ, His Son, our Lord and Judge, and in the power of the Holy ✠ Spirit, that you depart from this creature of God, N., whom our Lord has deigned to call to His holy temple, that he (she) may become a temple of the living God and the Holy Spirit may dwell in him (her). Through the same Christ our Lord, who is to come to judge the living and the dead, and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
r: 13. Postea Sacerdos pollice accipit de saliva oris sui, et tangit aures et nares infantis: tangendo vero aurem dexteram et sinistram, dicit (singulariter singulis): || 13. Then the priest takes a little spittle from his mouth with his thumb and touches the ears and nostrils of the child; touching the right and the left ear, he says (to each individually):
t: (Use the Latin) Ephpheta, quod est, Adaperíre. || (Use the Latin) Ephpheta, that is, Be opened.
r: Deinde tangit nares, dicens: || Then he touches the nostrils, saying:
t: (Use the Latin) In odórem suavitátis. Tu autem effugáre, diábole; appropinquábit enim judícium Dei. || (Use the Latin) Unto the odor of sweetness. But you, devil, take flight; for the judgment of God draws near.
r: 14. Postea interrogat baptizandum nominatim, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 14. Then he questions the one to be baptized by name, saying (to each individually):
t: N., Abrenúntias sátanæ? || N., do you renounce Satan?
t: *Respondet Patrinus:* Abrenúntio. || *The godparent answers:* I do renounce him.
t: *Sacerdos:* Et ómnibus opéribus ejus? || *Priest:* And all his works?
t: *Patrinus:* Abrenúntio. || *Godparent:* I do renounce them.
t: *Sacerdos:* Et ómnibus pompis ejus? || *Priest:* And all his allurements?
t: *Patrinus:* Abrenúntio. || *Godparent:* I do renounce them.
r: 15. Deinde Sacerdos intingit pollicem in oleo Catechumenorum, et infantem ungit in pectore, et inter scapulas in modum crucis, dicens semel (singulariter singulis): || 15. Then the priest dips his thumb in the oil of catechumens and anoints the child on the breast and between the shoulders in the form of a cross, saying once (to each individually):
t: (Use the Latin) Ego te línio ✠ óleo salútis in Christo Jesu, Dómino nostro, ut hábeas vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || (Use the Latin) I anoint you ✠ with the oil of salvation in Christ Jesus our Lord, that you may have life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
r: 16. Subinde pollicem, et inuncta loca abstergit bombacio, vel re simili. || 16. He then wipes his thumb and the anointed places with cotton or something similar.
h: Stolam Albi || White Stole
h: Intra Baptisterium || Within the Baptistery
r: 17. Stans ibidem extra cancellos deponit stolam violaceam, et sumit stolam albi coloris. Tunc ingreditur Baptisterium, in quod intrat etiam patrinus cum infante. Sacerdos ad Fontem interrogat, expresso nomine, baptizandum (singulos baptizandos), patrino respondente: || 17. Standing there outside the railing, he lays aside the violet stole and takes a white one. He then enters the baptistery, into which the godparent also enters with the child. At the font the priest questions the one to be baptized (each one), calling him by name, the godparent answering:
t: N., Credis in Deum Patrem omnipoténtem, Creatórem cæli et terræ? ℟. Credo. || N., do you believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth? ℟. I do believe.
t: Credis in Jesum Christum, Fílium ejus únicum, Dóminum nostrum, natum, et passum? ℟. Credo. || Do you believe in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord, who was born into this world and who suffered? ℟. I do believe.
t: Credis et in Spíritum Sanctum, sanctam Ecclésiam cathólicam, Sanctórum communiónem, remissiónem peccatórum, carnis resurrectiónem, et vitam ætérnam? ℟. Credo. || Do you believe also in the Holy Spirit, the holy Catholic Church, the communion of Saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting? ℟. I do believe.
r: 18. Subinde, expresso nomine baptizandi, Sacerdos dicit (singulariter singulis): || 18. Then, naming the one to be baptized, the priest says (to each individually):
t: N., Vis baptizári? || N., do you wish to be baptized?
t: *Respondet patrinus:* Volo. || *The godparent answers:* I do.
h: Baptismus || The Baptism
r: 19. Tunc patrino, vel matrina, vel utroque (si ambo admittantur) infantem tenente, Sacerdos vasculo, seu urceolo accipit aquam baptismalem, et eam ter fundit super caput infantis in modum crucis, et simul verba proferens, semel tantum distincte, et attente, dicit (singulariter singulis): || 19. Then, while the godfather or godmother, or both (if both are admitted), hold the child, the priest takes baptismal water in a small vessel or pitcher and pours it three times on the child's head in the form of a cross, at the same time saying the words once only, distinctly and attentively (to each individually):
t: (Must use the Latin) N., Ego te baptízo in nómine Patris, *(fundit primo)* et Fílii, *(fundit secundo)* et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. *(fundit tertio)* || (Must use the Latin) N., I baptize you in the name of the Father, *(he pours the first time)* and of the Son, *(he pours the second time)* and of the Holy ✠ Spirit. *(he pours the third time)*
r: 20. Ubi autem est consuetudo baptizandi per immersionem, Sacerdos accipit infantem, et, advertens ne lædatur, caute immergit, et trina immersione baptizat, et semel tantum dicit: || 20. Where the custom is to baptize by immersion, the priest takes the child and, taking care that it is not harmed, carefully immerses it, baptizing it by a threefold immersion, and says only once:
t: (Must use the Latin) N., Ego te baptízo in nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. || (Must use the Latin) N., I baptize you in the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit.
r: 21. Mox patrinus, vel matrina, vel uterque simul infantem de sacro Fonte levant, suscipientes illum de manu Sacerdotis. || 21. Then the godfather or godmother, or both together, raise the child from the sacred font, receiving it from the hand of the priest.
r: 22. Si vero dubitetur, an infans fuerit baptizatus, utatur hac forma: || 22. If there is doubt whether the child has already been baptized, this form is used:
t: (Must use the Latin) N., Si non es baptizátus (-a), ego te baptízo in nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. || (Must use the Latin) N., if you are not baptized, I baptize you in the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit.
h: Unctio Chrismatis || Anointing with Chrism
r: 23. Deinde intingit pollicem in sacro Chrismate, et ungit infantem in summitate capitis in modum crucis, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 23. Then he dips his thumb in the holy chrism and anoints the child on the crown of the head in the form of a cross, saying (to each individually):
t: (Must use the Latin) Deus omnípotens, Pater Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, qui te regenerávit ex aqua et Spíritu Sancto, quique dedit tibi remissiónem ómnium peccatórum, *(hic inungit)* ipse te líniat ✠ Chrísmate salútis in eódem Christo Jesu Dómino nostro in vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || (Must use the Latin) May almighty God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has given you new birth by water and the Holy Spirit and has granted you the forgiveness of all your sins, *(here he anoints)* Himself anoint you ✠ with the chrism of salvation in the same Christ Jesus our Lord unto life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
t: *Sacerdos:* Pax tibi. ℟. Et cum spíritu tuo. || *Priest:* Peace be with you. ℟. And with your spirit.
r: 24. Tum bombacio, vel re simili, abstergit pollicem, et locum inunctum, et imponit capiti ejus linteolum candidum loco vestis albæ, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 24. Then with cotton or something similar he wipes his thumb and the anointed place, and lays a white linen cloth on the child's head in place of the white garment, saying (to each individually):
t: Accipe vestem cándidam, quam pérferas immaculátam ante tribúnal Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, ut hábeas vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || Receive this white garment. Never let it become stained, so that, when you stand before the judgment seat of our Lord Jesus Christ, you may have life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
r: 25. Postea dat ei, vel patrino, candelam accensam, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 25. Then he gives to the child, or to the godparent, a lighted candle, saying (to each individually):
t: Accipe lámpadem ardéntem, et irreprehensíbilis custódi Baptísmum tuum: serva Dei mandáta, ut, cum Dóminus vénerit ad núptias, possis occúrrere ei una cum ómnibus Sanctis in aula cælésti, et vivas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Receive this burning light, and keep the grace of your Baptism throughout a blameless life. Observe the commandments of God. h: Rubricæ || Rubrics
r: 27. Ritus superius descriptus servandus est etiam a Diacono Baptismum sollemnem ministrante, qui tamen sale et aqua utetur a Sacerdote ad hunc usum rite prius benedictis. || 27. The rite described above is to be observed also by a deacon administering solemn Baptism; he shall, however, use salt and water previously blessed for this purpose by a priest.
r: 28. Si vero sint plures baptizandi, sive masculi, sive feminæ, in catechismo masculi statuantur ad dexteram, feminæ vero ad sinistram; et omnia pariter dicantur ut supra, in proprio genere, et numero plurali. Verum prima nominis interrogatio, exsufflatio, crucis impressio, seu signatio, tactus aurium et narium cum saliva, abrenuntiationis interrogatio, unctio Olei Catechumenorum, interrogatio de Fide, seu Symbolo, et ipse Baptismus, inunctio Chrismatis, candidæ vestis impositio, atque accensæ candelæ traditio, singulariter singulis, et primum masculis, deinde feminis, fieri debent. || 28. If there are several to be baptized, whether boys or girls, the boys are placed on the right during the catechism and the girls on the left; and everything is said as above in the proper gender and in the plural. But the first questioning by name, the breathing, the imposition or signing of the cross, the touching of the ears and nostrils with spittle, the questioning on the renunciation, the anointing with the oil of catechumens, the questioning on the faith or Creed, the Baptism itself, the anointing with chrism, the giving of the white garment, and the handing over of the lighted candle are to be done for each individually, first for the boys and then for the girls.
r: Sed pro majori commoditate adnotatum est propriis locis in rubricis parenthesi notatis; ita ut quando dicitur in tali rubrica singulariter singulis, intellegatur quod illa verba, vel actiones, usque ad aliam rubricam dici, vel fieri debent singulariter singulis, et primum masculis, deinde feminis, mutato solum genere, ut dictum est supra. Quando vero in simili rubrica dicitur in plurali pro pluribus, intellegatur, quod illa verba usque ad aliam rubricam dici debent semel, sed pro pluribus in plurali, mutato solum genere, si omnes sint feminæ. || For greater convenience this is noted in parentheses in the rubrics at the proper places: when such a rubric says "to each individually," the words or actions up to the next rubric are to be said or done for each individually, first for the boys and then for the girls, changing only the gender, as said above. When a similar rubric says "in the plural for several," the words up to the next rubric are said once, but in the plural for all, changing only the gender if all are girls.
r: 29. Si infans, vel adultus ægrotus adeo graviter laboret, ut periculum immineat, ne pereat antequam Baptismus perficiatur, Sacerdos, omissis quæ Baptismum præcedunt, eum baptizet, ter, vel etiam semel infundens aquam super caput ejus in modum crucis, dicens: Ego te baptízo in nómine Patris, etc., ut supra. Si non habeatur aqua Baptismalis, et periculum impendeat, Sacerdos utatur aqua simplici. Deinde si habeat Chrisma, liniat eum in vertice, dicens: Deus omnípotens, etc., ut supra. Postea dat ei linteolum candidum, dicens: Accipe vestem, etc., ut supra. Ac demum dat ei ceream candelam accensam, dicens: Accipe lámpadem, ut supra. Si supervixerit, suppleantur alii ritus omissi. || 29. If a child, or a sick adult, is in such grave danger that he may die before the Baptism is completed, the priest, omitting what precedes the Baptism, baptizes him, pouring water on his head three times, or even once, in the form of a cross, saying: N., I baptize you in the name of the Father, etc., as above. If baptismal water is not at hand and danger is imminent, the priest uses ordinary water. Then, if he has the chrism, he anoints him on the crown of the head, saying: May almighty God, etc., as above. Afterward he gives him the white cloth, saying: Receive this white garment, etc., as above. Finally he gives him a lighted wax candle, saying: Receive this burning light, as above. If the person survives, the other rites that were omitted are to be supplied.
r: 30. Si plures sint baptizandi, et periculum mortis immineat, tempusque non suppetat, ut singuli separatim baptizentur, poterit minister singulorum capitibus aquam infundens omnes simul baptizare, dicendo: Ego vos baptízo in nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. Quam tamen formam ad plures simul baptizandos solum in mortis periculo, et ubi tempus non patiatur, ut singuli separatim baptizentur, alias numquam licet adhibere. || 30. If there are several to be baptized, danger of death is imminent, and there is not time to baptize each one separately, the minister may baptize them all together, pouring water on the head of each and saying: I baptize you all in the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit. This form for baptizing several at once may be used only in danger of death and when time does not allow each to be baptized separately; otherwise it is never permitted.
r: 31. Admonendi sunt susceptores de spirituali cognatione, quam contraxerunt cum baptizato; quæ cognatio impedit Matrimonium, ac dirimit. || 31. The sponsors are to be reminded of the spiritual relationship they have contracted with the one baptized, a relationship that is an impediment to marriage and renders it invalid.
r: 32. Curet Parochus parentes infantis admoneri, ne in lecto secum ipsi, vel nutrices parvulum habeant, propter oppressionis periculum; sed eum diligenter custodiant, et opportune ad christianam disciplinam instituant. || 32. The pastor should see that the child's parents are warned not to keep the little one in bed with them, nor let nurses do so, because of the danger of smothering; but to watch over it carefully and, in due time, bring it up in the Christian way of life.
r: 33. Commonendi sunt etiam parentes, et alii, si opus fuerit, ne filios hebræis, aliisve infidelibus, vel hæreticis mulieribus ullo modo lactandos, aut nutriendos tradant. || 33. Parents and others are also to be warned, if need be, not to give their children in any way to be nursed or raised by Jewish, other non-Christian, or heretical women.
r: 34. Antequam infans ex ecclesia asportetur, aut susceptores discedant, eorum nomina, et alia de administrato Baptismo ad præscriptam formam in Baptismali libro Parochus accurate describat. || 34. Before the child is carried out of the church or the sponsors leave, the pastor is carefully to record their names and the other particulars of the Baptism in the baptismal register, in the prescribed form.
r: 35. Si Baptismus nec a proprio Parocho nec eo præsente administratus fuerit, minister de ipso collato quamprimum proprium ratione domicilii Parochum baptizati certiorem reddat. || 35. If the Baptism was administered neither by the proper pastor nor in his presence, the minister is to inform the pastor of the baptized person's domicile as soon as possible that it has been conferred.
r: 36. Ad collatum Baptismum comprobandum, si nemini fiat præjudicium, satis est unus testis omni exceptione major, vel ipsius baptizati jusjurandum, si ipse in adulta ætate Baptismum receperit. || 36. To prove that Baptism has been conferred, provided no one's rights are prejudiced, one entirely trustworthy witness suffices, or the sworn statement of the baptized person himself if he received Baptism as an adult.
