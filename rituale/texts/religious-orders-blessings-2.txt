// Blessing and Investiture with the Black Scapular of the Holy Cross and Passion of Our Lord Jesus Christ

# passionist-scapular | Benedictio et Impositio Scapularis Nigri Ss. Crucis et Passionis D. N. J. C. | Blessing and Investiture with the Black Scapular of the Holy Cross and Passion of Our Lord Jesus Christ | Sacramentals & Scapulars | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ (Congr. Clericorum Regularium Ss. Crucis et Passionis)
r: Propria Congregationis Clericorum Regularium Sanctissimæ Crucis et Passionis D. N. J. C. || Proper to the Congregation of the Discalced Clerics of the Most Holy Cross and Passion of Our Lord Jesus Christ (Passionists).
r: Sacerdos indutus superpelliceo et stola rubea, eo genuflexo, cui Scapulare est imponendum, dicere potest: || The priest, vested in surplice and red stole, while the one who is to receive the scapular kneels, may say:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Jesu Christe, qui pro redemptióne mundi voluísti nasci, circumcídi, a Judǽis reprobári, a Juda traditóre ósculo tradi, vínculis alligári, sicut agnus ínnocens ad víctimam duci, atque conspéctibus Annæ, Cáiphæ, Piláti et Heródis indecénter offérri, a falsis téstibus accusári, flagéllis et oppróbriis vexári, sputis cónspui, spinis coronári, cólaphis cædi, arúndine pércuti, fácie velári, véstibus éxui, Cruci clavis affígi et láncea vulnerári: tu, Dómine, per has sanctíssimas pœnas tuas, et sanctam Crucem et mortem tuam, hujus fámuli tui (fámulæ tuæ) intelléctum illúmina, afféctum inflámma, ut caritátis tuæ dulcédine perfúsus (-a) lácrimas compunctiónis júgiter effúndat, totáque virtúte te díligat, et quæ tibi plácita sunt, tota dilectióne perfíciat: Qui vivis et regnas cum Deo Patre in unitáte Spíritus Sancti Deus, per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus Christ, who for the redemption of the world willed to be born, to be circumcised, to be rejected by the Jews, to be betrayed with a kiss by Judas the traitor, to be bound with chains, to be led like an innocent lamb to the slaughter, to be shamefully brought before the eyes of Annas, Caiaphas, Pilate, and Herod, to be accused by false witnesses, to be tormented with scourges and insults, to be spat upon, crowned with thorns, struck with blows, beaten with a reed, blindfolded, stripped of Your garments, nailed to the Cross, and pierced with a lance: do You, O Lord, by these Your most holy sufferings, and by Your holy Cross and death, enlighten the understanding and inflame the heart of this Your servant (handmaid), that, filled with the sweetness of Your love, he (she) may continually shed tears of compunction, love You with all his (her) strength, and with complete love carry out whatever is pleasing to You: who live and reign with God the Father in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Prædictæ tamen preces omitti possunt ad libitum. Deinde dicit: || The foregoing prayers, however, may be omitted at will. Then he says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Sit nomen Dómini benedíctum. || Blessed be the name of the Lord.
x: Ex hoc nunc et usque in sǽculum. || Now and for ever.
v: Dómine, exáudi oratiónem meam. || O Lord, hear my prayer.
x: Et clamor meus ad te véniat. || And let my cry come unto You.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Jesu Christe, qui tégumen mortalitátis nostræ assúmere dignátus es: te humíliter deprecámur; ut hoc induméntum, quod in memóriam tuæ sanctíssimæ passiónis et mortis institútum fuit, bene✠dícere, et sancti✠ficáre dignéris; ut hic fámulus tuus qui (hæc fámula tua, quæ) ipsum gestáverit, ejúsdem Passiónis párticeps efféctus (-a), ætérnam glóriam tuam cónsequi mereátur: Qui vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus Christ, who deigned to take upon Yourself the garment of our mortality: we humbly beseech You to ✠ bless and ✠ sanctify this garment, which was instituted in memory of Your most holy passion and death, that this Your servant (handmaid) who wears it, being made a sharer in that same Passion, may be worthy to attain Your eternal glory: who live and reign for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Mox Sacerdos sacrum Scapulare aspergit aqua benedicta, et illud imponit, dicens: || The priest then sprinkles the holy scapular with holy water and places it on the person, saying:
t: Induat te Dóminus novum hóminem, ut hac lúgubri pæniténtiæ téssera munítus (-a) júgiter aspícias ad Jesum, quem manus impiórum confixérunt, et dóleas super eum, ut doléri solet in morte primogéniti. ℟. Amen. || May the Lord clothe you with the new man, that, protected by this mournful token of penance, you may ever look upon Jesus, whom the hands of the wicked have pierced, and mourn over Him as one mourns at the death of a firstborn son. ℟. Amen.
r: Deinde subjungit: || Then he adds:
t: Et ego ex facultáte mihi concéssa recípio te ad participatiónem ómnium bonórum spirituálium, quibus ex Apostólico privilégio gaudet Congregátio Sanctíssimæ Crucis et Passiónis Dómini nostri Jesu Christi. In nómine Patris, et Fílii, ✠ et Spíritus Sancti. ℟. Amen. || And I, by the faculty granted to me, receive you into participation in all the spiritual goods which the Congregation of the Most Holy Cross and Passion of our Lord Jesus Christ enjoys by Apostolic privilege. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, ✠ and of the Holy Spirit. ℟. Amen.
r: In fine tandem dicitur: || Finally, at the end, is said:
t: Pássio Dómini nostri Jesu Christi sit semper in córdibus nostris. Amen. || May the Passion of our Lord Jesus Christ be always in our hearts. Amen.

# red-scapular | Benedictio et Impositio Scapularis Rubri Passionis et Sacratissimi Cordis D. N. J. C. atque Cordis Amantissimi et Compatientis B. Mariæ Virg. Immaculatæ | Blessing and Investiture with the Red Scapular of the Passion and of the Most Sacred Heart of Our Lord Jesus Christ and of the Most Loving and Compassionate Heart of the Blessed Virgin Mary Immaculate | Sacramentals & Scapulars | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ (Congr. Missionis)
r: Propria Congregationis Missionis (Approbata a S. R. C. die 25 Junii 1847). || Proper to the Congregation of the Mission (approved by the Sacred Congregation of Rites, 25 June 1847).
r: Genuflexo eo, qui suscepturus est Scapulare, Sacerdos, superpelliceo et stola rubra indutus, dicit: || While the one who is to receive the scapular kneels, the priest, vested in surplice and red stole, says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Jesu Christe, qui tégumen nostræ mortalitátis indúere dignátus, temetípsum exinanivísti, formam servi accípiens, et factus obœdiens usque ad mortem Crucis: tuæ largitátis cleméntiam humíliter implorámus, ut hoc genus vestiménti, quod in honórem et memóriam dolorosíssimæ Passiónis tuæ, tuíque sacratíssimi Cordis, necnon et Cordis amantíssimi ac compatiéntis immaculátæ Matris tuæ institútum fuit, atque ut illo indúti hæc mystéria devótius récolant, bene✠dícere dignéris; ut hic fámulus tuus, qui (hæc fámula tua, quæ) ipsum gestáverit, te quoque, per tua mérita et intercessiónem beatíssimæ Vírginis Maríæ, indúere mereátur: Qui vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus Christ, who, deigning to put on the garment of our mortality, emptied Yourself, taking the form of a servant and becoming obedient even unto death on the Cross: we humbly implore the mercy of Your bounty, that You would ✠ bless this kind of garment, which was instituted in honor and memory of Your most sorrowful Passion and of Your most Sacred Heart, and also of the most loving and compassionate Heart of Your immaculate Mother, so that those clothed with it may recall these mysteries more devoutly; that this Your servant (handmaid) who wears it may, through Your merits and the intercession of the most blessed Virgin Mary, be worthy also to put on You: who live and reign for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Mox Sacerdos sacrum Scapulare aqua benedicta aspergit, et illud imponit, dicens: || The priest then sprinkles the holy scapular with holy water and places it on the person, saying:
t: Accipe, caríssime frater (caríssima soror), hunc hábitum benedíctum, ut véterem hóminem exútus (-a) novúmque indútus (-a) ipsum digne pérferas, et ad vitam pervénias sempitérnam. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Receive, dearest brother (sister), this blessed habit, that, having put off the old man and put on the new, you may wear it worthily and come to life everlasting. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Deinde subjungit: || Then he adds:
t: Et ego, ex facultáte mihi concéssa, recípio te (vos) ad participatiónem ómnium bonórum spirituálium, quæ per Sanctæ Sedis Apostólicæ privilégium huic sancto scapulári, in grátiam Congregatiónis Missiónis, concéssa sunt. In nómine Patris, et Fílii, ✠ et Spíritus Sancti. ℟. Amen. || And I, by the faculty granted to me, receive you into participation in all the spiritual goods which have been granted to this holy scapular by privilege of the Holy Apostolic See, as a favor to the Congregation of the Mission. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, ✠ and of the Holy Spirit. ℟. Amen.
r: Denique dicitur trina vice versiculus sequens: || Finally the following verse is said three times:
t: Te ergo quǽsumus, tuis fámulis súbveni, quos pretióso sánguine redemísti. || We therefore beseech You, help Your servants, whom You have redeemed with Your precious blood.

# blue-scapular | Benedictio et Impositio Scapularis Cærulei B. Mariæ Virg. Immaculatæ | Blessing and Investiture with the Blue Scapular of the Immaculate Conception | Sacramentals & Scapulars | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ (Cler. Regul. Theatinorum)
r: Propria Cler. Regul. Theatinorum. || Proper to the Theatine Clerics Regular.
r: Qui suscepturus est Scapulare, genuflectit; et Sacerdos superpelliceo et stola alba indutus dicit: || The one who is to receive the scapular kneels, and the priest, vested in surplice and white stole, says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Jesu Christe, qui tégumen nostræ mortalitátis indúere dignátus es, tuæ largitátis cleméntiam humíliter implorámus: ut hoc genus vestiménti, quod in honórem et memóriam Conceptiónis beátæ Maríæ Vírginis immaculátæ, nec non ut illo indúti exórent in hóminum pravórum morum reformatiónem, institútum fuit, bene✠dícere dignéris; ut hic fámulus tuus, qui eo usus fúerit (vel hæc fámula tua, quæ eo usa fúerit, vel hi fámuli tui, qui eo usi fúerint, vel hæ fámulæ tuæ, quæ eo usæ fúerint), eádem beáta María Vírgine intercedénte, te quoque indúere mereátur (-ántur): Qui vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus Christ, who deigned to put on the garment of our mortality, we humbly implore the mercy of Your bounty, that You would ✠ bless this kind of garment, which was instituted in honor and memory of the Conception of the immaculate Virgin Mary, and also so that those clothed with it might pray for the reformation of men of evil ways; that this Your servant who wears it (or this Your handmaid who wears it, or these Your servants, or these Your handmaids, who wear it), through the intercession of the same blessed Virgin Mary, may be worthy also to put on You: who live and reign for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Postea Sacerdos nihil dicendo aspergit Scapulare (vel Scapularia) aqua benedicta: deinde illud (vel illa) imponit, cuilibet dicens: || Then the priest, saying nothing, sprinkles the scapular (or scapulars) with holy water; then he places it (or them) on each one, saying:
t: Accipe, frater (soror), Scapuláre Conceptiónis beátæ Maríæ Vírginis immaculátæ: ut, ea intercedénte, véterem hóminem exútus (-a) et ab omni peccatórum inquinaménto mundátus (-a), ipsum pérferas sine mácula, et ad vitam pervénias sempitérnam. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Receive, brother (sister), the Scapular of the Conception of the immaculate Virgin Mary, that, through her intercession, having put off the old man and been cleansed from every stain of sin, you may wear it without stain and come to life everlasting. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Postea subjungit: || Then he adds:
t: Et ego, ex facultáte mihi concéssa, recípio te (vos) ad participatiónem bonórum ómnium spirituálium, quæ in Clericórum Regulárium Congregatióne ex grátia Dei fiunt, et quæ per Sanctæ Sedis Apostólicæ privilégium concéssa sunt. In nómine Patris, et Fílii, ✠ et Spíritus Sancti. ℟. Amen. || And I, by the faculty granted to me, receive you into participation in all the spiritual goods which by the grace of God are done in the Congregation of Clerics Regular, and which have been granted by privilege of the Holy Apostolic See. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, ✠ and of the Holy Spirit. ℟. Amen.
r: Denique dicat trina vice, flexis genibus, Orationem sequentem vulgari sermone una cum adscripto (vel adscriptis): || Finally, kneeling, he says the following prayer three times in the vernacular together with the person (or persons) enrolled:
t: Laudes ac grátiæ sint omni moménto sanctíssimo ac diviníssimo Sacraménto. || Praise and thanks be every moment to the most holy and most divine Sacrament.
t: Et benedícta sit sancta et immaculáta Concéptio beatíssimæ Vírginis Maríæ, Matris Dei. || And blessed be the holy and immaculate Conception of the most blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of God.
r: Hortetur fideles ut hæc elogia sæpe sæpius repetant ad indulgentias iis adnexas consequendas. (Decr. Pii VII, die 30 Junii 1818, Breve Leonis XIII, die 10 Sept. 1878, Decr. Pii X, die 10 Apr. 1913.) || He should exhort the faithful to repeat these praises again and again in order to gain the indulgences attached to them. (Decree of Pius VII, 30 June 1818; Brief of Leo XIII, 10 September 1878; Decree of Pius X, 10 April 1913.)

# servite-scapular | Benedictio et Impositio Scapularis Nigri Septem Dolorum B. M. V. | Blessing and Investiture with the Black Scapular of the Seven Sorrows of the Blessed Virgin Mary | Sacramentals & Scapulars | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ (Ord. Servorum B.M.V.)
r: Propria Ordinis Servorum B. M. V. || Proper to the Order of Servants of the Blessed Virgin Mary (Servites).
r: Suscepturus Habitum genuflectit; et Sacerdos, superpelliceo ac stola alba indutus, dicit: || The one who is to receive the habit kneels, and the priest, vested in surplice and white stole, says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Omnípotens sempitérne Deus, qui morte Unigéniti tui mundum collápsum restauráre dignátus es, ut nos a morte ætérna liberáres, et ad gáudia regni cæléstis perdúceres: réspice, quǽsumus, super hanc famíliam servórum tuórum, in nómine beatíssimæ Vírginis septem Dolóribus sáuciæ congregátam, de cujus grémio hic fámulus tuus (hæc fámula tua) esse cupit (vel hi fámuli tui esse cúpiunt); ut augeátur númerus tibi fidéliter serviéntium, et ómnibus sǽculi et carnis perturbatiónibus liberátus (liberáta vel liberáti) et a láqueis diáboli secúrus (secúra vel secúri) intercessióne ejúsdem beátæ Maríæ Vírginis, beatórum Augustíni et Philíppi ac septem nostrórum beatórum Patrum Ordinis Fundatórum, vera gáudia possídeat (-ant). Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Almighty, everlasting God, who by the death of Your Only-begotten deigned to restore the fallen world, that You might free us from eternal death and lead us to the joys of the heavenly kingdom: look down, we beseech You, upon this family of Your servants, gathered in the name of the most blessed Virgin pierced by seven Sorrows, of whose company this Your servant (handmaid) desires to be (or these Your servants desire to be); that the number of those who serve You faithfully may be increased, and that he (she, they), freed from all the disturbances of the world and the flesh and safe from the snares of the devil, through the intercession of the same blessed Virgin Mary, of the blessed Augustine and Philip, and of our seven blessed Fathers, Founders of the Order, may possess true joys. Through the same Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Conversus ad Habitum super Altare positum, Sacerdos dicit: || Turning toward the habit placed upon the altar, the priest says:
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Jesu Christe, qui tégumen nostræ mortalitátis indúere dignátus es, obsecrámus imménsam largitátis tuæ abundántiam: ut hoc genus vestimentórum, quod sancti Patres nostri, ad innocéntiæ humilitatísque indícium, in memóriam Septem Dolórum beátæ Vírginis Maríæ nos ferre sanxérunt, ita bene✠dícere dignéris; ut, qui (quæ) illis fúerit indútus (indúta vel fúerint indúti), córpore páriter et ánimo índuat (-ant) te Salvatórem nostrum: Qui vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus Christ, who deigned to put on the garment of our mortality, we implore the boundless abundance of Your bounty, that You would so ✠ bless this kind of garment, which our holy Fathers decreed that we should wear as a sign of innocence and humility, in memory of the Seven Sorrows of the blessed Virgin Mary, that whoever is clothed with it may put on You, our Savior, in body and soul alike: who live and reign for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Aspergit Habitum aqua benedicta, dicens: || He sprinkles the habit with holy water, saying:
t: Aspérges me, Dómine, hyssópo et mundábor: lavábis me, et super nivem dealbábor. || You will sprinkle me with hyssop, O Lord, and I shall be cleansed; You will wash me, and I shall be made whiter than snow.
r: Deinde benedicit coronam septem Dolorum B. Mariæ Virg., dicens: Orémus. Omnípotens et miséricors Deus, etc., ut infra (Benedictio Coronæ Septem Dolorum B. M. V.), et coronam aspergit aqua benedicta. || Then he blesses the chaplet of the Seven Sorrows of the Blessed Virgin Mary, saying: Let us pray. Almighty and merciful God, etc., as below (Blessing of the Chaplet of the Seven Sorrows), and sprinkles the chaplet with holy water.
r: Mox Sacerdos imponit Habitum cuilibet coram se genuflexo, dicens: || Then the priest places the habit on each one kneeling before him, saying:
t: Accipe, caríssime frater (caríssima soror), hábitum beátæ Maríæ Vírginis, singuláre signum servórum suórum, in memóriam septem Dolórum, quos ipsa in vita et morte Unigéniti Fílii sui sustínuit; ut ita indútus (-a), sub ejus patrocínio, perpétuo vivas. ℟. Amen. || Receive, dearest brother (sister), the habit of the blessed Virgin Mary, the special sign of her servants, in memory of the seven Sorrows which she endured in the life and death of her Only-begotten Son, that, so clothed, you may live for ever under her protection. ℟. Amen.
r: Postea coronam porrigit, dicens: || Afterward he hands over the chaplet, saying:
t: Accipe corónam beátæ Maríæ Vírginis in memóriam septem Dolórum suórum contéxtam, ut, dum eam ore laudáveris, ejus pœnas toto corde compatiáris. ℟. Amen. || Receive the chaplet of the blessed Virgin Mary, woven in memory of her seven Sorrows, that, while you praise her with your lips, you may share her sufferings with all your heart. ℟. Amen.
r: Deinde personam benedicit, dicens: || Then he blesses the person, saying:
t: Benedíctio Dei omnipoténtis, Patris, et Fílii, ✠ et Spíritus Sancti, descéndat super te (vos), et máneat semper. ℟. Amen. || May the blessing of almighty God, the Father, and the Son, ✠ and the Holy Spirit, come down upon you and remain for ever. ℟. Amen.

# sorrows-crown | Benedictio Coronæ Septem Dolorum B. M. V. | Blessing of the Chaplet of the Seven Sorrows of the Blessed Virgin Mary | Proper to Religious Orders | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ (Ord. Servorum B.M.V.)
r: Propria ejusdem Ordinis. || Proper to the same Order (Servites).
r: Sacerdos, superpelliceo ac stola alba indutus, dicit: || The priest, vested in surplice and white stole, says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
