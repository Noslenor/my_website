# stations-cross | Ritus Erigendi Stationes Viæ Crucis | Rite of Erecting the Stations of the Way of the Cross | Churches & Sacred Things | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ (Ord. Minorum), 1
r: Benedictiones propriæ nonnullorum Ordinum Regularium seu Congregationum religiosarum a Sacerdotibus Apostolicum indultum habentibus adhibendæ. || Blessings proper to certain Regular Orders or religious Congregations, to be used by priests who have an Apostolic indult.
r: Proprius Ordinis Minorum. || Proper to the Order of Friars Minor.
r: Sacerdos, superpelliceo et stola violacei coloris indutus, uno saltem Clerico adhibito, qui ei opportuno tempore porrigere possit vasculum aquæ benedictæ cum aspersorio, et thuribulum cum incensi navicula, ascendit Altare, ibique stans brevi sermone super præstantia et utilitate pii exercitii Viæ Crucis populum alloquitur. Deinde genuflexus in infimo gradu intonat sequentem Hymnum, quem chorus prosequitur: || The priest, vested in surplice and violet stole, and assisted by at least one cleric who can hand him at the proper time the vessel of holy water with the sprinkler, and the thurible with the incense boat, goes up to the altar and, standing there, addresses the people in a brief sermon on the excellence and usefulness of the devout exercise of the Way of the Cross. Then, kneeling on the lowest step, he intones the following hymn, which the choir continues:

Amen. || Almighty, everlasting God, who do not disapprove of the carving or painting of images of Your Saints, so that as often as we look upon them with our bodily eyes, so often we may meditate with the eyes of memory on their deeds and holiness for our imitation: deign, we beseech You, to ✠ bless and ✠ sanctify these images, made in honor and remembrance of Your only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ; and grant that whoever humbly strives to worship and honor Your only-begotten Son before them may, by His merits and through His favor, obtain from You grace in this present life and everlasting glory in the life to come.
