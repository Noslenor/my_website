# baptism-adults-rules | De Baptismo Adultorum | On the Baptism of Adults | Sacraments | Tit. II, cap. 3
r: 1. Adultus, nisi sciens et volens probeque instructus, ne baptizetur; insuper admonendus ut de peccatis suis doleat. || 1. An adult is not to be baptized unless he does so knowingly and willingly and has been properly instructed; he is moreover to be admonished to be sorry for his sins.
r: In mortis autem periculo, si nequeat in præcipuis fidei mysteriis diligentius instrui, satis est, ad Baptismum conferendum, ut aliquo modo ostendat se eisdem assentire serioque promittat se christianæ religionis mandata servaturum. || In danger of death, however, if he cannot be more fully instructed in the chief mysteries of the faith, it is enough, for Baptism to be conferred, that he show in some way that he assents to them and seriously promise that he will keep the commandments of the Christian religion.
r: Quod si Baptismum ne petere quidem queat, sed vel antea vel in præsenti statu manifestaverit aliquo probabili modo intentionem illum suscipiendi, baptizandus est sub condicione; si deinde convaluerit et dubium de valore Baptismi collati permaneat, sub condicione Baptismus rursus conferatur. || But if he cannot even ask for Baptism, yet either earlier or in his present state has shown in some probable way the intention of receiving it, he is to be baptized conditionally; if he afterward recovers and doubt remains about the validity of the Baptism conferred, Baptism is to be conferred again conditionally.
r: 2. Adultorum Baptismus, ubi commode fieri possit, ad loci Ordinarium deferatur, ut, si illi placuerit, ab eo vel ab ejus delegato sollemnius conferatur, alioquin Parochus ipse baptizet, stata cæremonia. || 2. The Baptism of adults, where this can conveniently be done, should be referred to the local Ordinary, so that, if he so wishes, it may be conferred more solemnly by him or by his delegate; otherwise the pastor himself baptizes, with the prescribed ceremonies.
r: 3. Decet autem hujusmodi Baptismum, ex Apostolico instituto, in pervigilio Paschatis vel Pentecostes sollemniter celebrari. || 3. It is fitting, according to apostolic tradition, that Baptism of this kind be celebrated solemnly on the vigil of Easter or of Pentecost.
r: 4. Quare si circa hæc tempora Catechumeni sint baptizandi, in ipsos dies, si nihil impediat, Baptismum differri convenit. || 4. Therefore, if catechumens are to be baptized around these times, it is fitting that their Baptism be deferred to those very days, if nothing prevents it.
r: 5. Verum si circa, seu post tempus Pentecostes aliqui conversi fuerint, qui ægre ferant suum Baptismum in longum tempus differri, et ad illud festinent, instructique, ac rite parati esse noscantur, citius baptizari possunt. || 5. But if some have been converted around or after the season of Pentecost who would take it hard that their Baptism be put off for a long time, and who are eager for it and are known to be instructed and duly prepared, they may be baptized sooner.
r: 6. Catechumenus instructus baptizetur in ecclesia, seu Baptisterio, patrinus ei assistat, et ipse Catechumenus ad Sacerdotis interrogationes respondeat, nisi mutus sit, aut omnino surdus, vel ignotæ linguæ: quo casu vel per patrinum, si illam intellegat, aut alium interpretem, vel nutu consensum explicet suum. || 6. The instructed catechumen is to be baptized in the church or baptistery; a sponsor is to assist him, and the catechumen himself is to answer the priest's questions, unless he is mute, or entirely deaf, or of an unknown tongue; in that case he is to express his consent either through the sponsor, if the sponsor understands that language, or through another interpreter, or by a sign.
r: 7. Tam Sacerdotem, qui adultos baptizaturus est, quam ipsos adultos, qui sani sint, decet esse jejunos. || 7. It is fitting that both the priest who is to baptize adults and the adults themselves, if they are in good health, be fasting.
r: Nisi graves urgentesque causæ obsint, adultus baptizatus statim Missæ sacrificio assistat et sacram communionem percipiat. || Unless grave and urgent reasons stand in the way, the adult who has been baptized should at once assist at the Sacrifice of the Mass and receive Holy Communion.
r: 8. Quare non post epulas, aut prandia, sed ante meridiem (nisi ex rationabili causa aliter faciendum sit) eorum Baptismus celebretur. || 8. Therefore their Baptism is to be celebrated not after meals or dinners, but before noon (unless for a reasonable cause it must be done otherwise).
r: 9. Amentes et furiosi ne baptizentur, nisi tales a nativitate vel ante adeptum rationis usum fuerint; et tunc baptizandi sunt ut infantes. || 9. The insane and the violently deranged are not to be baptized unless they have been such from birth or from before attaining the use of reason; in that case they are to be baptized as infants.
r: Si autem dilucida habeant intervalla, dum mentis compotes sunt, baptizentur, si velint. || If, however, they have lucid intervals, they may be baptized while they are of sound mind, if they so wish.
r: Baptizentur quoque, imminente periculo mortis, si, antequam insanirent, suscipiendi Baptismi desiderium ostenderint. || They are also to be baptized when in imminent danger of death, if before becoming insane they showed a desire to receive Baptism.
r: Qui lethargo aut phrenesi laborat, vigilans tantum et volens baptizetur; at si periculum mortis impendeat, et prius ille ostenderit suscipiendi Baptismi desiderium, baptizetur. || One who suffers from lethargy or delirium is to be baptized only while awake and willing; but if danger of death threatens and he has previously shown a desire to receive Baptism, he is to be baptized.
r: 10. Sacerdos diligenter curet, ut certior fiat de statu et condicione eorum, qui baptizari petunt, præsertim exterorum: de quibus facta diligenti inquisitione, num alias, ac rite sint baptizati, caveat, ne quis jam baptizatus, imperitia, vel errore, aut ad quæstum, vel ob aliam causam, fraude, dolove iterum baptizari velit. || 10. The priest should take diligent care to be well informed about the state and condition of those who ask to be baptized, especially strangers; having made careful inquiry whether they have already been validly baptized, he should take care that no one already baptized seeks to be baptized again, whether through ignorance or error, or for gain, or for some other reason, by fraud or deceit.
r: 11. Omnes autem, de quibus, re diligenter investigata, prudens dubium exsistat, num revera vel num valide baptizati fuerint, sub condicione iterum baptizentur. || 11. All those, however, concerning whom, after careful investigation, a prudent doubt remains whether they were in fact baptized, or whether validly, are to be baptized again conditionally.
r: 12. Hæretici vero ad Catholicam Ecclesiam venientes, in quorum Baptismo debita forma, aut materia servata non est, rite baptizandi sunt; sed prius errorum suorum pravitatem agnoscant et detestentur, et in fide Catholica diligenter instruantur: ubi vero debita forma et materia servata est, omissa tantum suppleantur, nisi rationabili de causa aliter loci Ordinario videatur. || 12. Heretics coming to the Catholic Church, in whose Baptism the proper form or matter was not observed, are to be duly baptized; but first they must acknowledge and detest the wickedness of their errors and be carefully instructed in the Catholic faith. Where, however, the proper form and matter were observed, only what was omitted is to be supplied, unless for a reasonable cause the local Ordinary judges otherwise.
r: 13. Ceterum legantur, et serventur ea, quæ supra de Baptismo in communi præscripta sunt, cap. 1. || 13. For the rest, what has been prescribed above concerning Baptism in general (chapter 1) is to be read and observed.

# baptism-adults | Ordo Baptismi Adultorum | The Rite of Baptism for Adults | Sacraments | Tit. II, cap. 4
r: 1. In primis Sacerdos, paratis his, quæ supra de observandis in administratione Sacramenti Baptismi parvulorum dicuntur, indutus superpelliceo et stola, vel etiam pluviali violacei coloris, cum suis Clericis accedit ad gradus Altaris, et, genibus flexis, pias mente ad Deum preces effundit, ut tantum Sacramentum digne valeat ministrare; et ad implorandum divinum auxilium, surgens, se signat, et, si temporis ratio ferat, dicit: || 1. First the priest, having prepared what is described above as to be observed in administering the Sacrament of Baptism to children, vested in surplice and stole, or also in a violet cope, goes with his clerics to the steps of the altar and, kneeling, pours out devout prayers to God in his heart, that he may worthily administer so great a Sacrament; then, to implore the divine help, he rises, signs himself, and, if time allows, says:
v: Deus, in adjutórium meum inténde. || O God, come to my assistance.
x: Dómine, ad adjuvándum me festína. || O Lord, make haste to help me.
v: Glória Patri. || Glory be to the Father.
x: Sicut erat. || As it was.
r: 2. Postea incipit, prosequentibus Clericis, || 2. Afterward he begins, the clerics continuing,
t: *Ant.* Effúndam super vos aquam mundam, et mundabímini ab ómnibus inquinaméntis vestris, dicit Dóminus. || *Ant.* I will pour out upon you clean water, and you shall be cleansed from all your defilements, says the Lord.

Then, while the godfather or godmother, or both (if both are admitted), with hand placed upon him, hold or touch the elect, man or woman, who bows with head uncovered and clothing loosened at the neck, the priest draws baptismal water from the font in a small vessel or pitcher, and with it, pouring three times over the head in the form of a cross, baptizes the elect in the name of the Most Holy Trinity, saying:
t: N., Ego te baptízo in nómine Pa✠tris, *(fundit primo)* et Fí✠lii, *(fundit secundo)* et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. *(fundit tertio)* || N., I baptize you in the name of the ✠ Father, *(he pours the first time)* and of the ✠ Son, *(he pours the second time)* and of the Holy ✠ Spirit. *(he pours the third time)*
r: 40. Si aqua, quæ ex capite baptizati defluit, non dilabatur in sacrarium Baptisterii, recipiatur in subjecta aliqua pelvi, et in illud postmodum projiciatur. || 40. If the water that flows from the head of the one baptized does not run off into the sacrarium of the baptistery, it is to be caught in some basin placed beneath and afterward poured into the sacrarium.
r: Cum plures sunt Electi, singillatim singuli interrogantur et baptizantur, ut supra. Si sint mares et feminæ, primum mares, deinde feminæ. || When there are several of the elect, they are questioned and baptized one by one, as above. If there are men and women, first the men, then the women.
r: Verum si probabiliter dubitetur, an Electus fuerit alias baptizatus, dicat Sacerdos: || But if there is a probable doubt whether the elect has already been baptized, the priest says:
t: N., Si non es baptizátus (-a), ego te baptízo in nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. || N., if you are not baptized, I baptize you in the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit.
r: 41. Deinde Sacerdos intingit pollicem dexterum in sacro Chrismate, et perungit verticem Electi (singulorum Electorum) in modum crucis, dicens: || 41. Then the priest dips his right thumb in the holy chrism and anoints the crown of the head of the elect (of each of the elect) in the form of a cross, saying:
t: Deus omnípotens, Pater Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, qui te regenerávit ex aqua et Spíritu Sancto, quique dedit tibi remissiónem ómnium peccatórum, *(hic inungit)* ipse te líniat ✠ Chrísmate salútis in eódem Christo Jesu, Dómino nostro, in vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || May almighty God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has given you new birth by water and the Holy Spirit and has granted you the forgiveness of all your sins, *(here he anoints)* Himself anoint you ✠ with the chrism of salvation in the same Christ Jesus our Lord unto life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
v: Pax tecum. || Peace be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
r: 42. Tunc bombacio vel re simili pollicem tergit, et imponit capiti Electi chrismale, seu candidum linteolum, et dat illi vestem candidam, dicens (singulis, si plures): || 42. Then he wipes his thumb with cotton or something similar, places on the head of the elect the chrismal, that is, a white linen cloth, and gives him a white garment, saying (to each, if there are several):
t: Accipe vestem cándidam, quam pérferas immaculátam ante tribúnal Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, ut hábeas vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || Receive this white garment, and carry it without stain before the judgment seat of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you may have life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
r: 43. Et Electus deponit priores vestes, et induitur novis albi coloris, vel saltem exteriore candida, quam a Sacerdote accepit. || 43. And the elect lays aside his former clothes and is clothed in new white ones, or at least in the white outer garment that he received from the priest.
r: Postea dat ei Sacerdos cereum, seu candelam accensam in manu dextera, dicens (singulis, si plures): || Then the priest gives him a wax taper, that is, a lighted candle, in his right hand, saying (to each, if there are several):
t: Accipe lámpadem ardéntem, et irreprehensíbilis custódi Baptísmum tuum: serva Dei mandáta, ut, cum Dóminus vénerit ad núptias, possis occúrrere ei una cum ómnibus Sanctis in aula cælésti, et vivas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Receive this burning light, and keep your Baptism so as to be without blame. Keep the commandments of God, so that when the Lord comes to the wedding feast, you may go out to meet Him together with all the Saints in the heavenly court, and may live for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: 44. Ipse vero Neophytus eundem cereum accensum manu tenet usque in finem, præterquam dum confirmatur. || 44. The newly baptized holds this same lighted taper in his hand until the end, except while he is being confirmed.
r: 45. In ecclesiis autem, ubi Baptismus fit per immersionem, sive totius corporis, sive capitis tantum, Sacerdos accipit Electum per brachia prope humeros, et superiore parte corporis nudatum, reliqua honeste contectum, ter illum, vel caput ejus immergendo, et toties elevando, baptizat sub trina immersione, sanctam Trinitatem semel tantum sic invocando: N., Ego te baptízo in nómine Pa✠tris, *immergit primo,* et Fí✠lii, *immergit iterum,* et Spíritus ✠ Sancti, *immergit tertio;* patrino, vel matrina, vel utroque eum tenente, vel tangente. || 45. In churches, however, where Baptism is given by immersion, whether of the whole body or of the head only, the priest takes the elect by the arms near the shoulders, the upper part of the body uncovered and the rest modestly covered, and, immersing him or his head three times and as often raising him, baptizes him by a threefold immersion, invoking the Holy Trinity only once, thus: N., I baptize you in the name of the ✠ Father, *he immerses the first time,* and of the ✠ Son, *he immerses again,* and of the Holy ✠ Spirit, *he immerses the third time;* the godfather or godmother, or both, holding or touching him.
r: 46. Et cum Electus surrexerit de Fonte, patrinus vel matrina cum linteo in manibus suscipit eum de manu Sacerdotis: et Sacerdos, intincto pollice dextero in sacro Chrismate, illum in vertice in modum crucis perungit, dicens: Deus omnípotens, etc., ut supra n. 41. || 46. And when the elect has come up out of the font, the godfather or godmother, with a linen cloth in hand, receives him from the hand of the priest; and the priest, having dipped his right thumb in the holy chrism, anoints him on the crown of the head in the form of a cross, saying: May almighty God, etc., as above at no. 41.
r: 47. Deinde Sacerdos imponit capiti ejus linteolum, seu chrismale, et dat ei vestem candidam, dicens: Accipe vestem cándidam, etc., ut supra. || 47. Then the priest places on his head the linen cloth, or chrismal, and gives him the white garment, saying: Receive this white garment, etc., as above.
r: 48. Et statim prædicto linteo abstergitur, et in loco semoto vestitur novis et albis vestibus, et exteriore candida, quam accepit. || 48. And at once he is dried with the aforesaid linen cloth and, in a place apart, is clothed in new white garments and in the white outer garment he received.
r: 49. Postea dat ei Sacerdos candelam accensam in dextera manu, dicens: Accipe lámpadem, etc., ut supra. || 49. Afterward the priest gives him a lighted candle in his right hand, saying: Receive this burning light, etc., as above.
r: Eodem modo, si plures sint, fit pro singulis. || In the same way, if there are several, it is done for each.
r: 50. Postea Sacerdos dicit (in plurali pro pluribus): || 50. Then the priest says (in the plural for several):
t: N., Vade (Ite) in pace, et Dóminus sit tecum (vobíscum). ℟. Amen. || N., go in peace, and the Lord be with you. ℟. Amen.
h: Rubricæ || Rubrics
r: 51. Ritus superius descriptus servandus est etiam a Diacono Baptismum sollemnem ministrante, qui tamen sale et aqua utetur a Sacerdote ad hunc usum rite prius benedictis. || 51. The rite described above is to be observed also by a deacon administering solemn Baptism; he shall, however, use salt and water previously blessed for this purpose by a priest.
r: 52. Si adsit Episcopus, qui id legitime præstare possit, ab eo Neophyti Sacramento Confirmationis initiantur. || 52. If a bishop is present who can lawfully do so, the newly baptized are initiated by him with the Sacrament of Confirmation.
r: Deinde si hora sit congruens, celebratur Missa, cui Neophyti intersunt, et sanctissimam Eucharistiam devote suscipiunt. || Then, if the hour is suitable, Mass is celebrated, at which the newly baptized are present and devoutly receive the Most Holy Eucharist.
r: 53. Si vero ob baptizandorum multitudinem, ut quandoque contingit, in singulorum Baptismo præscripti ritus ad Baptismi essentiam non pertinentes adhiberi non possint, tunc vel pluribus simul adhibeantur, vel urgente necessitate omittantur. || 53. But if, because of the great number to be baptized, as sometimes happens, the prescribed rites not belonging to the essence of Baptism cannot be carried out at the Baptism of each one, then they are either to be carried out for several at once or, under urgent necessity, omitted.
