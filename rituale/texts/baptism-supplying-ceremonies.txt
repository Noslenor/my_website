// Rite of Supplying the Ceremonies Omitted in the Baptism of an Infant
// ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# supply-infant | Ordo Supplendi Omissa super Infantem Baptizatum | Rite of Supplying the Ceremonies Omitted in the Baptism of an Infant | Sacraments | Tit. II, cap. 5
r: 1. Cum, urgente mortis periculo, vel alia cogente necessitate, parvulus, sacris precibus ac cæremoniis prætermissis, fuerit baptizatus, ubi convaluerit, vel cessaverit periculum, et ad ecclesiam delatus fuerit, omissa omnia suppleantur. || 1. When, because of imminent danger of death or some other necessity, a little child has been baptized with the sacred prayers and ceremonies left out, then, once the child has recovered or the danger has passed, and the child has been brought to the church, everything that was omitted is to be supplied.
r: 2. Sacerdos itaque, lotis manibus, superpelliceo et stola violacea indutus, accedat: Clericum unum, seu plures, si potest, secum adhibeat, superpelliceo pariter indutos, qui sibi ministrent. || 2. The priest, therefore, having washed his hands and vested in surplice and violet stole, approaches; if possible he takes with him one or more clerics, likewise vested in surplices, to assist him.
r: 3. Ita paratus accedat ad limen ecclesiæ, extra quam exspectant, qui infantem (vel infantes) detulerunt. Tum illum proprio nomine eidem imposito appellat, et dicit (si plures sint, singulariter singulis): || 3. Thus vested, he goes to the threshold of the church, outside of which those who have brought the child (or children) are waiting. Then he calls the child by the name given to it, and says (if there are several, to each individually):
t: 4. N., Quid petis ab Ecclésia Dei? || 4. N., what do you ask of the Church of God?
t: *Patrinus respondet:* Fidem. || *The godparent answers:* Faith.
t: *Sacerdos:* Fides, quid tibi præstat? || *Priest:* What does faith offer you?
t: *Patrinus respondet:* Vitam ætérnam. || *The godparent answers:* Eternal life.
r: 5. Sacerdos (etiam singulariter singulis): || 5. The priest (again to each individually):
t: Si ígitur vis ad vitam íngredi, serva mandáta. Díliges Dóminum Deum tuum ex toto corde tuo, et ex tota ánima tua, et ex tota mente tua, et próximum tuum sicut teípsum. || If, then, you wish to enter into life, keep the commandments. You shall love the Lord your God with your whole heart, and with your whole soul, and with your whole mind, and your neighbor as yourself.
r: 6. Deinde ter exsufflat leniter in faciem infantis, et dicit semel (singulariter singulis): || 6. Then he breathes gently three times on the child's face, and says once (to each individually):
t: Exi ab eo (ea), immúnde spíritus, et da locum Spirítui Sancto Paráclito. || Go out from him (her), unclean spirit, and give place to the Holy Spirit, the Paraclete.
r: 7. Postea pollice facit signum crucis in fronte, et in pectore infantis, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 7. Then with his thumb he makes the sign of the cross on the forehead and on the breast of the child, saying (to each individually):
t: Accipe signum Crucis tam in fron✠te, quam in cor✠de, sume fidem cæléstium præceptórum: et talis esto móribus, ut templum Dei jam esse possis. || Receive the sign of the Cross both on your fore✠head and on your ✠heart; take hold of the faith of the heavenly precepts, and be such in your conduct that you may now be a temple of God.
t: Orémus. *(In plurali pro pluribus.)* || Let us pray. *(In the plural for several.)*
t: Preces nostras, quǽsumus, Dómine, cleménter exáudi, et hunc eléctum tuum N. (hanc eléctam tuam N.) Crucis Domínicæ impressióne signátum (-am) perpétua virtúte custódi: ut, magnitúdinis glóriæ tuæ rudiménta servans, per custódiam mandatórum tuórum ad regeneratiónis glóriam perveníre mereátur. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Mercifully hear our prayers, we beseech You, O Lord, and with Your unfailing power guard this Your chosen one, N., marked with the sign of the Lord's Cross; that, holding fast to these first lessons in the greatness of Your glory, he (she) may, by keeping Your commandments, be worthy to attain the glory of rebirth. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: 8. Deinde imponit manum super caput infantis (singulorum infantium), et postea manum extensam tenens dicit: || 8. Then he lays his hand on the head of the child (of each child), and afterward, holding his hand extended, he says:
t: Orémus. *(In plurali pro pluribus.)* || Let us pray. *(In the plural for several.)*
t: Omnípotens sempitérne Deus, Pater Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, respícere dignáre super hunc fámulum tuum N., quem (hanc fámulam tuam N., quam) dudum ad rudiménta fídei vocáre dignátus es: omnem cæcitátem cordis ab eo (ea) expélle: disrúmpe omnes láqueos sátanæ, quibus fúerat colligátus (-a): áperi ei, Dómine, jánuam pietátis tuæ, ut, signo sapiéntiæ tuæ imbútus (-a), ómnium cupiditátum fœtóribus cáreat, et ad suávem odórem præceptórum tuórum lætus (-a) tibi in Ecclésia tua desérviat, et profíciat de die in diem, ut idóneus (-a) sit frui grátia Baptísmi tui, quem suscépit, salis percépta medicína. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Almighty, everlasting God, Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, graciously look upon this Your servant N., whom You have already deigned to call to the first lessons of the faith. Drive out from him (her) all blindness of heart; break all the snares of Satan with which he (she) was bound. Open to him (her), O Lord, the gate of Your mercy, so that, filled with the sign of Your wisdom, he (she) may be free from the foulness of all evil desires, and in the sweet fragrance of Your commandments may joyfully serve You in Your Church and grow from day to day, so that, having received the medicine of salt, he (she) may be fit to enjoy the grace of Your Baptism, which he (she) has received. Through the same Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: 9. Deinde Sacerdos benedicit sal, quod semel benedictum, alias ad eundem usum deservire potest. || 9. Then the priest blesses the salt, which, once blessed, may be used again for the same purpose.
h: Benedictio salis || Blessing of the salt
t: Exorcízo te, creatúra salis, in nómine Dei ✠ Patris omnipoténtis, et in caritáte Dómini nostri Jesu ✠ Christi, et in virtúte Spíritus ✠ Sancti. Exorcízo te per Deum ✠ vivum, per Deum ✠ verum, per Deum ✠ sanctum, per Deum ✠, qui te ad tutélam humáni géneris procreávit, et pópulo veniénti ad credulitátem per servos suos consecrári præcépit, ut in nómine sanctæ Trinitátis efficiáris salutáre sacraméntum ad effugándum inimícum. Proínde rogámus te, Dómine Deus noster, ut hanc creatúram salis sanctificándo sanctí✠fices, et benedicéndo bene✠dícas, ut fiat ómnibus accipiéntibus perfécta medicína, pérmanens in viscéribus eórum, in nómine ejúsdem Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos, et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || I exorcise you, creature of salt, in the name of God the ✠ Father almighty, and in the love of our Lord Jesus ✠ Christ, and in the power of the Holy ✠ Spirit. I exorcise you by the ✠ living God, by the ✠ true God, by the ✠ holy God, by ✠ God who created you for the protection of the human race and commanded that you be consecrated by His servants for the people coming to the faith, so that in the name of the Holy Trinity you may become a saving sacrament to put the enemy to flight. Therefore we ask You, O Lord our God, that by sanctifying You may sanc✠tify, and by blessing You may ✠ bless this creature of salt, so that it may become for all who receive it a perfect medicine, abiding in their inmost being; in the name of the same Jesus Christ our Lord, who is to come to judge the living and the dead, and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
r: 10. Deinde immittit modicum salis benedicti in os infantis, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 10. Then he puts a little of the blessed salt into the child's mouth, saying (to each individually):
t: N., Accipe sal sapiéntiæ: propitiátio sit tibi in vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || N., receive the salt of wisdom; may it be for you a pledge of mercy unto life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
t: *Sacerdos:* Pax tecum. ℟. Et cum spíritu tuo. || *Priest:* Peace be with you. ℟. And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. *(In plurali pro pluribus.)* || Let us pray. *(In the plural for several.)*
t: Deus patrum nostrórum, Deus univérsæ cónditor veritátis, te súpplices exorámus, ut hunc fámulum tuum N. (hanc fámulam tuam N.) respícere dignéris propítius, et hoc pábulum salis gustántem, non diútius esuríre permíttas, quo minus cibo expleátur cælésti, quátenus sit semper spíritu fervens, spe gaudens, tuo semper nómini sérviens: et quem (quam) ad novæ regeneratiónis lavácrum perduxísti, quǽsumus, Dómine, ut cum fidélibus tuis promissiónum tuárum ætérna prǽmia cónsequi mereátur. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || O God of our fathers, God, the author of all truth, we humbly beseech You graciously to look upon this Your servant N., and as he (she) tastes this nourishment of salt, do not let him (her) hunger any longer, but fill him (her) with heavenly food, so that he (she) may be ever fervent in spirit, rejoicing in hope, always serving Your name. And since You have brought him (her) to the font of the new birth, grant, we beseech You, O Lord, that with Your faithful he (she) may be worthy to obtain the eternal rewards of Your promises. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
t: Exorcízo te, immúnde spíritus, in nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti, ut éxeas, et recédas ab hoc fámulo (hac fámula) Dei N.: Ipse enim tibi ímperat, maledícte damnáte, qui pédibus super mare ambulávit, et Petro mergénti déxteram porréxit. || I exorcise you, unclean spirit, in the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit, that you go out and depart from this servant of God, N. For He commands you, accursed and damned one, who walked upon the sea and stretched out His right hand to Peter as he was sinking.
t: Ergo, maledícte diábole, recognósce senténtiam tuam, et da honórem Deo vivo et vero, da honórem Jesu Christo, Fílio ejus, et Spirítui Sancto, et recéde ab hoc fámulo (hac fámula) Dei N., quia istum (istam) sibi Deus et Dóminus noster Jesus Christus ad suam sanctam grátiam, et benedictiónem, fontémque Baptísmatis vocáre dignátus est. || Therefore, accursed devil, acknowledge your sentence, and give honor to the living and true God; give honor to Jesus Christ, His Son, and to the Holy Spirit; and depart from this servant of God, N., for our God and Lord Jesus Christ has deigned to call him (her) to His holy grace and blessing and to the font of Baptism.
r: 11. Hic pollice in fronte signat infantem, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 11. Here with his thumb he signs the child on the forehead, saying (to each individually):
t: Et hoc signum sanctæ Cru✠cis, quod nos fronti ejus damus, tu, maledícte diábole, numquam áudeas violáre. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || And this sign of the holy ✠ Cross, which we place upon his (her) forehead, do you, accursed devil, never dare to violate. Through the same Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: 12. Mox imponit manum super caput infantis (singulorum infantium), et postea manum extensam tenens dicit: || 12. Next he lays his hand on the head of the child (of each child), and afterward, holding his hand extended, he says:
t: Orémus. *(In plurali pro pluribus.)* || Let us pray. *(In the plural for several.)*
t: Ætérnam ac justíssimam pietátem tuam déprecor, Dómine sancte, Pater omnípotens, ætérne Deus, auctor lúminis et veritátis, super hunc fámulum tuum N. (hanc fámulam tuam N.), ut dignéris eum (eam) illumináre lúmine intellegéntiæ tuæ: munda eum (eam), et sanctífica: da ei sciéntiam veram, ut dignus (-a) sit frui grátia Baptísmi tui, quem suscépit: téneat firmam spem, consílium rectum, doctrínam sanctam, ut aptus (-a) sit ad retinéndam grátiam Baptísmi tui. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || I implore Your eternal and most just mercy, holy Lord, Father almighty, everlasting God, author of light and truth, upon this Your servant N., that You would deign to enlighten him (her) with the light of Your understanding. Cleanse him (her) and sanctify him (her); give him (her) true knowledge, so that he (she) may be worthy to enjoy the grace of Your Baptism, which he (she) has received; may he (she) hold firm hope, right counsel, and holy doctrine, so as to be fit to keep the grace of Your Baptism. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: 13. Postea Sacerdos imponit extremam partem stolæ pendentem a suo humero sinistro super primum infantem, et introducit eum (quem sequuntur ceteri) in ecclesiam, dicens (in plurali pro pluribus): || 13. Then the priest places the end of the stole hanging from his left shoulder upon the first child and leads him (the others following) into the church, saying (in the plural for several):
t: N., Ingrédere in templum Dei, ut hábeas partem cum Christo in vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || N., enter into the temple of God, that you may have part with Christ unto life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
r: 14. Cum fuerint ecclesiam ingressi, Sacerdos procedens ad Fontem, cum susceptoribus conjunctim clara voce dicit: || 14. When they have entered the church, the priest, proceeding to the font, says aloud together with the sponsors:
t: Credo in Deum, Patrem omnipoténtem, Creatórem cæli et terræ. Et in Jesum Christum, Fílium ejus únicum, Dóminum nostrum: qui concéptus est de Spíritu Sancto, natus ex María Vírgine, passus sub Póntio Piláto, crucifíxus, mórtuus, et sepúltus: descéndit ad ínferos; tértia die resurréxit a mórtuis; ascéndit ad cælos; sedet ad déxteram Dei Patris omnipoténtis: inde ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos. Credo in Spíritum Sanctum, sanctam Ecclésiam cathólicam, Sanctórum communiónem, remissiónem peccatórum, carnis resurrectiónem, vitam ætérnam. Amen. || I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth; and in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried. He descended into hell; on the third day He rose again from the dead; He ascended into heaven and sits at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from there He will come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic Church, the communion of Saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.
t: Pater noster, qui es in cælis, sanctificétur nomen tuum. Advéniat regnum tuum. Fiat volúntas tua, sicut in cælo et in terra. Panem nostrum cotidiánum da nobis hódie. Et dimítte nobis débita nostra, sicut et nos dimíttimus debitóribus nostris. Et ne nos indúcas in tentatiónem: sed líbera nos a malo. Amen. || Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be Thy name; Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.
r: 15. Ac deinde, antequam accedat ad Baptisterium, versis renibus ostio cancellorum Baptisterii, dicit (in plurali pro pluribus): || 15. And then, before he approaches the baptistery, with his back turned to the gate of the baptistery railing, he says (in the plural for several):
h: Exorcismus || Exorcism
t: Exorcízo te, omnis spíritus immúnde, in nómine Dei ✠ Patris omnipoténtis, et in nómine Jesu ✠ Christi Fílii ejus, Dómini et Júdicis nostri, et in virtúte Spíritus ✠ Sancti, ut discédas ab hoc plásmate Dei N., quod Dóminus noster ad templum sanctum suum vocáre dignátus est, ut fíeret templum Dei vivi, et Spíritus Sanctus hábitet in eo. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum, qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos, et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || I exorcise you, every unclean spirit, in the name of God the ✠ Father almighty, and in the name of Jesus ✠ Christ, His Son, our Lord and Judge, and in the power of the Holy ✠ Spirit, that you depart from this creature of God, N., whom our Lord has deigned to call to His holy temple, that he (she) might become a temple of the living God and the Holy Spirit might dwell in him (her). Through the same Christ our Lord, who is to come to judge the living and the dead, and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
r: 16. Postea Sacerdos pollice accipit de saliva oris sui, et tangit aures et nares infantis: tangendo vero aurem dexteram et sinistram dicit (singulariter singulis): || 16. Then the priest takes a little spittle from his mouth with his thumb and touches the ears and nostrils of the child; touching the right and the left ear, he says (to each individually):
t: Ephphetha, quod est, Adaperíre. || Ephphetha, that is, Be opened.
r: Deinde tangit nares, dicens: || Then he touches the nostrils, saying:
t: In odórem suavitátis. Tu autem effugáre, diábole; appropinquábit enim judícium Dei. || Unto the odor of sweetness. But you, devil, take flight; for the judgment of God draws near.
r: 17. Postea interrogat baptizatum nominatim, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 17. Then he questions the baptized child by name, saying (to each individually):
t: N., Abrenúntias sátanæ? || N., do you renounce Satan?
t: *Respondet patrinus:* Abrenúntio. || *The godparent answers:* I do renounce him.
t: *Sacerdos:* Et ómnibus opéribus ejus? || *Priest:* And all his works?
t: *Patrinus:* Abrenúntio. || *Godparent:* I do renounce them.
t: *Sacerdos:* Et ómnibus pompis ejus? || *Priest:* And all his empty show?
t: *Patrinus:* Abrenúntio. || *Godparent:* I do renounce them.
r: 18. Deinde Sacerdos intingit pollicem in oleo Catechumenorum, et infantem ungit in pectore, et inter scapulas in modum crucis, dicens semel (singulariter singulis): || 18. Then the priest dips his thumb in the oil of catechumens and anoints the child on the breast and between the shoulders in the form of a cross, saying once (to each individually):
t: Ego te línio ✠ óleo salútis in Christo Jesu, Dómino nostro, ut hábeas vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || I anoint you ✠ with the oil of salvation in Christ Jesus our Lord, that you may have life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
r: 19. Subinde pollicem, et inuncta loca abstergit bombacio, vel re simili. || 19. He then wipes his thumb and the anointed places with cotton or something similar.
r: 20. Stans ibidem extra cancellos, deponit stolam violaceam, et sumit stolam albi coloris. Tunc ingreditur Baptisterium, in quod intrat etiam patrinus cum infante. Sacerdos ad Fontem interrogat, expresso nomine, baptizatum (singulos baptizatos), patrino respondente: || 20. Standing there outside the railing, he lays aside the violet stole and takes a white one. He then enters the baptistery, into which the godparent also enters with the child. At the font the priest questions the baptized child (each of the baptized), calling him by name, the godparent answering:
t: N., Credis in Deum Patrem omnipoténtem, Creatórem cæli et terræ? ℟. Credo. || N., do you believe in God the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth? ℟. I do believe.
t: Credis in Jesum Christum, Fílium ejus únicum, Dóminum nostrum, natum, et passum? ℟. Credo. || Do you believe in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord, who was born and who suffered? ℟. I do believe.
t: Credis et in Spíritum Sanctum, sanctam Ecclésiam cathólicam, Sanctórum communiónem, remissiónem peccatórum, carnis resurrectiónem, et vitam ætérnam? ℟. Credo. || Do you also believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic Church, the communion of Saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting? ℟. I do believe.
r: 21. Sacerdos intingit pollicem in sacro Chrismate, et ungit infantem (quem patrinus vel matrina, vel uterque simul tenet) in summitate capitis in modum crucis, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 21. The priest dips his thumb in the holy chrism and anoints the child (whom the godfather or godmother, or both together, are holding) on the crown of the head in the form of a cross, saying (to each individually):
t: Deus omnípotens, Pater Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, qui te regenerávit ex aqua et Spíritu Sancto, quique dedit tibi remissiónem ómnium peccatórum, *(hic inungit)* ipse te líniat ✠ Chrísmate salútis in eódem Christo Jesu, Dómino nostro, in vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || May almighty God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has given you new birth by water and the Holy Spirit and has granted you the forgiveness of all your sins, *(here he anoints)* Himself anoint you ✠ with the chrism of salvation in the same Christ Jesus our Lord unto life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
t: *Sacerdos:* Pax tibi. ℟. Et cum spíritu tuo. || *Priest:* Peace be with you. ℟. And with your spirit.
r: 22. Tum bombacio, vel re simili, abstergit pollicem, et locum inunctum, et imponit capiti infantis linteolum candidum loco vestis albæ, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 22. Then with cotton or something similar he wipes his thumb and the anointed place, and lays a white linen cloth on the child's head in place of the white garment, saying (to each individually):
t: Accipe vestem cándidam, quam pérferas immaculátam ante tribúnal Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, ut hábeas vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || Receive this white garment, and carry it without stain before the judgment seat of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you may have life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
r: 23. Postea dat ei, vel patrino, candelam accensam, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 23. Then he gives to the child, or to the godparent, a lighted candle, saying (to each individually):
t: Accipe lámpadem ardéntem, et irreprehensíbilis custódi Baptísmum tuum: serva Dei mandáta, ut, cum Dóminus vénerit ad núptias, possis occúrrere ei una cum ómnibus Sanctis in aula cælésti, et vivas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Receive this burning light, and keep your Baptism so as to be without blame. Keep the commandments of God, so that when the Lord comes to the wedding feast, you may go out to meet Him together with all the Saints in the heavenly court, and may live for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: 24. Postremo dicit (in plurali pro pluribus): || 24. Lastly he says (in the plural for several):
t: N., Vade (Ite) in pace, et Dóminus sit tecum (vobíscum). ℟. Amen. || N., go in peace, and the Lord be with you. ℟. Amen.
r: 25. Ritus superius descriptus servandus est etiam a Diacono Baptismi cæremonias supplente, qui tamen sale utetur a Sacerdote ad hunc usum rite prius benedicto. || 25. The rite described above is to be observed also by a deacon supplying the ceremonies of Baptism; he shall, however, use salt previously blessed for this purpose by a priest.
r: 26. Si vero sint plures infantes, masculi seu feminæ, in catechismo masculi statuantur ad dexteram, feminæ vero ad sinistram; et omnia pariter dicantur, ut supra, in proprio genere, et numero plurali. Verum prima nominis interrogatio, exsufflatio, crucis impressio, seu signatio, tactus aurium et narium cum saliva, abrenuntiationis interrogatio, unctio Olei Catechumenorum, interrogatio de Fide, seu Symbolo, inunctio Chrismatis, candidæ vestis impositio, atque candelæ accensæ traditio singulariter singulis, et primum masculis, deinde feminis fieri debent. || 26. If there are several children, boys or girls, the boys are placed on the right during the catechism and the girls on the left; and everything is said as above in the proper gender and in the plural. r: Sed pro majori commoditate adnotatum est propriis locis in rubricis parenthesi notatis; ita ut, quando dicitur in tali rubrica singulariter singulis, intellegatur, quod illa verba, vel actiones, usque ad aliam rubricam dici, vel fieri debent singulariter singulis, et primum masculis, deinde feminis, mutato solum genere, ut dictum est supra. When a similar rubric says "in the plural for several," the words up to the next rubric are said once, but in the plural for all, changing only the gender if all are girls.

He descended into hell; on the third day He rose again from the dead; He ascended into heaven and sits at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from there He will come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic Church, the communion of Saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
t: Pater noster, qui es in cælis, sanctificétur nomen tuum. Advéniat regnum tuum. Fiat volúntas tua, sicut in cælo, et in terra. Panem nostrum cotidiánum da nobis hódie. Et dimítte nobis débita nostra, sicut et nos dimíttimus debitóribus nostris. Et ne nos indúcas in tentatiónem: sed líbera nos a malo. Amen. || Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be Thy name; Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.
r: 31. Tunc Sacerdos, versis renibus ostio cancellorum Baptisterii, rursus imponit manum super caput Electi (singulorum Electorum), et postea manum extensam tenens dicit: || 31. Then the priest, with his back turned to the gate of the baptistery railing, again lays his hand on the head of the Elect (of each of the Elect), and afterward, holding his hand extended, he says:
h: Exorcismus *(In plurali pro pluribus.)* || Exorcism *(In the plural for several.)*
t: Nec te latet, sátana, imminére tibi pœnas, imminére tibi torménta, imminére tibi diem judícii, diem supplícii sempitérni, diem, qui ventúrus est velut clíbanus ardens, in quo tibi, atque univérsis ángelis tuis præparátus sempitérnus erit intéritus. Proínde, damnáte, atque damnánde, da honórem Deo vivo et vero, da honórem Jesu Christo, Fílio ejus, da honórem Spirítui Sancto Paráclito, in cujus nómine, atque virtúte præcípio tibi, quicúmque es, spíritus immúnde, ut éxeas, et recédas ab hoc fámulo Dei N., quem (hac fámula Dei N., quam) hódie idem Deus et Dóminus noster Jesus Christus ad suam sanctam grátiam et benedictiónem, fontémque Baptísmatis dono vocáre dignátus est, ut fíeret ejus templum per aquam regeneratiónis in remissiónem ómnium peccatórum. In nómine ejúsdem Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos, et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || You are not unaware, Satan, that punishments threaten you, that torments threaten you, that the day of judgment threatens you, the day of everlasting punishment, the day that is coming like a burning furnace, in which everlasting destruction will be prepared for you and all your angels. Therefore, you who are damned and deserve to be damned, give honor to the living and true God; give honor to Jesus Christ, His Son; give honor to the Holy Spirit, the Paraclete, in whose name and power I command you, whoever you are, unclean spirit, to go out and depart from this servant of God, N., whom today that same God and our Lord Jesus Christ has deigned to call by gift to His holy grace and blessing and to the font of Baptism, that he (she) might become His temple through the water of rebirth for the forgiveness of all sins. In the name of the same Lord Jesus Christ, who is to come to judge the living and the dead, and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
r: 32. Postea Sacerdos pollice accipit de saliva oris sui, et tangit aures et nares Electi (singulorum Electorum); tangendo vero aurem dexteram et sinistram, dicit: || 32. Then the priest takes a little spittle from his mouth with his thumb and touches the ears and nostrils of the Elect (of each of the Elect); touching the right and the left ear, he says:
t: Ephphetha, quod est, Adaperíre. || Ephphetha, that is, Be opened.
r: Deinde, tangendo nares, dicit: || Then, touching the nostrils, he says:
t: In odórem suavitátis. Tu autem effugáre, diábole; appropinquábit enim judícium Dei. || Unto the odor of sweetness. But you, devil, take flight; for the judgment of God draws near.
r: 33. Deinde interrogat Electum (singulos, si plures): || 33. Then he questions the Elect (each one, if there are several):
t: Quis vocáris? || What is your name?
t: *Et ipse respondet:* N. || *And he answers:* N.
t: *Interrogat:* N., Abrenúntias sátanæ? ℟. Abrenúntio. || *He asks:* N., do you renounce Satan? ℟. I do renounce him.
t: *Interrogat:* Et ómnibus opéribus ejus? ℟. Abrenúntio. || *He asks:* And all his works? ℟. I do renounce them.
t: *Interrogat:* Et ómnibus pompis ejus? ℟. Abrenúntio. || *He asks:* And all his empty show? ℟. I do renounce them.
r: 34. Tunc Sacerdos intingit pollicem dexteræ manus in Oleo sancto Catechumenorum, et inungit Electum (singulos Electos) primum in pectore, deinde inter scapulas in modum crucis, dicens (singulariter singulis): || 34. Then the priest dips the thumb of his right hand in the holy oil of catechumens and anoints the Elect (each of the Elect), first on the breast, then between the shoulders, in the form of a cross, saying (to each individually):
t: Ego te línio ✠ Oleo salútis in Christo Jesu, Dómino nostro, in vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || I anoint you ✠ with the oil of salvation in Christ Jesus our Lord, unto life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
v: Pax tibi. || Peace be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
r: 35. Mox bombacio, vel re simili, tergit pollicem et loca inuncta, et subjungit, dicens (singulis, si plures): || 35. Next, with cotton or something similar, he wipes his thumb and the anointed places, and adds, saying (to each, if there are several):
t: Exi, immúnde spíritus, et da honórem Deo vivo et vero. Fuge, immúnde spíritus, et da locum Jesu Christo, Fílio ejus. Recéde, immúnde spíritus, et da locum Spirítui Sancto Paráclito. || Go out, unclean spirit, and give honor to the living and true God. Flee, unclean spirit, and give place to Jesus Christ, His Son. Depart, unclean spirit, and give place to the Holy Spirit, the Paraclete.
r: 36. Stans ibidem extra cancellos, Sacerdos deponit pluviale ac stolam coloris violacei, et sumit stolam ac pluviale albi coloris. || 36. Standing there outside the railing, the priest lays aside the violet cope and stole and takes a white stole and cope.
r: 37. Tunc ducitur Electus ad Baptisterium, ubi Sacerdos interrogat Electum (singulos, si plures): || 37. The Elect is then led to the baptistery, where the priest questions the Elect (each one, if there are several):
t: Quis vocáris? || What is your name?
t: *Respondet:* N. || *He answers:* N.
r: 38. Interrogat (singulos, si plures): || 38. He asks (each one, if there are several):
t: N., Credis in Deum Patrem omnipoténtem, Creatórem cæli et terræ? ℟. Credo. || N., do you believe in God the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth? ℟. I do believe.
t: *Interrogat (singulos, si plures):* Credis in Jesum Christum, Fílium ejus únicum, Dóminum nostrum, natum, et passum? ℟. Credo. || *He asks (each one, if there are several):* Do you believe in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord, who was born and who suffered? ℟. I do believe.
t: *Interrogat (singulos, si plures):* Credis et in Spíritum Sanctum, sanctam Ecclésiam cathólicam, Sanctórum communiónem, remissiónem peccatórum, carnis resurrectiónem, et vitam ætérnam? ℟. Credo. || *He asks (each one, if there are several):* Do you also believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic Church, the communion of Saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting? ℟. I do believe.
r: 39. Deinde Sacerdos intingit pollicem dexterum in sacro Chrismate, et perungit verticem Electi (singulorum Electorum) in modum crucis, dicens: || 39. Then the priest dips his right thumb in the holy chrism and anoints the crown of the head of the Elect (of each of the Elect) in the form of a cross, saying:
t: Deus omnípotens, Pater Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, qui te regenerávit ex aqua et Spíritu Sancto, quique dedit tibi remissiónem ómnium peccatórum, *(hic inungit)* ipse te líniat ✠ Chrísmate salútis in eódem Christo Jesu, Dómino nostro, in vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || May almighty God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has given you new birth by water and the Holy Spirit and has granted you the forgiveness of all your sins, *(here he anoints)* Himself anoint you ✠ with the chrism of salvation in the same Christ Jesus our Lord unto life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
v: Pax tecum. || Peace be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
r: 40. Tunc bombacio, vel re simili, pollicem tergit, et imponit capiti Electi chrismale, seu candidum linteolum, et dat illi vestem candidam, dicens (singulis, si plures): || 40. Then with cotton or something similar he wipes his thumb, places on the head of the Elect the chrism-cloth, that is, a white linen cloth, and gives him a white garment, saying (to each, if there are several):
t: Accipe vestem cándidam, quam pérferas immaculátam ante tribúnal Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, ut hábeas vitam ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || Receive this white garment, and carry it without stain before the judgment seat of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you may have life everlasting. ℟. Amen.
r: 41. Et Electus deponit priores vestes, et induitur novis albi coloris, vel saltem exteriore candida, quam a Sacerdote accepit. Postea dat ei Sacerdos cereum, seu candelam accensam in manu dextera, dicens (singulis, si plures): || 41. And the Elect lays aside his former garments and is clothed in new ones of white, or at least in the white outer garment which he has received from the priest. Then the priest places in his right hand a wax taper, that is, a lighted candle, saying (to each, if there are several):
t: Accipe lámpadem ardéntem, et irreprehensíbilis custódi Baptísmum tuum: serva Dei mandáta, ut, cum Dóminus vénerit ad núptias, possis occúrrere ei una cum ómnibus Sanctis in aula cælésti, et vivas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Receive this burning light, and keep your Baptism so as to be without blame. Keep the commandments of God, so that when the Lord comes to the wedding feast, you may go out to meet Him together with all the Saints in the heavenly court, and may live for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: 42. Ipse vero Neophytus eundem cereum accensum manu tenet usque in finem, præterquam dum confirmatur. || 42. The newly baptized holds this same lighted taper in his hand until the end, except while he is being confirmed.

t: N., Vade (Ite) in pace, et Dóminus sit tecum (vobíscum). ℟. Amen. || N., go in peace, and the Lord be with you. ℟. Amen.
r: 44. Ritus superius descriptus servandus est etiam a Diacono Baptismi cæremonias supplente, qui tamen sale utetur a Sacerdote ad hunc usum rite prius benedicto. || 44. The rite described above is to be observed also by a deacon supplying the ceremonies of Baptism; he shall, however, use salt previously blessed for this purpose by a priest.
r: 45. Si adsit Episcopus, qui id legitime præstare possit, ab eo Neophyti, si sint confirmandi, præmissa, si opus sit, sacramentali confessione, Sacramento Confirmationis initiantur. Deinde si hora sit congruens, celebratur Missa, cui Neophyti intersunt, et sanctissimam Eucharistiam devote suscipiunt. || 45. If a bishop is present who can lawfully do so, the newly baptized, if they are to be confirmed, are initiated by him with the Sacrament of Confirmation, sacramental confession first being made if need be. Then, if the hour is suitable, Mass is celebrated, at which the newly baptized assist and devoutly receive the most holy Eucharist.

// Rite to Be Observed When a Bishop Baptizes
// ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# baptism-by-bishop | Ritus Servandus cum Episcopus Baptizat | Rite to Be Observed When a Bishop Baptizes | Sacraments | Tit. II, cap. 7
r: 1. Si Episcopus, vel S. R. E. Cardinalis parvulos vel adultos baptizare voluerit, parantur et servantur omnia, ut superius de ordine Baptismi dictum est, atque præterea hæc, quæ infra notantur. || 1. If a bishop, or a Cardinal of the Holy Roman Church, wishes to baptize children or adults, everything is prepared and observed as stated above concerning the order of Baptism, and in addition the following, which are noted below.
r: 2. Adsint Capellani, vel alii Presbyteri, et Clerici superpelliceis induti, qui ei assistant, ac ministrent. || 2. Chaplains, or other priests, and clerics vested in surplices should be present to assist and serve him.
r: 3. Ipse vero super rochettum accipiat amictum, albam, cingulum, stolam, et pluviale violacei coloris, ac mitram auriphrygiatam, atque, ita paratus, cum ministris procedat ad Baptismi ministerium. Et dum interrogat: N. Quid petis ab Ecclésia Dei? ac dum facit reliquas interrogationes, sedet cum mitra: cum vero exsufflat, dicendo: Exi ab eo, immúnde spíritus, surgit cum mitra. Rursus sedet, cum signat Catechumenum signo crucis in fronte, et in pectore, vel dicit: Accipe signum Crucis, etc. Et cum dicit Orationes, quæ præcedunt, vel sequuntur benedictionem salis, surgit deposita mitra; similiter cum ipsum sal benedicit. Cum autem sal benedictum immittit in os baptizandi, mitram accipit, et sedet. Cum vero legit Exorcismos, et dum, saliva aures et nares Catechumeni tangens, dicit: Ephphetha, ac dum introducit eum in ecclesiam, stat cum mitra. Cum autem dicit: Credo in Deum Patrem, etc., et Pater noster, super Catechumenum, stat sine mitra. Sed cum nomen quærit, et interrogat: N. Abrenúntias sátanæ, etc.; et baptizandum Oleo sacro in pectore et inter scapulas inungit, sedet cum mitra; quo facto accipit stolam et pluviale album. Et cum rursus interrogat de fide: Credis in Deum Patrem, etc., Vis baptizári, etc., et cum baptizat per infusionem, sedet cum mitra. Si vero baptizet per immersionem, mitram retinens stare debet. || 3. Over the rochet he puts on the amice, alb, cincture, stole, and a violet cope, with the gold-embroidered mitre, and, thus vested, proceeds with the ministers to administer Baptism. While he asks, "N., what do you ask of the Church of God?" and while he puts the other questions, he sits wearing the mitre; but when he breathes on the candidate, saying, "Go out from him, unclean spirit," he stands with the mitre. He sits again when he signs the catechumen with the sign of the cross on the forehead and breast, or says, "Receive the sign of the Cross," etc. When he says the prayers that precede or follow the blessing of the salt, he stands without the mitre; likewise when he blesses the salt itself. But when he puts the blessed salt into the mouth of the one to be baptized, he takes the mitre and sits. When he reads the exorcisms, and while, touching the ears and nostrils of the catechumen with spittle, he says "Ephphetha," and while he leads him into the church, he stands with the mitre. But when he says "I believe in God the Father," etc., and the Our Father over the catechumen, he stands without the mitre. When he asks the name and puts the questions, "N., do you renounce Satan?" etc., and anoints the one to be baptized with the holy oil on the breast and between the shoulders, he sits with the mitre; after this he takes a white stole and cope. And when he again questions him on the faith, "Do you believe in God the Father," etc., "Do you wish to be baptized?" etc., and when he baptizes by pouring, he sits with the mitre. But if he baptizes by immersion, he must stand, keeping the mitre on.
r: 4. Cum demum Chrismate verticem baptizati linit, et dat ei vestem candidam, et candelam accensam, ac dicit: Vade in pace, etc., sedet cum mitra. || 4. Finally, when he anoints the crown of the head of the baptized with chrism, gives him the white garment and the lighted candle, and says "Go in peace," etc., he sits with the mitre.
r: 5. Si autem Pontifex quempiam, a Presbytero jam catechizatum, tantum baptizare voluerit, sic paratus vestibus albis incipiat, postquam ad Baptisterium deventum fuerit, dicens: Quo nómine vocáris? ℟. N. Episcopus interrogat: N. Credis in Deum Patrem omnipoténtem, etc., et prosequitur usque in finem juxta ordinem Baptismi, ut supra. || 5. If, however, the bishop wishes only to baptize someone already catechized by a priest, he begins, vested in white, once they have reached the baptistery, saying: "By what name are you called?" ℟. N. The bishop asks: "N., do you believe in God the Father almighty," etc., and continues to the end according to the order of Baptism, as above.
