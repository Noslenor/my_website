r: 1. Accepto diplomate pontificio, constituatur dies Dominica, Delegato et populo commodior, pro ejusdem diplomatis publicatione, et exsecutionis inchoatione. || 1. When the papal document has been received, a Sunday convenient to the delegate and the people is appointed for its publication and the beginning of its execution.
r: 2. Die constituta, mane fiat contio, qua doceatur populus de contentis in diplomate pontificio, et de præparatione ad futuram Absolutionem et Benedictionem. Deinde a Delegato celebretur Missa pro remissione peccatorum, in paramentis violaceis, sine Glória in excélsis, et cum Credo, ad instar Missæ votivæ sollemnis pro re gravi et publica simul causa; qua finita, Celebrans, depositis casula et manipulo, induatur pluviali violaceo, et, si sit Episcopus, mitram auriphrygiatam assumat: tum, omnibus genuflexis ante Altare, cantentur Litaniæ Sanctorum, omissis Precibus et Orationibus post eas dici solitis. || 2. On the appointed day, in the morning, a sermon is given in which the people are taught about the contents of the papal document and about preparing for the coming absolution and blessing. Then the delegate celebrates the Mass for the remission of sins, in violet vestments, without the Gloria and with the Creed, in the manner of a solemn votive Mass for a grave and public cause. When it is finished, the celebrant lays aside the chasuble and maniple and puts on a violet cope (and, if he is a bishop, the orphreyed mitre); then, all kneeling before the altar, the Litany of the Saints is sung, omitting the prayers and orations usually said after it.

# apostolic-blessing | Ritus Benedictionis Apostolicæ statis diebus super populum elargiendæ | The Apostolic Blessing to Be Given to the People on Appointed Days | Proper to Religious Orders | Tit. VIII, cap. 32
r: Servandus a Regularibus quibus a S. Sede hujusmodi facultas indulta est vel indulgebitur. || To be observed by religious to whom the Holy See has granted or will grant this faculty.
r: 1. Admoneatur populus de Indulgentia a Sede Apostolica concessa, de præceptis operibus pro ea lucrifacienda, de die, quo visitanda est designata ecclesia, de hora denique, qua dabitur pontificia Benedictio. De quibus, quatenus opus sit, etiam schedis impressis, et consuetis locis palam affixis, certior fiat. || 1. The people are to be informed of the indulgence granted by the Apostolic See, of the works prescribed for gaining it, of the day on which the designated church is to be visited, and of the hour at which the papal blessing will be given. If necessary, they should also be informed of these things by printed notices publicly posted in the usual places.
r: 2. Postquam statutis die et hora populus ad ecclesiam convenerit, alta voce legantur apostolicæ litteræ, seu decreta, quibus Indulgentia conceditur, una cum potestate Benedictionem apostolicam super populum effundendi, ut de delegatione audientibus constet; et concessio, ex latino sermone in vulgarem accommodatum ad populi intellegentiam conversa, pronuntietur. Populus ad suorum scelerum detestationem pio brevique sermone excitetur: post quæ Sacerdos, nullis circumstantibus ministris, superpelliceo et stola alba indutus, ante Altare genuflexus, sequentibus verbis Dei opem imploret: || 2. When the people have gathered in the church on the appointed day and hour, the apostolic letters or decrees granting the indulgence, together with the faculty to impart the Apostolic Blessing to the people, are read aloud, so that those present may know of the delegation; and the grant is announced in the vernacular, translated from the Latin so that the people may understand it. The people are moved to detest their sins by a short and devout sermon. After this the priest, with no ministers attending, vested in surplice and white stole and kneeling before the altar, implores God's help with these words:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Salvum fac pópulum tuum, Dómine. || Save Your people, O Lord,
x: Et bénedic hereditáti tuæ. || And bless Your inheritance.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
