# marriage-rules | De Sacramento Matrimonii | On the Sacrament of Matrimony | Sacraments | Tit. VII, cap. 1
r: 1. Parochus, admonitus de aliquo Matrimonio in sua parœcia contrahendo, primum cognoscat ex iis ad quos spectat, qui et quales sint, qui Matrimonium contrahere volunt: an inter eos sit aliquod canonicum impedimentum: utrum sponte, libere et secundum honestatem Sacramenti velint contrahere: utrum sint in ætate legitima, et vir saltem sexdecim, mulier quatuordecim annos expleverit; et an in doctrina christiana sufficienter instructi sint. || 1. When the pastor has been informed of a marriage to be contracted in his parish, he should first learn from those whom it concerns who and of what sort are the persons who wish to contract marriage; whether there is any canonical impediment between them; whether they wish to contract of their own accord, freely, and in keeping with the dignity of the Sacrament; whether they are of lawful age, the man having completed at least sixteen years and the woman fourteen; and whether they are sufficiently instructed in Christian doctrine.
r: 2. Noverit, ex Codice Juris Canonici, quæ sint impedimenta Matrimonii, sive impedientia sive dirimentia; et qui sint gradus consanguinitatis et affinitatis. || 2. He should know from the Code of Canon Law what the impediments to marriage are, whether impeding or diriment, and what the degrees of consanguinity and affinity are.
r: 3. Habeat in primis ipse bene cognita præcepta illa omnia, quæ in Matrimoniis rite conficiendis servari oportet juxta sacros Canones: dabitque operam, ut illa in parœcia sua accurate exacteque serventur. || 3. Above all he himself should be well acquainted with all those precepts which, according to the sacred Canons, must be observed in the proper celebration of marriages; and he shall take care that they are carefully and exactly observed in his parish.
r: 4. Præsertim vero meminerit, Matrimonia inter virum raptorem et mulierem, intuitu Matrimonii raptam, quamdiu ipsa in raptoris potestate manserit, inita, et generatim Matrimonia, quæ aliter quam coram Parocho, vel loci Ordinario, vel Sacerdote ab alterutro delegato, et duobus saltem testibus contrahuntur, ex ipsis sacris Canonibus irrita omnino ac nulla esse. || 4. Above all he should remember that marriages entered into between a man who has abducted a woman and the woman abducted with a view to marriage, as long as she remains in the abductor's power, and in general marriages contracted otherwise than before the pastor, or the local Ordinary, or a priest delegated by either of them, and at least two witnesses, are by the sacred Canons themselves entirely invalid and null.
r: 5. Quamvis autem Parochus et loci Ordinarius, intra fines sui territorii, Matrimoniis nedum suorum subditorum, sed etiam non subditorum valide assistant, pro regula tamen habeatur, ut, ad normam juris, Matrimonium coram sponsæ Parocho celebretur, nisi justa causa excuset, quo in casu coram sponsi Parocho celebrabitur. Matrimonia autem catholicorum mixti ritus, nisi aliud particulari jure cautum sit, in ritu viri et coram ejusdem Parocho sunt celebranda. || 5. Although the pastor and the local Ordinary, within the limits of their territory, validly assist at the marriages not only of their own subjects but also of those who are not their subjects, it is nevertheless to be held as the rule that, according to the norm of law, the marriage is celebrated before the pastor of the bride, unless a just cause excuses, in which case it shall be celebrated before the pastor of the bridegroom. Marriages of Catholics of mixed rite, unless particular law provides otherwise, are to be celebrated in the rite of the man and before his pastor.
r: 6. Antequam Matrimonium contrahatur, a proprio contrahentium Parocho publice denuntietur, inter quosnam Matrimonium sit contrahendum. Quæ publicationes fiant tribus continuis diebus dominicis aliisque festis de præcepto, in ecclesia inter Missarum sollemnia, aut inter alia divina Officia, ad quæ populus frequens accedat. Potest autem loci Ordinarius pro suo territorio publicationibus substituere publicam, ad valvas ecclesiæ parœcialis, aliusve ecclesiæ, affixionem nominum contrahentium per spatium saltem octo dierum, ita tamen ut, hoc spatio, duo dies festi de præcepto comprehendantur. || 6. Before the marriage is contracted, the proper pastor of the contracting parties shall publicly announce between whom the marriage is to be contracted. These publications are to be made on three successive Sundays and other holydays of obligation, in church during the solemnities of Mass, or during other divine Offices to which the people come in numbers. The local Ordinary, however, may for his territory substitute for the publications the public posting of the names of the contracting parties on the doors of the parish church or of another church for a space of at least eight days, yet so that two holydays of obligation fall within that space.
r: 7. Si vero vir et mulier parœciæ sint diversæ, in utraque parœcia fiant publicationes. || 7. If the man and the woman belong to different parishes, the publications are to be made in both parishes.
r: 8. Publicationes autem fiant hoc modo. Inter Missarum sollemnia, aut inter alia divina Officia, ut supra, Parochus populum admoneat in hanc sententiam vulgari sermone: || 8. The publications are to be made in this way. During the solemnities of Mass, or during other divine Offices, as above, the pastor shall address the people in the vernacular to this effect:
t: Notum sit ómnibus hic præséntibus, quod N., fílius N., famíliæ N., parœ́ciæ S. N., et N., fília N., famíliæ N., parœ́ciæ S. N., intendunt inter se contráhere Matrimónium. Proínde admonémus omnes et síngulos, ut si quis nóverit áliquod consanguinitátis, vel affinitátis, vel cognatiónis spirituális, vel quodvis áliud impediméntum inter eos esse, quod Matrimónium contrahéndum ínvicem impédiat, illud quamprímum Parócho aut loci Ordinário reveláre débeat: et hoc admonémus primo *(si sit prima)*, vel secúndo *(si sit secunda)* vel tértio *(si sit tertia publicatio)*. || Be it known to all here present that N., son of N., of the family N., of the parish of St. N., and N., daughter of N., of the family N., of the parish of St. N., intend to contract marriage with each other. Therefore we admonish each and every one that, if anyone knows of any impediment of consanguinity, or of affinity, or of spiritual relationship, or of any other kind existing between them, which would stand in the way of the marriage they are to contract, he is bound to make it known as soon as possible to the pastor or to the local Ordinary. And this we announce for the first time *(if it is the first)*, or the second *(if it is the second)*, or the third time *(if it is the third publication)*.
r: 9. Loci Ordinarius proprius pro suo prudenti judicio potest ex legitima causa a publicationibus etiam in aliena diœcesi faciendis dispensare. || 9. The proper local Ordinary may, according to his prudent judgment and for a lawful cause, dispense from the publications, even those to be made in another diocese.
r: Si plures sint Ordinarii proprii, ille jus habet dispensandi, in cujus diœcesi Matrimonium celebratur; quod si Matrimonium extra proprias ineatur diœceses, quilibet Ordinarius proprius dispensare potest. || If there are several proper Ordinaries, the one in whose diocese the marriage is celebrated has the right to dispense; but if the marriage is entered into outside their proper dioceses, any one of the proper Ordinaries may dispense.
r: 10. Si alius Parochus investigationem aut publicationes peregerit, de harum exitu statim per authenticum documentum certiorem reddat Parochum, qui Matrimonio assistere debet. || 10. If another pastor has carried out the investigation or the publications, he shall at once inform the pastor who is to assist at the marriage of their outcome by an authentic document.
r: 11. Peractis investigationibus et publicationibus, Parochus Matrimonio ne assistat, antequam omnia documenta necessaria receperit, et præterea, nisi rationabilis causa aliud postulet, tres dies decurrerint ab ultima publicatione. || 11. When the investigations and publications have been completed, the pastor shall not assist at the marriage before he has received all the necessary documents, and moreover, unless a reasonable cause requires otherwise, before three days have passed since the last publication.
r: Si intra sex menses Matrimonium contractum non fuerit, publicationes repetantur, nisi aliud loci Ordinario videatur. || If the marriage has not been contracted within six months, the publications are to be repeated, unless the local Ordinary judges otherwise.
r: 12. Si nullum detectum fuerit impedimentum, nec dubium nec certum, Parochus, expletis publicationibus, ad Matrimonii celebrationem partes admittat. || 12. If no impediment, whether doubtful or certain, has been discovered, the pastor, once the publications are completed, shall admit the parties to the celebration of the marriage.
r: 13. Matrimonio vagorum, eorum videlicet qui nullibi domicilium habeant vel quasi-domicilium, Parochus, excepto casu necessitatis, numquam assistat, nisi, re ad loci Ordinarium vel ad Sacerdotem ab eo delegatum delata, licentiam assistendi obtinuerit. || 13. At the marriage of transients, that is, of those who have no domicile or quasi-domicile anywhere, the pastor, except in case of necessity, shall never assist unless, having referred the matter to the local Ordinary or to a priest delegated by him, he has obtained permission to assist.
r: 14. Ne omittat Parochus, secundum diversam personarum condicionem, sponsos docere sanctitatem Sacramenti Matrimonii, mutuas conjugum obligationes et obligationes parentum erga prolem; eosdemque vehementer adhortetur, ut ante Matrimonii celebrationem sua peccata diligenter confiteantur et Sanctissimam Eucharistiam pie recipiant. || 14. The pastor should not fail, according to the varying condition of the persons, to teach the engaged couple the holiness of the Sacrament of Matrimony, the mutual obligations of husband and wife, and the obligations of parents toward their children; and he should earnestly exhort them, before the celebration of the marriage, to confess their sins diligently and to receive the Most Holy Eucharist devoutly.
r: Catholici vero qui Sacramentum Confirmationis nondum receperunt, illud, antequam ad Matrimonium admittantur, recipiant, si id possint sine gravi incommodo. || Catholics who have not yet received the Sacrament of Confirmation should receive it before they are admitted to marriage, if they can do so without grave inconvenience.
r: 15. Parochus graviter filiosfamilias minores hortetur ne nuptias ineant, insciis aut rationabiliter invitis parentibus; quod si abnuerint, eorum Matrimonio ne assistat, nisi consulto prius loci Ordinario. || 15. The pastor shall earnestly exhort minors still under their parents' authority not to enter into marriage without the knowledge, or against the reasonable wishes, of their parents; if they refuse, he shall not assist at their marriage without first consulting the local Ordinary.
r: 16. Parochus curet ut sponsi, celebrato Matrimonio, benedictionem sollemnem accipiant, quæ dari eis potest etiam postquam diu vixerint in Matrimonio, sed solum in Missa, servata speciali rubrica et excepto tempore feriato. || 16. The pastor shall see to it that the spouses, once the marriage has been celebrated, receive the solemn blessing, which may be given to them even after they have long lived in marriage, but only during Mass, the special rubric being observed and the closed season excepted.
r: Sollemnem benedictionem ille tantum Sacerdos per se ipse vel per alium dare potest, qui valide et licite Matrimonio potest assistere. || Only that priest who can validly and lawfully assist at the marriage may give the solemn blessing, either himself or through another.
r: 17. Nihilominus Parochus conjuges admoneat, maxime si neophyti sint, vel ante conversionem ab hæresi valide Matrimonium contraxerint, benedictionem ipsam ad ritum et sollemnitatem, non vero ad substantiam et validitatem pertinere conjugii. || 17. Nevertheless the pastor should remind the spouses, especially if they are newly baptized, or if they validly contracted marriage before their conversion from heresy, that the blessing itself belongs to the rite and solemnity, and not to the substance and validity, of the marriage.
r: 18. Caveat autem Parochus, ne, quando conjuges in primis nuptiis benedictionem acceperint, eos in secundis benedicat, sive mulier sive etiam vir ad secundas nuptias transeat. Sed ubi ea viget consuetudo, ut, si hanc benedictionem vir tantum alias obtinuerit, nuptiæ benedicantur, ea servanda est; mulier vero vidua, cui semel benedictio sollemnis data sit, nequit in subsequentibus nuptiis eam iterum accipere, etiamsi ejus vir numquam uxorem duxerit. || 18. The pastor should take care, however, that when spouses have received the blessing at a first marriage, he does not bless them at a second, whether it is the woman or the man who passes to a second marriage. But where the custom prevails that the marriage is blessed if only the man has previously received this blessing, that custom is to be kept; a widow, however, to whom the solemn blessing has once been given, cannot receive it again at a subsequent marriage, even if her husband has never before taken a wife.
r: 19. Matrimonium quolibet anni tempore contrahi potest. Sollemnis tantum nuptiarum benedictio vetatur a prima Dominica Adventus usque ad diem Nativitatis Domini inclusive, et a Feria IV Cinerum usque ad Dominicam Paschatis inclusive. || 19. Marriage may be contracted at any season of the year. Only the solemn nuptial blessing is forbidden from the first Sunday of Advent until the day of the Nativity of the Lord inclusive, and from Ash Wednesday until Easter Sunday inclusive.
r: Ordinarii tamen locorum possunt, salvis legibus liturgicis, etiam prædictis temporibus eam permittere ex justa causa, monitis sponsis ut a nimia pompa abstineant. || Local Ordinaries, however, may, without prejudice to the liturgical laws, permit it even during the aforesaid seasons for a just cause, the spouses being admonished to refrain from excessive display.
r: 20. Matrimonium inter catholicos celebretur in ecclesia parœciali; in alia autem ecclesia vel oratorio sive publico sive semi-publico, nonnisi de licentia Ordinarii loci vel Parochi celebrari poterit. || 20. Marriage between Catholics is to be celebrated in the parish church; in another church or oratory, whether public or semi-public, it may be celebrated only with the permission of the local Ordinary or of the pastor.
r: Matrimonium in ædibus privatis celebrari Ordinarii locorum in extraordinario tantum aliquo casu et accedente semper justa ac rationabili causa permittere possunt: sed in ecclesiis vel oratoriis sive Seminarii sive religiosarum, Ordinarii id ne permittant, nisi urgente necessitate, ac opportunis adhibitis cautelis. || Local Ordinaries may permit a marriage to be celebrated in a private house only in some extraordinary case and always when a just and reasonable cause is present; but they should not permit it in the churches or oratories of a seminary or of women religious, except in urgent necessity and with suitable precautions taken.
r: Matrimonia vero inter partem catholicam et partem acatholicam extra ecclesiam celebrentur; quod si Ordinarius prudenter judicet id servari non posse quin graviora oriantur mala, prudenti ejus arbitrio committitur hac super re dispensare. || Marriages between a Catholic party and a non-Catholic party, however, are to be celebrated outside the church; but if the Ordinary prudently judges that this cannot be observed without graver evils arising, it is left to his prudent discretion to dispense in this matter.
n: The rite of celebrating marriage follows in Tit. VII, cap. 2 (The Rite for Celebrating the Sacrament of Matrimony).
