# garment-bvm | Benedictio Vestis in honorem beatæ Mariæ Virginis | Blessing of a Garment in Honor of the Blessed Virgin Mary | Sacramentals & Scapulars | Appendix, Benedictiones non reservatæ, 49
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Béne✠dic, Dómine, vestem istam, in honórem et sub protectióne beatíssimæ Vírginis Maríæ suméndam: et præsta per invocatiónem sancti tui nóminis; ut qui (quæ) eam indúerit, córporis sanitátem et ánimæ tutélam percípiat. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || ✠ Bless, O Lord, this garment, to be taken up in honor and under the protection of the most blessed Virgin Mary; and grant, through the invocation of Your holy name, that he (she) who puts it on may receive health of body and protection of soul. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Et aspergatur aqua benedicta. || And it is sprinkled with holy water.

# litter-sick | Benedictio Lecticæ pro Infirmis | Blessing of a Litter for the Sick | The Sick | Appendix, Benedictiones non reservatæ, 54
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Jesu Christe, Fili Dei vivi, qui, dum peregrinabáris in terris, pertransíbas benefaciéndo et sanándo omnem languórem et omnem infirmitátem in pópulis, quique hóminem paralýticum, jacéntem in lecto, ad salútem mentis et córporis restituísti; réspice, quǽsumus, ad fidem et ad sensus commiseratiónis servórum tuórum, qui animáti spíritu veræ caritátis, qua tu eis exémplo præivísti, et quam in præcéptum traduxísti, vehículum hoc, ad instar léctuli artificióse éxstrui voluérunt eo fine, ut ad locum curatiónis vel aptíssime deférri possint quicúmque, aut vulnéribus sint affécti, aut quavis infirmitáte detineántur. Ægrótis ígitur, qui hoc componúntur vehículo, quod nunc in tui nóminis virtúte bene✠dícimus, esto, mitíssime Jesu, in itínere solácium, in perículis tutámen, in dolóribus refrigérium. Præsta, ut iídem, tuis Ángelis comitántibus, ad curatiónis sedem tranquíllo cursu pervéniant, ibíque prístinam sanitátem recúperent, eáque, te miseránte, per intercessiónem sanctíssimæ tuæ Matris Maríæ, percépta, abeúntes in domos suas, honoríficent te Deum verum: Qui cum Patre et Spíritu Sancto vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God, who, while You journeyed upon earth, went about doing good and healing every sickness and every infirmity among the people, and who restored to health of mind and body the paralytic lying on his bed: look, we pray, upon the faith and the compassionate feelings of Your servants who, moved by the spirit of true charity, in which You went before them by Your example and which You made a commandment, have had this conveyance skillfully built in the likeness of a little bed, so that all who are afflicted with wounds or held fast by any infirmity may be carried most fittingly to a place of healing. Be, then, O most gentle Jesus, to the sick who are laid in this conveyance, which we now ✠ bless in the power of Your name, a comfort on the journey, a safeguard in dangers, and a relief in pain. Grant that, with Your Angels accompanying them, they may arrive by a peaceful course at the place of healing, and there recover their former health; and having received it through Your mercy and the intercession of Your most holy Mother Mary, may they go back to their homes and glorify You, the true God: who with the Father and the Holy Spirit live and reign for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Et aspergatur aqua benedicta. || And it is sprinkled with holy water.
