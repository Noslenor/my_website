# precious-blood-scapular | Benedictio Scapularis Pretiosi Sanguinis | Blessing of the Scapular of the Precious Blood | Sacramentals & Scapulars | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ, 15
r: Scapulare fieri solet ex lana rubei coloris cum imagine Sanctissimi Crucifixi, vel Sanctissimi Cordis D. N. J. C. Sanguinem manantis super Calicem. || The scapular is usually made of red wool, bearing an image of the most holy Crucified, or of the most Sacred Heart of our Lord Jesus Christ pouring out Blood over a chalice.
r: Suscepturus Scapulare genuflectit; et Sacerdos, superpelliceo ac stola rubea indutus, dicit: || The one who is to receive the scapular kneels; and the priest, vested in surplice and red stole, says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Redemísti nos, Dómine, in Sánguine tuo. || You have redeemed us, O Lord, in Your Blood.
x: Et fecísti nos Deo nostro regnum. || And have made us a kingdom for our God.
v: Te ergo quǽsumus, tuis fámulis súbveni. || We therefore pray You, help Your servants,
x: Quos pretióso Sánguine redemísti. || Whom You have redeemed with Your precious Blood.
v: Dómine, exáudi oratiónem meam. || O Lord, hear my prayer.
x: Et clamor meus ad te véniat. || And let my cry come unto You.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Ætérne Pater, et omnípotens Deus, qui Unigénitum tuum vestem nostræ mortalitátis indúere, et Sánguinem suum pretiósum pro mundi salúte effúndere voluísti: obsecrámus cleméntiam tuam, ut hoc genus vestiménti quod in signum devotiónis fidélium erga salútis nostræ prétium institútum est, bene✠dícere, et sancti✠ficáre dignéris; ut, quicúmque eo usus fúerit, indúere mereátur eúndem Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti Deus, per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Eternal Father and almighty God, who willed that Your Only-begotten should put on the garment of our mortality and pour out His precious Blood for the salvation of the world: we implore Your mercy that You would ✠ bless and ✠ sanctify this kind of garment, instituted as a sign of the devotion of the faithful toward the price of our salvation, so that whoever wears it may be worthy to put on the same Jesus Christ our Lord, Your Son, who lives and reigns with You in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Et aspergatur aqua benedicta. || And it is sprinkled with holy water.

# precious-blood-cords | Benedictio Funiculorum et Vittarum Pretiosi Sanguinis | Blessing of Cords and Ribbons of the Precious Blood | Sacramentals & Scapulars | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ, 16
r: Vittæ fiunt ex lana rubei coloris, quæ, si exterius a mulieribus ferantur, a sinistro latere pendent. Funiculi pariter ex lana rubea conficiuntur. || The ribbons are made of red wool; if worn outwardly by women, they hang from the left side. The cords likewise are made of red wool.
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Redemísti nos, Dómine, in Sánguine tuo. || You have redeemed us, O Lord, in Your Blood.
x: Et fecísti nos Deo nostro regnum. || And have made us a kingdom for our God.
v: Te ergo quǽsumus, tuis fámulis súbveni. || We therefore pray You, help Your servants,
x: Quos pretióso Sánguine redemísti. || Whom You have redeemed with Your precious Blood.
v: Dómine, exáudi oratiónem meam. || O Lord, hear my prayer.
x: Et clamor meus ad te véniat. || And let my cry come unto You.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Omnípotens sempitérne Deus, qui Unigénitum Fílium tuum constituísti humáni géneris Redemptórem, ac ejus Sánguine placári voluísti: da nobis tuæ dilectiónis augméntum, et super hos funículos (vel super has vittas) bene✠dictiónem tuam infúndere dignáre; ut, quicúmque eis cinctus fúerit, suórum delictórum contritiónem in terris habére mereátur, et ab omni carnis labe, cunctísque perículis mentis et córporis liberátus, ad vitam pervéniat ætérnam. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Almighty, everlasting God, who appointed Your only-begotten Son the Redeemer of the human race, and willed to be appeased by His Blood: grant us an increase of Your love, and graciously pour out Your ✠ blessing upon these cords (or these ribbons), so that whoever is girded with them may deserve to have contrition for his sins on earth, and, freed from every stain of the flesh and from all dangers of mind and body, may come to eternal life. Through the same Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Et aspergantur aqua benedicta. || And they are sprinkled with holy water.

# sacred-heart-scapular | Benedictio et Impositio Scapularis Sacri Cordis Jesu | Blessing and Investiture with the Scapular of the Sacred Heart of Jesus | Sacramentals & Scapulars | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ, 18
r: (Approbata a S. R. C. die 4 Aprilis 1900) || (Approved by the Sacred Congregation of Rites, 4 April 1900)
r: Suscepturus Scapulare genuflectit; et Sacerdos, superpelliceo et stola alba indutus, dicit: || The one who is to receive the scapular kneels; and the priest, vested in surplice and white stole, says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Osténde nobis, Dómine, misericórdiam tuam. || Show us, O Lord, Your mercy.
x: Et salutáre tuum da nobis. || And grant us Your salvation.
v: Dómine, exáudi oratiónem meam. || O Lord, hear my prayer.
x: Et clamor meus ad te véniat. || And let my cry come unto You.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Jesu, qui ineffábiles Cordis tui divítias Ecclésiæ sponsæ tuæ singulári dilectiónis benefício aperuísti: hoc Scapuláre ejúsdem Cordis tui emblémate decorátum bene✠dícere dignéris; ut, quicúmque illud devóte gestáverit, intercedénte beáta et clementíssima Genetríce tua María, virtútibus et donis cæléstibus ditári mereátur: Qui vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus, who by a singular gift of love opened the unspeakable riches of Your Heart to the Church, Your bride: be pleased to ✠ bless this scapular, adorned with the emblem of that same Heart of Yours, so that whoever wears it devoutly may, through the intercession of Your blessed and most merciful Mother Mary, be enriched with virtues and heavenly gifts: who live and reign for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Postea Sacerdos Scapulare aspergat aqua benedicta, illudque imponat, dicens: || Then the priest sprinkles the scapular with holy water and puts it on the person, saying:
t: Accipe, frater (soror) hoc Scapuláre sacri Cordis Jesu, quo ornátus (-a) in honórem et memóriam amóris et passiónis ejus, per intercessiónem beátæ Maríæ Vírginis, Matris misericórdiæ, divínæ grátiæ largitátem et ætérnæ glóriæ fructum cónsequi mereáris. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Receive, brother (sister), this scapular of the Sacred Heart of Jesus, so that, adorned with it in honor and memory of His love and Passion, you may deserve, through the intercession of the blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of mercy, to obtain the abundance of divine grace and the fruit of eternal glory. Through the same Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Si Scapulare pluribus imponatur, omnia plurali numero dicantur. || If the scapular is given to several persons, everything is said in the plural.
r: Deinde una vice cum adscripto dicat sive latino sive vernaculo idiomate sequentes preces jaculatorias: || Then, once, together with the one enrolled, he says the following ejaculatory prayers, either in Latin or in the vernacular:
t: Jesu, mitis et húmilis corde, fac cor nostrum sicut cor tuum. || Jesus, meek and humble of heart, make our heart like unto Your Heart.
t: María, Mater grátiæ, Mater misericórdiæ, tu nos ab hoste prótege, et mortis hora súscipe. || Mary, Mother of grace, Mother of mercy, protect us from the enemy and receive us at the hour of death.

# immaculate-heart-scapular | Benedictio et Impositio Scapularis Immaculati Cordis B. M. V. | Blessing and Investiture with the Scapular of the Immaculate Heart of Mary | Sacramentals & Scapulars | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ, 19
r: Suscepturus Scapulare genuflectit; et Sacerdos, superpelliceo et stola coloris albi indutus, dicit: || The one who is to receive the scapular kneels; and the priest, vested in surplice and white stole, says:
t: *Ant.* Suscépimus, Deus, misericórdiam tuam in médio templi tui: secúndum nomen tuum, Deus, sic et laus tua in fines terræ: justítia plena est déxtera tua. || *Ant.* We have received Your mercy, O God, in the midst of Your temple; as is Your name, O God, so also is Your praise to the ends of the earth; Your right hand is full of justice.
t: Kýrie, eléison. Christe, eléison. Kýrie, eléison. || Lord, have mercy. Christ, have mercy. Lord, have mercy.
t: Pater noster *(secreto usque ad)* || Our Father *(silently until)*
v: Et ne nos indúcas in tentatiónem. || And lead us not into temptation.
x: Sed líbera nos a malo. || But deliver us from evil.
v: Salvum (salvam, vel salvos) fac servum tuum (ancíllam tuam, vel servos tuos). || Save Your servant (handmaid, or servants),
x: Deus meus, sperántem (-es) in te. || Who hope(s) in You, O my God.
v: Mitte ei(s), Dómine, auxílium de sancto. || Send him (her, them) help, O Lord, from Your holy place.
x: Et de Sion tuére eum (eam, vel eos). || And defend him (her, them) out of Sion.
v: Nihil profíciat inimícus in eo (ea, vel eis). || Let the enemy have no power over him (her, them).
x: Et fílius iniquitátis non appónat nocére ei(s). || And let the son of iniquity do nothing to harm him (her, them).
v: Dómine, exáudi oratiónem meam. || O Lord, hear my prayer.
x: Et clamor meus ad te véniat. || And let my cry come unto You.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Suscípiat te (vos) Christus in número fidélium suórum, et nos, licet indígni, te (vos) suscípimus in oratiónibus nostris. Concédat tibi (vobis) Deus, per Unigénitum suum, mediatórem Dei et hóminum, tempus bene vivéndi, locum bene agéndi, constántiam bene perseverándi, et ad ætérnæ vitæ hereditátem felíciter perveniéndi. Et sicut nos hódie fratérna cáritas spirituáliter jungit in terris, ita divína píetas, quæ dilectiónis est auctrix, nos cum fidélibus suis conjúngere dignétur in cælis: Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || May Christ receive you into the number of His faithful; and we, though unworthy, receive you into our prayers. May God, through His Only-begotten, the mediator between God and men, grant you time to live well, place to act well, constancy to persevere well, and a happy arrival at the inheritance of eternal life. And as brotherly charity spiritually joins us today on earth, so may divine goodness, which is the author of love, deign to join us with His faithful in heaven. Through the same Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Postea benedicit Scapulare, dicens: || Afterwards he blesses the scapular, saying:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Deus, cujus verbo sanctificántur ómnia, bene✠dictiónem tuam effúnde super hábitum istum (hábitus istos): et præsta; ut, qui eo (eis) secúndum legem et voluntátem tuam cum gratiárum actióne usus fúerit (usi fúerint), per invocatiónem sanctíssimi nóminis tui, córporis sanitátem et ánimæ tutélam, te auctóre, percípiat (percípiant). Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || O God, by whose word all things are made holy, pour out Your ✠ blessing upon this habit (these habits), and grant that whoever uses it (they who use them) with thanksgiving according to Your law and will may, through the invocation of Your most holy name, receive by Your gift health of body and protection of soul. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Deinde aspergit aqua benedicta Scapulare, et singulis petentibus illud imponit, dicens: || Then he sprinkles the scapular with holy water, and puts it on each of those who ask for it, saying:
t: Accipe, frater (soror), hunc hábitum benedíctum, precans sanctíssimam Vírginem, ut ejus méritis illum pérferas sine mácula, teque ab omni adversitáte deféndat, et ad vitam perdúcat ætérnam. ℟. Amen. || Receive, brother (sister), this blessed habit, asking the most holy Virgin that by her merits you may wear it without stain, and that she may defend you from all adversity and lead you to eternal life. ℟. Amen.
