# scapular-st-michael | Benedictio et Impositio Scapularis Sancti Michaelis Archangeli | Blessing and Investiture of the Scapular of Saint Michael the Archangel | Sacramentals & Scapulars | Appendix, Benedictiones approbatæ, 1
h: Benedictiones approbatæ pro aliquibus locis a Sacerdotibus Apostolicum indultum habentibus adhibendæ || Approved Blessings to Be Used in Certain Places by Priests Having an Apostolic Indult
r: Pro archisodalicio sub hoc titulo in perinsigni ecclesia Sancti Angeli in Foro Piscium Romæ erecto, et pro aliis hujusmodi sodalitatibus extra Urbem eidem rite aggregatis. || For the archconfraternity of this title erected in the most distinguished church of Sant'Angelo in Pescheria at Rome, and for other such confraternities outside the City duly aggregated to it.
r: Notandum quod benedictio et impositio Scapularis fieri debet a Sacerdote, qui præest Consilio directivo, aut ab alio Sacerdote ab eo legitime delegato. || Note that the blessing and investiture of the scapular must be performed by the priest who presides over the directing council, or by another priest lawfully delegated by him.
r: Suscepturus Scapulare genuflectit; et Sacerdos, superpelliceo ac stola alba indutus, dicit: || The one who is to receive the scapular kneels; and the priest, vested in surplice and white stole, says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Omnípotens sempitérne Deus, qui Ecclésiam tuam contra diabólicas nequítias per sanctum Michaélem Archángelum deféndere dignáris: te súpplices exorámus; ut hoc signum ad excitándam et foméndam inter fidéles tuos in tantum Patrónum devotiónem institútum, bene✠dícas et sancti✠fices; et iis, qui illud gestáverint, ejúsdem sancti Archángeli auxílio roborátis, inimícos ánimæ et córporis in vita et in morte superáre concédas. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Almighty, everlasting God, who deign to defend Your Church against the wickedness of the devil through Saint Michael the Archangel: we humbly implore You to ✠ bless and ✠ sanctify this sign, instituted to arouse and foster among Your faithful devotion to so great a patron; and grant that those who wear it, strengthened by the help of the same holy Archangel, may overcome the enemies of soul and body in life and in death. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Aspergit Scapulare aqua benedicta, ac deinde illud imponit, dicens: || He sprinkles the scapular with holy water, and then puts it on the person, saying:
t: Accipe, frater (soror), scapuláre sancti Michaélis Archángeli, ut, eo júgiter interveniénte, vitam sanctam dúcere váleas. ℟. Amen. || Receive, brother (sister), the scapular of Saint Michael the Archangel, that by his unceasing intercession you may be able to lead a holy life. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Preces nostras, quǽsumus, Dómine, benígnus exáudi et hunc fámulum tuum (hanc fámulam tuam) speciáli patrocínio sancti Michaélis Archángeli commíssum (-am) bene✠dícere dignéris; ut, per ejus intercessiónem, quámlibet offénsam tuam evitándo et impediéndo, in tuo famulátu própriam aliorúmque sanctificatiónem cónsequi mereátur. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Graciously hear our prayers, we beseech You, O Lord, and deign to ✠ bless this Your servant (handmaid), entrusted to the special patronage of Saint Michael the Archangel; that through his intercession, by avoiding and hindering every offense against You, he (she) may merit in Your service to attain his (her) own sanctification and that of others. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.

# water-holy-lance | Benedictio Aquæ in Festo SS. Lanceæ et Clavorum Domini | Blessing of Water on the Feast of the Holy Lance and Nails of the Lord | Holy Water | Appendix, Benedictiones approbatæ, 4
r: Approbata a S. R. C. pro Archidiœcesi Coloniensi. || Approved by the Sacred Congregation of Rites for the Archdiocese of Cologne.
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Jesu Christe, qui pro salúte mundi passus es de corde tuo aquam et sánguinem per lánceam mílitis emanáre: concéde propítius; ut præsens aqua hujus sacræ Lánceæ tactu bene✠dicátur ac sancti✠ficétur, ut sit ómnibus suméntibus salus ánimæ et córporis, et ubicúmque fúerit aspérsa, per sanctíssimæ passiónis tuæ invocatiónem omnis infestátio immúndi spíritus abigátur infirmitásque procul pellátur, sánitas mentis ac córporis tribuátur per te, Salvátor mundi: Qui vivis et regnas Deus per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus Christ, who for the salvation of the world allowed water and blood to flow from Your heart through the soldier's lance: graciously grant that this water may be ✠ blessed and ✠ sanctified by the touch of this sacred Lance, that it may be health of soul and body to all who take it; and wherever it is sprinkled, through the invocation of Your most holy Passion, may every assault of the unclean spirit be driven off, sickness be cast far away, and health of mind and body be granted, through You, Savior of the world: who live and reign, God, for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Imponitur Reliquia S. Lanceæ in aquam dicendo: || The relic of the Holy Lance is placed in the water, with the words:
t: In nómine Patris, et Fílii, ✠ et Spíritus Sancti. Amen. || In the name of the Father, and of the Son, ✠ and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
r: Interim cum Reliquia S. Lanceæ efformatur crux per aquam. || Meanwhile a cross is traced through the water with the relic of the Holy Lance.

Amen. || I exorcise you, creature of water, by the ✠ living God, by the ✠ true God, by the ✠ holy God, that you may become a saving remedy for soul and body, through Him who shall come to judge the living and the dead and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Béne✠dic, Dómine sancte Pater, hanc creatúram aquæ, ut sit remédium humáno géneri ad ómnia mala córporis et ánimæ tollénda et ómnia nóxia inimíci expellénda, et præsta in ea per invocatiónem sanctíssimi nóminis tui tutélam mentis et córporis. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || ✠ Bless, O Lord, holy Father, this creature of water, that it may be a remedy for the human race, to take away all evils of body and soul and to drive out every harm of the enemy; and grant in it, through the invocation of Your most holy name, protection of mind and body. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.

# st-hubert | Benedictio Aquæ, Salis et Panis in honorem S. Huberti Ep. contra morsum rabidi canis | Blessing of Water, Salt and Bread in Honor of Saint Hubert, Bishop, against the Bite of a Rabid Dog | Food & Drink | Appendix, Benedictiones approbatæ, 7
r: Approbata a S. R. C. pro Archidiœcesi Coloniensi. || Approved by the Sacred Congregation of Rites for the Archdiocese of Cologne.
r: Hæc benedictio fieri solet tam pro hominibus quam pro jumentis in Sacello S. Huberti coram Reliquiis ejusdem S. Episcopi. || This blessing is customarily given for men as well as for beasts of burden in the chapel of Saint Hubert, before the relics of the same holy bishop.
r: Petentes benedictionem conferunt panem et aquam, et Sacerdos, superpelliceo ac stola alba indutus, hæc benedicit coram expositis Reliquiis, ut infra sequitur: dein benedicuntur et ipsi petentes, prout etiam inferius sequitur. Tum insinuatur, ut de pane et aqua benedicta sumant per novem dies, ter in die, scilicet mane jejuni, ante prandium et ante cenam; deinde orent singulis his diebus dicendo semel Credo et novies Pater et Ave, et his novem diebus suscipiant interea Ss. Sacramenta Pœnitentiæ et Eucharistiæ, et singulis annis festum S. Huberti venerentur ut præceptum, offerant ad altare, osculentur sacras Reliquias, et sic abeant in pace. || Those asking for the blessing bring bread and water, and the priest, vested in surplice and white stole, blesses them before the exposed relics, as follows below; then the petitioners themselves are blessed, as also follows below. They are then advised to take of the blessed bread and water for nine days, three times a day, namely in the morning fasting, before the midday meal, and before supper; and to pray on each of these days, saying the Creed once and the Our Father and Hail Mary nine times; and during these nine days to receive the holy sacraments of Penance and the Eucharist; and every year to keep the feast of Saint Hubert as a feast of obligation, make an offering at the altar, and kiss the sacred relics; and so they depart in peace.
h: Exorcismus aquæ || Exorcism of the Water
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
t: Exorcízo te, creatúra aquæ, in nómine Dei ✠ Patris omnipoténtis, et in nómine Jesu ✠ Christi Fílii ejus, et in nómine Spíritus ✠ Sancti, ut omne virus rabiéi et omnis spíritus immúndus et incúrsio Sátanæ superétur et expellátur, et ut fias aqua exorcizáta ad expelléndam et effugándam rábiem et venénum ejus, et omnem morbum tam hóminum quam pécorum et invídiam malórum spirítuum atque omne phantásma inimíci, et ut ipsum virus rabiéi et stabíliter ipsum inimícum eradicáre váleas, per virtútem Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || I exorcise you, creature of water, in the name of God the ✠ Father almighty, and in the name of Jesus ✠ Christ His Son, and in the name of the Holy ✠ Spirit, that every venom of rabies, every unclean spirit and every assault of Satan may be overcome and driven out; and that you may become exorcised water to expel and put to flight rabies and its poison, and every disease of men and of cattle, and the malice of evil spirits and every phantom of the enemy; and that you may root out that venom of rabies and the enemy himself for good, by the power of our Lord Jesus Christ, who shall come to judge the living and the dead and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
h: Exorcismus salis || Exorcism of the Salt
t: Exorcízo te, creatúra salis, per Deum ✠ vivum, per Deum ✠ verum, per Deum ✠ sanctum, per Deum totíus creatúræ, ut efficiáris sal exorcizátum ad evacuándam et expelléndam rábiem et omne virus ejus, inimicúmque et omnem virtútem ejus atque étiam omnem morbum tam hóminum quam pécorum, quæ necessitátibus humánis dignátus es donáre, ut possint salva ad usus necessários profícere, et hómines salváti mente et córpore cóngruam tibi exhíbeant servitútem per virtútem Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, per quem creáta sunt ómnia et perficiúntur univérsa: Qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || I exorcise you, creature of salt, by the ✠ living God, by the ✠ true God, by the ✠ holy God, by the God of all creation, that you may become exorcised salt to empty out and expel rabies and all its venom, the enemy and all his power, and also every disease of men and of the cattle which You have deigned to give for human needs, so that they may be kept safe for necessary uses, and that men, saved in mind and body, may render You fitting service; by the power of our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom all things were created and all are brought to completion: who shall come to judge the living and the dead and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
h: Benedictio panis || Blessing of the Bread
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.

# profession-of-faith | Modus excipiendi Professionem Fidei Catholicæ a Neo-Conversis | The Manner of Receiving the Profession of the Catholic Faith from Converts | Sacraments | Appendix, Modus excipiendi professionem fidei catholicæ a neo-conversis
r: Juxta formam a S. Congregatione S. Officii die 20 Julii 1859 præscriptam. || According to the form prescribed by the Sacred Congregation of the Holy Office on 20 July 1859.
r: In conversione hæreticorum inquirendum est primo de validitate baptismi in hæresi suscepti. Instituto igitur diligenti examine, si compertum fuerit, aut nullum, aut nulliter collatum fuisse, baptizandi erunt absolute. Si autem, investigatione peracta, adhuc probabile dubium de baptismi validitate supersit, tunc sub condicione iteratur, juxta ordinem baptismi Adultorum. Demum, si constiterit validum fuisse, recipiendi erunt tantummodo ad Abjurationem, seu Professionem Fidei. Triplex igitur in conciliandis hæreticis distinguitur procedendi methodus: || In the conversion of heretics, inquiry must first be made into the validity of the baptism received in heresy. If, therefore, after careful examination, it is found that no baptism was conferred, or that it was conferred invalidly, they are to be baptized absolutely. If, however, after the investigation a probable doubt about the validity of the baptism still remains, it is repeated conditionally, according to the order of the Baptism of Adults. Finally, if it is established that it was valid, they are to be received only to the abjuration, or profession of faith. Thus a threefold method of procedure is distinguished in reconciling heretics:
r: I. Si Baptismus absolute conferatur, nulla sequitur abjuratio, nec absolutio, eo quod omnia abluit Sacramentum Regenerationis. || I. If baptism is conferred absolutely, no abjuration or absolution follows, because the sacrament of regeneration washes away everything.
r: II. Si Baptismus sit sub condicione iterandus, hoc ordine procedendum erit: 1° Abjuratio, seu fidei professio; 2° Baptismus condicionalis; 3° Confessio sacramentalis cum Absolutione condicionata. || II. If baptism is to be repeated conditionally, the procedure will be in this order: 1. the abjuration, or profession of faith; 2. conditional baptism; 3. sacramental confession with conditional absolution.

r: I. In ineundis istiusmodi nuptiis, Ecclesia ea generatim omittenda indixit, per quæ Catholicorum matrimonia decorantur. Proclamationes Bannorum possunt fieri in mixtis nuptiis quæ Apostolica dispensatione contrahuntur, suppressa tamen mentione Religionis conjugum: at solum quando ad detegenda si quæ sint impedimenta, eas necessarias atque opportunas Ordinarius in Domino censuerit. || I. In the contracting of such marriages the Church has ordered that, in general, those things by which the marriages of Catholics are adorned be omitted. The banns may be published for mixed marriages contracted by apostolic dispensation, the mention of the religion of the parties being suppressed; but only when the Ordinary judges in the Lord that they are necessary and opportune for uncovering any impediments there may be.
r: II. Matrimonia Catholicorum cum Acatholicis non sunt benedicenda. Plures enim summi Pontifices, præsertim Clemens VIII, expresse prohibuerunt, ne hujusmodi connubiis sacerdotalis benedictio impendatur. Hinc Concilium Baltimorense Provinciale IV in Decreto primo sic habet: "Meminerint Sacerdotes, pluribus Summorum Pontificum decretis vetari, ne ullus sacer ritus fiat, vel vestis sacra adhibeatur, dum fœdera hujusmodi ineuntur, quæ neque intra ecclesiam ineunda sunt." Denique S. Congregatio de Propaganda Fide, die 3 Julii 1847, ad Patres Concilii Baltimorensis Provincialis VI rescripsit, matrimonia mixta nullo adhibito religioso ritu celebrari oportere. || II. Marriages of Catholics with non-Catholics are not to be blessed. For many Supreme Pontiffs, especially Clement VIII, have expressly forbidden that the priestly blessing be given to such unions. Hence the Fourth Provincial Council of Baltimore, in its first decree, says: "Let priests remember that by several decrees of the Supreme Pontiffs it is forbidden that any sacred rite be performed or any sacred vestment be used while such unions are contracted; nor are they to be contracted within the church." Finally, the Sacred Congregation for the Propagation of the Faith, on 3 July 1847, replied to the Fathers of the Sixth Provincial Council of Baltimore that mixed marriages must be celebrated without the use of any religious rite.
r: III. In dubio de validitate Baptismi collati parti hæreticæ, quæ, conversa, novas nuptias cum parte Catholica inire cupit, referatur casus per Ordinarium ad Sedem Apostolicam. Consulantur hi Canones novi Juris Canonici: Can. 1070, § 2. Si pars tempore contracti matrimonii tamquam baptizata communiter habebatur aut ejus Baptismus erat dubius, standum est, ad normam can. 1014, pro valore matrimonii, donec certo probetur alteram partem baptizatam esse, alteram vero non baptizatam. — Can. 1014. Matrimonium gaudet favore juris; quare in dubio standum est pro valore matrimonii, donec contrarium probetur, salvo præscripto can. 1127. — Can. 1127. In re dubia privilegium fidei gaudet favore juris. || III. In a doubt about the validity of the baptism conferred on a heretical party who, having been converted, wishes to enter a new marriage with a Catholic party, the case is to be referred through the Ordinary to the Apostolic See. The following canons of the new Code of Canon Law are to be consulted: Can. 1070 § 2: If at the time the marriage was contracted a party was commonly held to be baptized, or his baptism was doubtful, the validity of the marriage must be upheld, according to can. 1014, until it is proved with certainty that one party was baptized and the other was not. — Can. 1014: Marriage enjoys the favor of the law; therefore in doubt the validity of a marriage must be upheld until the contrary is proved, without prejudice to the prescription of can. 1127. — Can. 1127: In a doubtful matter the privilege of the faith enjoys the favor of the law.
r: Sacerdos, dispensatione obtenta pro matrimonii celebratione inter catholicam partem et acatholicam, primum cognoscat utrum acatholica pars Baptismi Sacramentum receperit, et, si non fuerit baptizata, petat circa hoc aliam necessariam dispensationem. Insuper ipsi constet de libertate catholici cultus concessa parti catholicæ, et de præmittendis aliis necessariis opportunisque cautionibus in naturali ac divino jure fundatis, ut scilicet non solum catholicus conjux ab acatholico perverti non possit, quin immo catholicus ipse conjux teneri se sciat ad acatholicum pro viribus ab errore retrahendum, verum etiam ut universa utriusque sexus proles procreanda in sanctitate catholicæ religionis educari omnino debeat. Fiant ista opportuno tempore ante celebrationem matrimonii. || The priest, a dispensation having been obtained for the celebration of a marriage between a Catholic and a non-Catholic party, should first ascertain whether the non-Catholic party has received the sacrament of baptism, and, if that party has not been baptized, he should request the further dispensation needed for this. Moreover, he must be assured that freedom of Catholic worship has been granted to the Catholic party, and that the other necessary and fitting guarantees founded in natural and divine law have been given beforehand: namely, not only that the Catholic spouse cannot be perverted by the non-Catholic, but rather that the Catholic spouse knows himself or herself bound to draw the non-Catholic away from error as far as possible; and also that all the children of both sexes to be born must be brought up in the holiness of the Catholic religion. These things are to be done at a suitable time before the celebration of the marriage.
