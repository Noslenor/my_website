# school-another | Alia Benedictio Domus Scholaris | Another Blessing of a School | Homes & Places | Appendix, Benedictiones non reservatæ, 21
r: Die Dominicali vel festiva, a Parocho et Patrono constituta et rite promulgata, Officians et qui adsunt Sacerdotes et Ministri, in domo parœciali, vel, si in loco, ubi schola benedicenda sit, domus parœcialis non adsit, in aliqua alia decenti domo, paramentis coloris albi induti, omnibus bene paratis, hora statuta, præcedente scholari juventute cum vexillo suo, choro et Subdiacono cum Cruce, et sequente Patrono vel ejus commissario, cum reliquis fidelibus ad hanc sollemnitatem invitatis, bono ordine in sollemni processione, sub pulsu campanarum et cantu Litaniarum ad omnes Sanctos vel piorum Hymnorum, ex domo parœciali in ecclesiam se conferunt, ubi Officians ad aram majorem in infimo gradu genuflexus, tono consueto intonat sequentem Hymnum, quem chorus prosequitur. Quod si nulla ecclesia sit in loco, sequentia dicantur in domo, e qua egrediuntur. || On a Sunday or feast day appointed by the pastor and the patron and duly announced, the officiant and the priests and ministers present, vested in white, assemble in the parish house, or, if there is no parish house in the place where the school is to be blessed, in some other suitable house. When all is ready, at the appointed hour, they go in solemn procession and good order from the parish house to the church, while the bells are rung and the Litany of All Saints or devout hymns are sung; the school children go first with their banner, then the choir and the subdeacon with the cross, followed by the patron or his representative together with the other faithful invited to this solemnity. In the church the officiant kneels on the lowest step of the high altar and intones the following hymn in the usual tone, and the choir continues it. If there is no church in the place, what follows is said in the house from which they set out.

t: Veni, Creátor Spíritus, / Mentes tuórum vísita, / Imple supérna grátia, / Quæ tu creásti péctora. || Come, Creator Spirit, / visit the minds of Your own; / fill with grace from on high / the hearts that You have made.
t: Qui díceris Paráclitus, / Altíssimi donum Dei, / Fons vivus, ignis, cáritas, / Et spiritális únctio. || You who are called the Paraclete, / gift of God most high, / living spring, fire, charity, / and spiritual anointing.
t: Tu septifórmis múnere, / Dígitus patérnæ déxteræ, / Tu rite promíssum Patris, / Sermóne ditans gúttura. || Sevenfold in Your gifts, / finger of the Father's right hand, / You, the Father's true promise, / enriching tongues with speech.
t: Accénde lumen sénsibus, / Infúnde amórem córdibus, / Infírma nostri córporis / Virtúte firmans pérpeti. || Kindle a light in our senses, / pour love into our hearts, / strengthening the weakness of our body / with Your unfailing power.
t: Hostem repéllas lóngius, / Pacémque dones prótinus: / Ductóre sic te prǽvio / Vitémus omne nóxium. || Drive the enemy far away, / and grant us peace without delay; / so, with You as our guide going before, / may we shun all that is harmful.
t: Per te sciámus da Patrem, / Noscámus atque Fílium: / Teque utriúsque Spíritum / Credámus omni témpore. || Grant that through You we may know the Father, / and know the Son as well, / and believe in You at all times, / the Spirit of them both.
t: Deo Patri sit glória, / Et Fílio, qui a mórtuis / Surréxit, ac Paráclito, / In sæculórum sǽcula. Amen. || Glory be to God the Father, / and to the Son, who rose / from the dead, and to the Paraclete, / for ages upon ages. Amen.

t: Deus, cui omne cor patet, et omnis volúntas lóquitur, et quem nullum latet secrétum: purífica per infusiónem Sancti Spíritus cogitatiónes cordis nostri; ut hanc nostram benedictiónem digne perfícere, et tuis fidélibus perínde optátam salútem procuráre valeámus. || O God, to whom every heart lies open, to whom every will speaks, and from whom no secret is hidden: cleanse the thoughts of our hearts by the outpouring of the Holy Spirit, that we may be able worthily to perform this our blessing and thereby obtain for Your faithful the salvation they desire.
t: Actiónes nostras, quǽsumus, Dómine, aspirándo prǽveni, et adjuvándo prosequére: ut cuncta nostra orátio et operátio a te semper incípiat, et per te cœpta finiátur. Per Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Almighty and everlasting God, we humbly beseech You to look kindly upon Your little ones: pour the grace of the Holy Spirit into their hearts, so that, enlightened and taught by Him, they may know at all times what is pleasing in Your sight, and may advance in wisdom, age, and grace. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.

t: Dómine sancte, Pater omnípotens, per intercessiónem sanctórum Ignátii et Aloísii béne✠dic domum istam, béne✠dic intróitum nostrum, béne✠dic ingréssum pedum nostrórum: sicut dignátus es domum Patriarchárum Abraham, Isaac, et Jacob benedícere. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.

Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.

t: Dómine Jesu Christe, qui discípulis tuis dixísti: In quamcúmque domum intravéritis, salutáte eam, dicéntes: Pax huic dómui; véniat, quǽsumus, pax illa super hanc domum, instituéndæ juventúti destinátam, et super omnes habitántes, docéntes et discéntes in ea; et eos, Dómine, ab omni infirmitáte erípere, et liberáre dignéris: reple docéntes in ea spíritu sciéntiæ, sapiéntiæ, et timóris tui; reple discéntes in ea grátia tua, ut, quæ salutáriter et utíliter edocéntur, intelléctu cápiant, corde retíneant, ópere exsequántur, et in ómnibus nomen tuum honorificétur. Ad intróitum ergo nostrum bene✠dícere, et sancti✠ficáre dignéris hanc scholam, et intra paríetes domus istíus Angeli tuæ lucis hábitent, eámque et in ea habitántes, docéntes et discéntes custódiant: Qui vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus Christ, who said to Your disciples, "Into whatever house you enter, greet it, saying: Peace be to this house": may that peace come, we beseech You, upon this house set apart for the instruction of the young, and upon all who dwell, teach, and learn in it; and deign, O Lord, to rescue and deliver them from every infirmity. Fill those who teach here with the spirit of knowledge, wisdom, and Your fear; fill those who learn here with Your grace, so that what they are taught for their salvation and profit they may grasp with the mind, keep in the heart, and carry out in deed, and that in all things Your name may be honored. At our entrance, then, deign to ✠ bless and ✠ sanctify this school; and within the walls of this house may Angels of Your light dwell, and guard it and all who dwell, teach, and learn in it. Who live and reign for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.

Amen. || Through the intercession of Saint Michael the Archangel, who stands at the right of the altar of incense, and of all the holy Angels, deign, O Lord, to ✠ bless this incense and to accept it as a sweet fragrance. Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.

h: Psalmus 8 || Psalm 8
t: Dómine, Dóminus noster, * quam admirábile est nomen tuum in univérsa terra! || O Lord, our Lord, * how wonderful is Your name in all the earth!
t: Quóniam eleváta est magnificéntia tua, * super cælos. || For Your magnificence is lifted up * above the heavens.
t: Ex ore infántium et lacténtium perfecísti laudem propter inimícos tuos, * ut déstruas inimícum et ultórem. || Out of the mouths of infants and sucklings You have perfected praise because of Your enemies, * that You may destroy the enemy and the avenger.
t: Quóniam vidébo cælos tuos, ópera digitórum tuórum: * lunam et stellas, quæ tu fundásti. || For I shall behold Your heavens, the works of Your fingers, * the moon and the stars which You have established.
t: Quid est homo, quod memor es ejus? * aut fílius hóminis, quóniam vísitas eum? || What is man, that You are mindful of him? * or the son of man, that You visit him?
