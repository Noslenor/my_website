# crown-our-lord | Benedictio Coronæ Domini | Blessing of the Crown (Chaplet) of Our Lord | Proper to Religious Orders | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ (Ord. Camaldulensium)
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Benedíces corónæ anni benignitátis tuæ. || You will bless the crown of the year of Your goodness.
x: Et campi tui replebúntur ubertáte. || And Your fields shall be filled with plenty.
v: Dómine, exáudi oratiónem meam. || O Lord, hear my prayer.
x: Et clamor meus ad te véniat. || And let my cry come unto You.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Jesu Christe, qui in similitúdinem hóminum factus, trigínta et tribus annis es conversátus in mundo, et multa passus pro hóminum salúte, in Cruce demum mori voluísti: dignáre, quǽsumus, hanc (has) bene✠dícere et sancti✠ficáre corónam, quam (corónas, quas) beáto Michaéli Eremítæ Camaldulénsi ad vitam, passiónem et mortem tuam recoléndam institúere jussísti; ut, qui eam (eas) pie gestáverit (gestáverint) et devóte recitáverit (recitáverint), multíplici gratiárum et indulgentiárum consecutióne corónas in cælo ætérnas obtinére mereátur (mereántur). Per te, Jesu Christe, Salvátor mundi: Qui vivis et regnas cum Deo Patre in unitáte Spíritus Sancti Deus, per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus Christ, who, made in the likeness of men, lived thirty-three years in the world, suffered much for the salvation of men, and at last willed to die on the Cross: deign, we beseech You, to ✠ bless and ✠ sanctify this crown (these crowns), which You commanded blessed Michael, the Camaldolese hermit, to institute for the recalling of Your life, passion, and death; that whoever piously wears it (they who piously wear them) and devoutly recites it (recite them) may be worthy, by obtaining manifold graces and indulgences, to gain eternal crowns in heaven. Through You, Jesus Christ, Savior of the world, who live and reign with God the Father in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Et aspergatur (vel aspergantur) aqua benedicta. || And it is (or they are) sprinkled with holy water.

# precious-blood-chaplets | Benedictio Coronarum Pretiosissimi Sanguinis | Blessing of Chaplets of the Most Precious Blood | Proper to Religious Orders | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ (Congr. Missionariorum pretiosissimi Sanguinis)
r: Constat corona precatoria triginta tribus granulis, in septem distinctis partes, quarum quæque quinque habet granulos, septima parte excepta, quæ tantum tres numerat; ut ita honorentur septem Sanguinis Christi præcipuæ effusiones recitatione triginta trium Pater et septem Glória. || The chaplet consists of thirty-three beads, divided into seven parts, each of which has five beads, except the seventh part, which counts only three; so that the seven chief sheddings of the Blood of Christ may thus be honored by the recitation of thirty-three Our Fathers and seven Glory Be's.
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Redemísti nos, Dómine, in Sánguine tuo. || You have redeemed us, O Lord, in Your Blood.
x: Et fecísti nos Deo nostro regnum. || And have made us a kingdom for our God.
v: Te ergo quǽsumus, tuis fámulis súbveni. || We therefore beseech You, help Your servants,
x: Quos pretióso Sánguine redemísti. || Whom You have redeemed with Your precious Blood.
v: Dómine, exáudi oratiónem meam. || O Lord, hear my prayer.
x: Et clamor meus ad te véniat. || And let my cry come unto You.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Omnípotens et miséricors Deus, qui propter exímiam caritátem tuam, qua dilexísti nos, Fílium tuum unigénitum, Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum, de cælis in terram descéndere, et de beatíssimæ Vírginis Maríæ Dóminæ nostræ útero sacratíssimo, Angelo nuntiánte, carnem suscípere voluísti, ut nos eríperes de potestáte diáboli: obsecrámus imménsam cleméntiam tuam; ut has corónas in honórem, et laudem pretiósi Sánguinis ejúsdem Fílii tui ab Ecclésia tua fidéli dicátas bene✠dícas, et sancti✠fices, eísque tantam infúndas virtútem Spíritus ✠ Sancti, ut, quicúmque harum quámlibet secum portáverit, aut in domo sua reverénter tenúerit, ab omni hoste visíbili et invisíbili semper et ubíque in hoc sǽculo liberétur, et post éxitum suum, méritis ipsíus pretiosíssimi Sánguinis, ad ætérnam beatitúdinem felíciter pervéniat. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Almighty and merciful God, who, because of the exceeding love with which You loved us, willed that Your only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, should come down from heaven to earth and take flesh at the message of an Angel in the most sacred womb of our Lady, the most blessed Virgin Mary, to rescue us from the power of the devil: we implore Your boundless mercy that You would ✠ bless and ✠ sanctify these chaplets, dedicated by Your faithful Church to the honor and praise of the precious Blood of the same Son of Yours, and pour into them such power of the Holy ✠ Spirit that whoever carries any one of them with him, or keeps it reverently in his home, may be delivered always and everywhere in this world from every enemy, visible and invisible, and after his departure may happily attain to eternal blessedness through the merits of that most precious Blood. Through the same Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Et aspergantur aqua benedicta. || And they are sprinkled with holy water.

Through Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.

# sick-st-maurus | Benedictio Infirmorum cum Ligno Sanctissimæ Crucis D. N. J. C. seu Signum S. Mauri Abbatis | Blessing of the Sick with the Wood of the Most Holy Cross, or the Sign of St. Maurus, Abbot | The Sick | Appendix, Benedictiones propriæ (Ord. S. Benedicti)
r: Antea rite exponatur Reliquia veræ Crucis Dominicæ, duabus saltem candelis accensis. Excitetur deinde in infirmo actus contritionis, et firma fiducia, ut adjutus meritis S. P. Benedicti et S. Mauri, ipsis intercedentibus, si Deo placeat, sanitatem obtineat. Recitentur tres Pater totidemque Ave, María et Glória in honorem sanctissimæ Trinitatis: tum Sacerdos, indutus superpelliceo et stola rubri coloris, manu dextera tenens elevatam Reliquiam sanctæ Crucis coram infirmo, dicit: || First a relic of the true Cross of the Lord is duly exposed, with at least two candles lit. Then the sick person is stirred to an act of contrition and to firm confidence that, helped by the merits of our holy Father Benedict and of St. Maurus, and through their intercession, he may obtain health, if it please God. Three Our Fathers and as many Hail Marys and Glory Be's are recited in honor of the most Holy Trinity; then the priest, vested in surplice and red stole, holding the relic of the holy Cross raised in his right hand before the sick person, says:
v: Benedíctio et cláritas et sapiéntia et gratiárum áctio, honor, virtus et fortitúdo Deo nostro in sǽcula sæculórum. || Blessing and glory and wisdom and thanksgiving, honor, power, and strength be to our God for ever and ever.
x: Amen. || Amen.
v: Pes meus stetit in dirécto. || My foot has stood on level ground.
x: In ecclésiis benedícam te, Dómine. || In the churches I will bless You, O Lord.
h: Invocatio || Invocation
t: Per sanctíssimum nomen Dómini invocátum, optátam tibi donet sanitátem illa fides, qua sanctus Maurus per sequéntia verba ægrótos sanávit, et in qua ego, licet indígnus peccátor, eásdem preces super te humíliter prófero: || By the most holy name of the Lord which has been invoked, may that faith grant you the health you desire, by which St. Maurus healed the sick with the following words, and in which I, though an unworthy sinner, humbly pronounce the same prayers over you:
t: In nómine sanctíssimæ et indivíduæ Trinitátis, adjútus méritis sanctíssimi Patris Benedícti, sta sanus (-a), o N., et incólumis super pedes tuos rectus (-a). In nómine Patris, et Fílii, ✠ et Spíritus Sancti. ℟. Amen. || In the name of the most holy and undivided Trinity, helped by the merits of our most holy Father Benedict, stand up healthy, O N., and sound, upright upon your feet. In the name of the Father, and of the Son, ✠ and of the Holy Spirit. ℟. Amen.
t: *Ant.* Vere languóres nostros ipse tulit, et dolóres nostros ipse portávit, cujus livóre sanáti sumus. || *Ant.* Surely He has borne our infirmities and carried our sorrows; by His bruises we are healed.
v: Qui propitiátur iniquitátibus hóminum. || May He who forgives the iniquities of men
x: Ipse sanet infirmitátes tuas. || Himself heal your infirmities.
v: Dómine, exáudi oratiónem meam. || O Lord, hear my prayer.
x: Et clamor meus ad te véniat. || And let my cry come unto You.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Deus ómnium cónditor creaturárum, qui ad restauratiónem humáni géneris únicum Fílium tuum, cooperánte Spíritu Sancto, ex María Vírgine incarnári constituísti, et per sacrosánctum ac gloriósum vivíficæ Crucis lignum vulnéribus et languóribus animárum nostrárum, nos rediméndo, subveníre dignátus es: ipse hunc fámulum tuum N. (hanc fámulam tuam N.) per vitális hujus signi virtútem ad prístinam redúcere dignéris sanitátem. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || O God, Maker of all creatures, who for the restoration of the human race decreed that Your only Son should become incarnate of the Virgin Mary by the working of the Holy Spirit, and who deigned, in redeeming us, to relieve the wounds and infirmities of our souls through the most holy and glorious wood of the life-giving Cross: deign Yourself to restore this Your servant N. (this Your handmaid N.) to former health by the power of this life-giving sign. Through the same Christ our Lord. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Jesu Christe, qui magístro meo beáto Benedícto dedísti potestátem apud te impetráre, quod in nómine tuo péteret: tu per ipsíus intercessiónem cunctos ab hoc fámulo tuo (ab hac fámula tua) languóres repéllere dignáre; ut sanitáte percépta, gratiárum actiónem réferat nómini sancto tuo: Qui cum Patre et Spíritu Sancto vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Lord Jesus Christ, who gave my master, blessed Benedict, the power to obtain from You whatever he asked in Your name: through his intercession deign to drive away all ailments from this Your servant (this Your handmaid); that, having regained health, he (she) may render thanks to Your holy name. You who live and reign with the Father and the Holy Spirit for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
h: Signum || The Sign
t: Per invocatiónem immaculátæ Dei Matris et semper Vírginis Maríæ, et per intercessiónem sanctórum Benedícti et Mauri, Poténtia ✠ Dei Patris, Sapiéntia ✠ Dei Fílii, Virtus ✠ Spíritus Sancti líberet te ab ista infirmitáte. Amen. || Through the invocation of the immaculate Mother of God and ever Virgin Mary, and through the intercession of Saints Benedict and Maurus, may the ✠ Power of God the Father, the ✠ Wisdom of God the Son, and the ✠ Strength of the Holy Spirit deliver you from this infirmity. Amen.
