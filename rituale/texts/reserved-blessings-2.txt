# cross-sollemnior | Benedictio Sollemnior Crucis | More Solemn Blessing of a Cross | Objects of Devotion | Appendix, Benedictiones reservatæ, 2
r: Paratis thure thuribulóque cum igne, et aqua benedicta, Sacerdos delegatus, superpelliceo, stola et pluviali rubri coloris indutus, stans dicit: || When incense and a thurible with fire, and holy water, have been made ready, the delegated priest, vested in surplice, stole, and red cope, standing, says:
v: Adjutórium nostrum in nómine Dómini. || Our help is in the name of the Lord.
x: Qui fecit cælum et terram. || Who made heaven and earth.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Bene✠dic, Dómine Jesu Christe, hanc Crucem, per quam eripuísti mundum a potestáte dǽmonum, et superásti passióne tua suggestórem peccáti, qui gaudébat in prævaricatióne primi hóminis per ligni vétiti sumptiónem: Qui cum Deo Patre et Spíritu Sancto vivis et regnas in sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || ✠ Bless, Lord Jesus Christ, this Cross, by which You rescued the world from the power of demons, and by Your Passion overcame the instigator of sin, who rejoiced in the transgression of the first man through his eating of the forbidden tree: who live and reign with God the Father and the Holy Spirit for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Rogámus te, Dómine sancte, Pater omnípotens, sempitérne Deus: ut dignéris bene✠dícere hoc signum Crucis, ut sit remédium salutáre géneri humáno: sit solíditas fídei, proféctus bonórum óperum, redémptio animárum: sit solámen et protéctio ac tutéla contra sæva jácula inimicórum. Per Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti Deus. || We beseech You, holy Lord, almighty Father, eternal God, that You would be pleased to ✠ bless this sign of the Cross, that it may be a saving remedy for the human race; that it may be the firmness of faith, the advancement of good works, the redemption of souls; that it may be a comfort, a protection, and a defense against the cruel darts of the enemy. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, Your Son, who lives and reigns with You in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God.
r: Deinde dicit voce mediocri, extensis manibus ante pectus, Præfationem. || Then, in a moderate voice, with hands extended before the breast, he says the Preface.
v: Per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. || For ever and ever.
x: Amen. || Amen.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
v: Sursum corda. || Lift up your hearts.
x: Habémus ad Dóminum. || We have lifted them up to the Lord.
v: Grátias agámus Dómino, Deo nostro. || Let us give thanks to the Lord our God.
x: Dignum et justum est. || It is right and just.
t: Vere dignum et justum est, æquum et salutáre, nos tibi semper et ubíque grátias ágere, Dómine sancte, Pater omnípotens, ætérne Deus: cujus sanctum ac terríbile Nomen, inter céteras visíbiles creatúras, ligna quoque fructífera laudáre ac benedícere non cessant: qui in figúram unigénitæ Sapiéntiæ tuæ ligno vitæ a princípio paradísum voluptátis ornásti, ut ejúsdem fructu, sacro mystério, Protoparéntes nostri géneris mortem cavére, et vitam admonéres obtinére perpétuam: quique nos vétitæ árboris attáctu juste morti addíctos, ejúsdem coætérnæ tibi Sapiéntiæ, Dei et Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, innóxia morte ad vitam misericórditer revocáre dignátus es: Te súpplices exorámus, ut hoc singuláre signum, quod ad exémplum primi illíus sacratíssimi vexílli, quo pretióso Fílii tui sánguine triumphásti, fidélium tuórum devotióne compáctum, erectúmque est, cælésti tua bene✠dictióne sanctificáre dignéris; ut ómnibus hic génua flecténtibus, ac tuæ Majestáti supplicántibus, lárgior et cordis compúnctio, et admissórum indulgéntia concedátur, atque intercedénte ipsa victoriosíssima unigéniti Fílii tui passióne, et tibi plácita postuláre, et cítius váleant postuláta percípere. || It is truly right and just, fitting and for our salvation, that we should at all times and in all places give thanks to You, holy Lord, almighty Father, eternal God, whose holy and awesome Name, among the other visible creatures, even the fruit-bearing trees never cease to praise and bless; who from the beginning adorned the paradise of delight with the tree of life as a figure of Your only-begotten Wisdom, that by its fruit, in a sacred mystery, You might warn the first parents of our race to shun death and to obtain everlasting life; and who, when we had been justly condemned to death for touching the forbidden tree, were pleased in Your mercy to call us back to life through the innocent death of that same Wisdom, coeternal with You, our God and Lord Jesus Christ. We humbly implore You to be pleased to sanctify with Your heavenly ✠ blessing this singular sign, which, after the pattern of that first and most holy standard by which You triumphed in the precious blood of Your Son, has been fashioned and set up by the devotion of Your faithful; that to all who kneel here and make supplication to Your Majesty there may be granted a more abundant compunction of heart and pardon of their offenses, and that, through the intercession of the most victorious Passion of Your only-begotten Son itself, they may both ask for what is pleasing to You and the more swiftly obtain what they ask.
t: Da, quǽsumus, clementíssime Pater, in quo vívimus, movémur et sumus: ut, quóties triúmphum divínæ humilitátis, quæ supérbiam nostri hostis dejécit, óculis intuémur quotiésque mente recólimus, et contra hostem ipsum fidúciam fortitúdinis, et majórem tibi devótæ humilitátis grátiam consequámur: quátenus in illo treméndo tuæ Majestátis exámine, cum pavéntibus eleméntis, cælorúmque commótis virtútibus, signum istud gloríficum redemptiónis nostræ apparúerit in cælo, ipsi de morte ad vitam transíre, ac perpétuæ beátæ resurrectiónis vidére gáudia mereámur. || Grant, we beseech You, most merciful Father, in whom we live and move and have our being, that as often as we behold with our eyes, and as often as we call to mind, the triumph of the divine humility which cast down the pride of our enemy, we may gain both confident strength against that same enemy and a greater grace of devout humility before You; so that in that dreadful judgment of Your Majesty, when the elements tremble and the powers of heaven are shaken, and this glorious sign of our redemption appears in the sky, we may be found worthy to pass from death to life and to behold the joys of the everlasting and blessed resurrection.
r: Quod sequitur, dicitur submissa voce legendo, ita tamen, ut a circumstantibus audiri possit: || What follows is said in a low voice, reading, yet so that it can be heard by those standing around:
t: Per eúndem Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti Deus, per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Through the same Jesus Christ, our Lord, Your Son, who lives and reigns with You in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Deus, qui beátæ Crucis patíbulum, quod prius erat sceléstis ad pœnam, convertísti redémptis ad vitam: concéde plebi tuæ, ejus vallári præsídio, cujus est armáta vexíllo. Sit ei Crux fídei fundaméntum, spei suffrágium, in advérsis defénsio, in prósperis adjuvámen: sit ei in hostes victória, in civitáte custódia, in campis protéctio, in domo fultúra: ut per eam Pastor in futúro gregem consérvet incólumem, quæ nobis, Agno ✠ vincénte, convérsa est in salútem. Per eúndem Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum, Fílium tuum: Qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti Deus, per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || O God, who turned the gibbet of the blessed Cross, once the punishment of the wicked, into life for the redeemed: grant to Your people to be defended by the protection of that standard with which they are armed. May the Cross be for them the foundation of faith, the support of hope, their defense in adversity, their help in prosperity; may it be for them victory over their enemies, a guard in the city, a protection in the fields, a stay in the home; that through it the Shepherd may keep His flock unharmed in the time to come, through that Cross which, by the victory of the ✠ Lamb, has been turned into salvation for us. Through the same Jesus Christ, our Lord, Your Son, who lives and reigns with You in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Sancti✠fica, Dómine Jesu Christe, signáculum istud passiónis tuæ: ut sit inimícis tuis obstáculum, et credéntibus in te perpétuum efficiátur victóriæ vexíllum: Qui cum Deo Patre vivis et regnas in unitáte Spíritus Sancti Deus, per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || ✠ Sanctify, Lord Jesus Christ, this sign of Your Passion, that it may be an obstacle to Your enemies and may become for those who believe in You an everlasting standard of victory: who live and reign with God the Father in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
r: Tum offertur Sacerdoti thus in navicula, quod stans benedicit, dicens: || Then incense in the boat is offered to the priest, which he blesses standing, saying:
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
t: Dómine Deus omnípotens, cui assístit exércitus Angelórum cum tremóre, quorum servítium spirituále et ígneum esse cognóscitur: dignáre respícere, bene✠dícere, et sancti✠ficáre hanc creatúram incénsi, ut omnes languóres, omnésque infirmitátes, atque insídiæ inimíci, odórem ejus sentiéntes, effúgiant, et separéntur a plásmate tuo; ut numquam lædátur a morsu antíqui serpéntis, quod pretióso Fílii tui sánguine redemísti. Per eúndem Christum, Dóminum nostrum. ℟. Amen. || Lord God almighty, before whom the host of Angels stands in trembling, whose service is known to be spiritual and fiery: be pleased to look upon, ✠ bless, and ✠ sanctify this creature of incense, that all sicknesses, all infirmities, and all the snares of the enemy, on perceiving its fragrance, may flee and be parted from the work of Your hands, so that what You have redeemed with the precious blood of Your Son may never be harmed by the bite of the ancient serpent. Through the same Christ, our Lord. ℟. Amen.
r: Quo facto, Sacerdos imponit thus in thuribulum. Deinde aspergit Crucem aqua benedicta, et mox eam incensat. Tunc dicit adhuc stans: || This done, the priest puts the incense into the thurible. Then he sprinkles the Cross with holy water and immediately incenses it. Then, still standing, he says:
r: 1. Si Crux sit ex ligno: || 1. If the Cross is of wood:
t: Sanctificétur lignum istud in nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti: et benedíctio illíus ligni, in quo membra sancta Salvatóris suspénsa sunt, sit in isto ligno; ut orántes inclinantésque se propter Deum ante istam Crucem, invéniant córporis et ánimæ sanitátem. Per eúndem Dóminum... in unitáte ejúsdem Spíritus. ℟. Amen. || May this wood be sanctified in the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit; and may the blessing of that wood on which the holy limbs of the Savior hung be upon this wood, so that those who pray and bow down for God's sake before this Cross may find health of body and soul. Through the same Lord... in the unity of the same Spirit. ℟. Amen.
r: Tum Sacerdos flexis ante Crucem genibus ipsam devote adorat, et osculatur. Idem faciunt quicumque voluerint. || Then the priest, kneeling before the Cross, devoutly venerates and kisses it. All who wish do the same.
r: 2. Si vero Crux sit ex metallo vel lapide: || 2. But if the Cross is of metal or stone:
t: Deus glóriæ, Deus excélse Sábaoth, fortíssime Emmánuel, Deus Pater veritátis, Pater sapiéntiæ, Pater beatitúdinis, Pater illuminatiónis ac vigilatiónis nostræ; qui mundum regis, qui cuncta regna dispónis, qui es bonórum collátor múnerum, et bonórum ómnium attribútor; cui omnes gentes, pópuli, tribus et linguæ sérviunt; cui assístit omnis Angelórum légio; qui largíris fámulis tuis fidem et laudem tui nóminis, ut débita tibi obláta persólvant: cui prius fides offeréntium cómplacet, deínde sacrificátur oblátio: quǽsumus exorábilem misericórdiæ tuæ pietátem, ut sancti✠fices tibi hoc signum Crucis, et cónse✠cres, quod tota mentis devotióne famulórum tuórum religiósa fides constrúxit, trophǽum scílicet victóriæ tuæ ac redemptiónis nostræ, quod in amórem Christi triumphális glória consecrávit. || God of glory, God most high of hosts, most mighty Emmanuel, God the Father of truth, Father of wisdom, Father of blessedness, Father of our enlightenment and watchfulness; who rule the world, who order all kingdoms, who are the bestower of good gifts and the giver of all good things; whom all nations, peoples, tribes, and tongues serve; before whom stands every legion of the Angels; who grant to Your servants faith and the praise of Your name, that they may render to You the offerings they owe; to whom the faith of those who offer is first pleasing, and then the offering is sacrificed: we beseech Your merciful kindness, ever ready to be entreated, to ✠ sanctify and ✠ consecrate to Yourself this sign of the Cross, which the religious faith of Your servants has built with the whole devotion of their minds, the trophy, that is, of Your victory and of our redemption, which triumphal glory has consecrated in the love of Christ.
t: Aspice hoc signum Crucis insuperábile, per quod diáboli exinaníta est potéstas, mortálium restitúta libértas: quæ licet fúerit aliquándo in pœnam, sed nunc versa est in honórem per grátiam, et quæ reos quondam puniébat supplício, nunc et nóxios absólvit a débito. Et tibi quid per hoc placére pótuit, nisi id, per quod tibi plácuit nos redímere? Et nullum tibi débitum ámplius munus est, quam quod tibi tunc córporis dedicávit affíxio: nec tibi est magis familiáris oblátio, quam quæ familiári mánuum tuárum extensióne sacráta est. Illis ergo mánibus hanc Crucem áccipe, quibus illam ampléxus es, et de sanctitáte illíus hanc sancti✠fica: et sícuti per illam mundus expiátus est a reátu, ita offeréntium famulórum tuórum ánimæ, devotíssimæ hujus crucis mérito, omni cáreant perpetráto peccáto, et tuæ veræ Crucis obtéctu enitéscant succéssibus assíduis triumphatóres. || Look upon this invincible sign of the Cross, by which the power of the devil was brought to nothing and the freedom of mortals restored; which, though once it served for punishment, has now by grace been turned to honor, and which once punished the guilty with torment now also absolves the guilty from their debt. And what in this could please You, if not that by which it pleased You to redeem us? No gift is more due to You than that which the fastening of His body to it then dedicated to You; nor is any offering more dear to You than that which was hallowed by the outstretching of His hands, so dear to You. With those hands, then, receive this Cross, with which You embraced that Cross, and from its holiness ✠ sanctify this one; and as through that Cross the world was cleansed from guilt, so may the souls of Your servants who offer this, through the merit of this most devout cross, be free of every sin committed, and under the shelter of Your true Cross shine forth as victors in continual success.
t: Rádiet hic unigéniti Fílii tui, Dómini nostri, splendor divinitátis in auro, émicet glória passiónis ejus in ligno: in Cruce rútilet nostræ mortis redémptio; in crystálli splendóre vitæ nostræ purificátio. Sit suórum protéctio, spei certa fidúcia; eos simul cum gente et plebe fide confírmet, spe et pace consóciet: áugeat triúmphis, amplíficet in secúndis, profíciat eis ad perpetuitátem témporis, ad vitam æternitátis, ut eos temporáli floréntes glória múniat, et ad perpétuam redémptos corónam, ad regna cæléstia poténti virtúte perdúcat. Præsta per propitiatiónem sánguinis ejus, per ipsum datórem qui seípsum dedit redemptiónem pro multis, qui se hóstiam pro delíctis offérre dignátus est, qui exaltátus in ligno Crucis suæ principátus et potestátes humiliávit, qui tecum sidéreo cónsidet throno, indissolúbili conexióne Spíritus Sancti, per infiníta sæculórum sǽcula. ℟. Amen. || Here may the splendor of the divinity of Your only-begotten Son, our Lord, shine out in the gold; may the glory of His Passion gleam in the wood; may the redemption from our death glow red in the Cross; and in the brightness of the crystal, the purification of our life. May it be the protection of His own, the sure confidence of hope; may it strengthen them in faith together with their nation and people, unite them in hope and peace, increase them in triumphs, enlarge them in prosperity, and profit them for lasting time and for the life of eternity; that it may guard them as they flourish in temporal glory, and by its mighty power lead them, redeemed, to the everlasting crown and to the heavenly kingdom. Grant this through the atonement of His blood, through the very Giver who gave Himself as a redemption for many, who deigned to offer Himself as a victim for sins, who, lifted up on the wood of His Cross, humbled the principalities and powers, who sits with You upon the starry throne in the indissoluble bond of the Holy Spirit, through endless ages of ages. ℟. Amen.
r: Tum Sacerdos flexis ante Crucem genibus ipsam devote adorat, et osculatur. Idem faciunt quicumque voluerint. || Then the priest, kneeling before the Cross, devoutly venerates and kisses it. All who wish do the same.
