# font-full | Benedictio Fontis seu Aquæ Baptismalis | Blessing of the Font or Baptismal Water | Sacraments | Tit. II, cap. 8
r: Extra pervigilium Paschæ et Pentecostes, cum aqua consecrata non habetur. || Outside the Vigils of Easter and Pentecost, when consecrated water is not at hand.
r: 1. Fons primum lavatur, et mundatur vas Baptisterii, deinde limpida aqua repletur. Tum Sacerdos cum suis Clericis, vel etiam aliis Presbyteris, cruce et duobus cereis præcedentibus, ac thuribulo et incenso, et cum vasculis Chrismatis, et Olei Catechumenorum accedit ad Fontem, et ibi, vel ante Altare Baptisterii, dicit Litanias ordinarias, prout habentur infra (pag. 104), post septem Psalmos Pœnitentiales. || 1. First the font is washed and the basin of the baptistery cleansed; then it is filled with clear water. Then the priest with his clerics, or also with other priests, preceded by the cross and two candles, with the thurible and incense, and with the vessels of Chrism and of the Oil of Catechumens, goes to the font, and there, or before the altar of the baptistery, says the ordinary Litanies as they are given below (page 104), after the seven Penitential Psalms.
n: See "The Seven Penitential Psalms with the Litany of the Saints" (Septem Psalmi Pœnitentiales cum Litaniis Sanctorum).
r: 2. Potest etiam dicere Litanias breviores, ut in Missali in Sabbato Sancto. || 2. He may also say the shorter Litanies, as in the Missal on Holy Saturday.
r: 3. Sed ante ℣. Ut nos exaudire digneris, dicat et secundo repetat sequentem versum: || 3. But before the verse "That You would graciously hear us," he says, and repeats a second time, the following verse:
t: Ut Fontem istum ad regenerándam tibi novam prolem bene✠dícere, et conse✠cráre dignéris. ℟. Te rogámus, audi nos. || That You would deign to ✠ bless and ✠ consecrate this font for the regeneration of new offspring unto You. ℟. We beseech You, hear us.
r: 4. Dicto autem ultimo Kyrie, eleison, Sacerdos dicit: Pater noster et Credo in Deum, etc. (pag. 59), omnia clara voce: quibus finitis dicit: || 4. When the last Kyrie, eleison has been said, the priest says the Our Father and the I believe in God, etc. (page 59), all in a clear voice; and when these are finished he says:
v: Apud te, Dómine, est fons vitæ. || With You, O Lord, is the fountain of life.
x: Et in lúmine tuo vidébimus lumen. || And in Your light we shall see light.
v: Dómine, exáudi oratiónem meam. || O Lord, hear my prayer.
x: Et clamor meus ad te véniat. || And let my cry come unto You.
v: Dóminus vobíscum. || The Lord be with you.
x: Et cum spíritu tuo. || And with your spirit.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
h: Oratio || Prayer
t: Omnípotens sempitérne Deus, adésto magnæ pietátis tuæ mystériis, adésto sacraméntis: et ad recreándos novos pópulos, quos tibi fons Baptísmatis párturit, spíritum adoptiónis emítte; ut, quod nostræ humilitátis geréndum est ministério, virtútis tuæ impleátur efféctu. Per Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum, Fílium tuum, qui tecum vivit et regnat in unitáte Spíritus Sancti Deus, per ómnia sǽcula sæculórum. ℟. Amen. || Almighty, everlasting God, be present at the mysteries of Your great goodness, be present at Your sacraments; and send forth the spirit of adoption to create anew the new peoples whom the font of Baptism brings forth for You; that what is to be carried out by the ministry of our lowliness may be fulfilled by the working of Your power. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, Your Son, who lives and reigns with You in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. ℟. Amen.
h: Exorcismus aquæ || Exorcism of the Water
t: Exorcízo te, creatúra aquæ, per Deum ✠ vivum, per Deum ✠ verum, per Deum ✠ sanctum, per Deum, qui te in princípio verbo separávit ab árida: cujus Spíritus super te ferebátur, qui te de paradíso manáre jussit. || I exorcise you, creature of water, by the ✠ living God, by the ✠ true God, by the ✠ holy God, by the God who in the beginning separated you by His word from the dry land; whose Spirit moved over you; who commanded you to flow forth from paradise,
r: 5. Hic manu aquam dividit, et deinde effundit eam extra marginem Fontis, versus quatuor mundi partes, prosequens: || 5. Here he divides the water with his hand, and then pours some of it out over the rim of the font toward the four quarters of the world, continuing:
t: Et in quátuor flumínibus totam terram rigáre præcépit: qui te in desérto amáram per lignum, dulcem fecit atque potábilem; qui te de petra prodúxit, ut pópulum, quem ex Ægýpto liberáverat, siti fatigátum recreáret. Exorcízo te per Jesum Christum, Fílium ejus únicum, Dóminum nostrum: qui te in Cana Galilǽæ signo admirábili sua poténtia convértit in vinum: qui super te pédibus ambulávit, et a Joánne in Jordáne in te baptizátus est. Qui te una cum sánguine de látere suo prodúxit: et discípulis suis jussit, ut credéntes baptizárent in te, dicens: Ite, docéte omnes gentes, baptizántes eos in nómine Patris, et Fílii, et Spíritus Sancti; ut efficiáris aqua sancta, aqua benedícta, aqua, quæ lavat sordes, et mundat peccáta. Tibi ígitur præcípio, omnis spíritus immúnde, omne phantásma, omne mendácium, eradicáre, et effugáre ab hac creatúra aquæ, ut qui in ipsa baptizándi erunt, fiat eis fons aquæ saliéntis in vitam ætérnam, regénerans eos Deo Patri, et Fílio, et Spirítui Sancto, in nómine ejúsdem Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, qui ventúrus est judicáre vivos et mórtuos, et sǽculum per ignem. ℟. Amen. || and bade you water the whole earth in four rivers; who in the desert made you, once bitter, sweet and drinkable by means of the wood; who brought you forth from the rock, to refresh the people He had set free from Egypt when they were worn out with thirst. I exorcise you through Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord, who in Cana of Galilee by a wondrous sign of His power changed you into wine; who walked upon you with His feet, and was baptized in you by John in the Jordan; who brought you forth from His side together with blood, and commanded His disciples to baptize believers in you, saying: Go, teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit; that you may become holy water, blessed water, water that washes away defilement and cleanses sins. Therefore I command you, every unclean spirit, every phantasm, every lie, to be rooted out and to flee from this creature of water, so that for those who are to be baptized in it, it may become a fountain of water springing up into eternal life, regenerating them for God the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, in the name of the same Jesus Christ our Lord, who will come to judge the living and the dead and the world by fire. ℟. Amen.
t: Orémus. || Let us pray.
h: Oratio || Prayer
t: Dómine sancte, Pater omnípotens, ætérne Deus, aquárum spirituálium sanctificátor, te supplíciter deprecámur: ut ad hoc ministérium humilitátis nostræ respícere dignéris, et super has aquas, abluéndis et purificándis homínibus præparátas, Ángelum sanctitátis emíttas, quo, peccátis vitæ prióris ablútis, reatúque detérso, purum Sancto Spirítui habitáculum regeneráti éffici mereántur. Per Dóminum nostrum Jesum Christum etc. ℟. Amen. || Holy Lord, Father almighty, eternal God, sanctifier of the spiritual waters, we humbly entreat You to look upon this ministry of our lowliness, and to send the Angel of holiness upon these waters, made ready for the washing and cleansing of men; that by him, the sins of their former life being washed away and their guilt wiped clean, they may be found worthy, once reborn, to become a pure dwelling-place for the Holy Spirit. Through our Lord Jesus Christ, etc. ℟. Amen.
r: 6. Tunc sufflat ter in aquam versus tres partes secundum hanc figuram Ψ: deinde imponit incensum in thuribulo, et Fontem incen✠sat. Postea infundens de Oleo Catechumenorum in aquam in modum crucis, clara voce dicit: || 6. Then he breathes three times upon the water toward three directions, according to this figure Ψ; then he puts incense into the thurible and ✠ incenses the font. Afterwards, pouring some of the Oil of Catechumens into the water in the form of a cross, he says in a clear voice:
t: Sanctificétur, et fecundétur fons iste óleo salútis renascéntibus ex eo in vitam ætérnam, in nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. ℟. Amen. || May this font be sanctified and made fruitful by the oil of salvation for those who are reborn from it unto eternal life, in the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit. ℟. Amen.
r: 7. Deinde infundit de Chrismate, modo quo supra, dicens: || 7. Then he pours in some of the Chrism in the same manner as above, saying:
t: Infúsio Chrísmatis Dómini nostri Jesu Christi, et Spíritus Sancti Parácliti, fiat in nómine sanctæ Trinitátis. ℟. Amen. || May this pouring of the Chrism of our Lord Jesus Christ and of the Holy Spirit, the Paraclete, be done in the name of the Holy Trinity. ℟. Amen.
r: 8. Postea accipit ambas ampullas dicti Olei sancti et Chrismatis, et de utroque simul in modum crucis infundendo, dicit: || 8. Afterwards he takes both vessels, of the said holy Oil and of the Chrism, and pouring from both together in the form of a cross, he says:
t: Commíxtio Chrísmatis sanctificatiónis, et Olei unctiónis, et aquæ Baptísmatis páriter fiat in nómine Pa✠tris, et Fí✠lii, et Spíritus ✠ Sancti. ℟. Amen. || May this mingling of the Chrism of sanctification, and of the Oil of anointing, and of the water of Baptism, together be done in the name of the ✠ Father, and of the ✠ Son, and of the Holy ✠ Spirit. ℟. Amen.
r: 9. Tum, depositis ampullis, dextera manu Oleum sanctum, et Chrisma infusum miscet cum aqua et spargit per totum Fontem. Deinde medulla panis manum tergit; et si quis baptizandus sit, eum baptizat, ut supra. Quod si neminem baptizet, statim manus abluat, et ablutio effundatur in sacrarium. || 9. Then, having set down the vessels, with his right hand he mixes the holy Oil and the Chrism that have been poured in with the water, and spreads it through the whole font. Then he wipes his hand with the crumb of bread; and if anyone is to be baptized, he baptizes him as above. But if he baptizes no one, he at once washes his hands, and the water of the ablution is poured into the sacrarium.
